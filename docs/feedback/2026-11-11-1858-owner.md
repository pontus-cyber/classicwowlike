## Lead's notes (draft, for discussion)
- Goal: a skilled player on a fresh profile (P0) wins most runs; meta helps learners more than experts.
- Today: tactician (best bot) P0 48.3 % classic (report 2026-11-11f); planner about 33 %; masher about 1 %.
- Proposed targets: skill ceiling (tactician P0 >= 60-70 %), unavoidable losses (a full-knowledge bot loses <= 10-15 % at P0), meta helps the planner more than the tactician.
- Tension with meta v2 draft (persistent character, capped talents): a fresh character must still be winnable by skill; unlocks should widen options more than raise power. Discuss before meta v2 is built.
- Levers: readable, learnable enemies (intents, archetype openings, family patterns, boss phases); low damage variance; fewer unfair seeds; optional difficulty ladder for experts (the Furnace / Ascendancy).
