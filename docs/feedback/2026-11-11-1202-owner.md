# Owner feedback 2026-11-11 12:02

> revive pet as a hunter should cost at least 2 turns. it should be expensive. but then might be worth it if the pet can taunt.

## Today's rules
- Mend Companion (rules 5.x, lvl 6, 25 Focus, CD 3): heals the pet, or revives a dead pet at 30 % HP, in one action.
- Beastcall Horn (item, CD 12): pet to full or revived, pet TAUNT 2 rounds.
- After a fight a dead pet comes back at PET_REVIVE_PCT (25 %).

## Lead's proposal (draft, for discussion)
- Mend Companion heals a living pet only.
- New Hunter action "Call Back the Companion" (name draft, originality check pending): 2-round channel, about 40 Focus, revives at 30 %; on revive the pet taunts every enemy for 2 rounds. A hit that deals over a set share of max HP during the channel breaks it (Focus spent).
- Beastcall Horn stays the expensive instant option.
- Sim check before it ships: the Hunter is already weakest below its capstone, so the taunt must pay back the 2 rounds (target: Hunter win rate within B-targets, revive used in a meaningful share of pet-death fights, not always and not never).
