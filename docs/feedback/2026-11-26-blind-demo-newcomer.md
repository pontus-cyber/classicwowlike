## Scores

## Top 3 changes (tester)

1. Explain tolls, the Furnace and the Dimming once, with the consequence stated; fix the Smouldering / counts as Calm contradiction.
2. Give the Mage a real second decision earlier: a third spell before the first elite, or a mid-fight way to stop a wind-up.
3. Make leaving deliberate and cut hint noise: one exit with distinct wording, a clear "this ends your run" moment before the last elite, fewer bubbles in the first three fights.
