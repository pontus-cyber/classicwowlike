# Legacy progression: summary for the owner (draft)

Status: **draft for your steering** (D154). The full design, measurements and constants: docs/specs/legacy.md.

## What changes for the player
1. You keep one hero per class. Its level (1-25), XP, talents and trained spells stay between runs; a death ends the run and never takes them away.
2. A run is still six zones, the Camp and Oruvath, about 35-40 minutes. Each zone is set to your hero's level when you arrive, from a little under you (zone 1) to one or two levels over (zone 6), and enemies hit harder zone by zone, so a run still gets harder as it goes.
3. A good player reaches level 25 in about 7 hours (measured 6.5-7.6), about a dozen runs: level 4-5 after the first run, 10 after about 2 hours, 20 after about 5.
4. Every run starts with a common kit at your level, your spells trained for free and gold for two potions; everything found in the run is lost at its end.
5. Every run pays Legacy points (today's Echo lines, with levels counted per hero level). They buy 13 small account-wide upgrades in the Legacy hall.
6. Two ways to play, and both pay: play for the win (low tolls, dungeons, contracts) for more Legacy points and more wins, or farm the Camp before Oruvath (up to 10 waves at double XP, each making him stronger) to level about a quarter faster (about 6 hours to 25).
7. Your first talent point (level 5) picks your hero's tree. From level 20, Legacy points open the other two trees; a respec at the start camp costs a few Legacy points.
8. From level 20 the Armoury opens: nine three-piece sets, one per talent tree, that change how the tree plays. Pieces drop from dungeon bosses and Oruvath or are forged with Legacy points; you choose up to three to wear at the start of each run.
9. Echoes become Legacy points, and everything you spent comes back to spend again. The guided first run stays, at the new levels.
10. First tuning task, found in the measure: the classes climb unevenly (at level 10 the Rogue wins far more often than the Mage and the Hunter); the fix is when each class gets its spells on the new 1-25 scale.

## Open questions (the team builds on the default until you answer)

| # | Question | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Before level 20, is a hero's talent tree fixed to its first pick, so that opening other trees is the level-20 reward? | Yes (respec inside the tree allowed; "Retire hero" restarts at level 1) |
| Q2 | Between runs the hero keeps level, XP, talents, spells and Armoury set pieces, and nothing else (no gear, gold or potions). Right? | Yes |
| Q3 | When should a good player first beat Oruvath? | Around the fourth run, near level 10-12, about two and a half hours in; the first runs stay very hard |
| Q4 | Is a Camp grind (up to 10 waves at double XP, each making Oruvath stronger) the right levelling route? | Yes: it is the only lever measured that makes levelling pay; heat or harder zones alone did not |
| Q5 | Legacy hall upgrades are shared by all heroes; talent points and trees belong to each hero; set pieces belong to their class. Right? | Yes |
