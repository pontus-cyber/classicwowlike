## Pitch for the owner (5 lines)
1. Heat is now a real choice: a run that goes into heat on purpose wins as often as one that stays ahead of the line (36.0 % against 34.3 %), because each 10 h past the par now costs the boss +2 % instead of +4 % and a Hot node turns 1 in 2 drops a rarity up.
2. Oruvath hits harder (+17 %) but has 6 % less health: the fight is about 5 rounds shorter (37), and a slow run like yours (140-150 h) faces about the same boss as before; only fast runs lost their free win.
3. The Rogue has more health (100 + 21 per level instead of 70 + 20): it is no longer the weak class (37 % against 32-36 %), and Sap holds for 4 turns as you asked.
4. Packed Satchel: the first potion in every fight is a free action; Vigor and Edge give half as much per rank to pay for it.
5. Open: the early zones still kill 19 % of runs, and the Rogue's Premeditate is still almost never the best opening.

## Owner-review items (default in bold; the team builds on the default)
1. **B56 redefined** (heat-seek within 5 pp of the planner, and it really uses heat) instead of "the default planner's wins touch Hot", which a time-efficient planner never does on any reward level tried.
2. **Free potion also in the end-boss fight** (as agreed) with **Vigor and Edge halved** (0.5 % per rank) to pay for it. Alternative: no free potion in the end-boss fight and Vigor/Edge unchanged (+0.6 pp at PALL), which fails the PALL class spread instead (11.4 pp).
3. **Boss: +17 % damage, -6 % health.** Time-efficient play gets harder (the weight-3 planner 48 % on today's rules -> 35 %); slow play stays where it was. Alternative: keep the boss and move the B1 band up about 13 pp.
4. **Rogue fix through health.** Sap at 4 turns changes nothing for autoplay (it never saps, D49) and almost nothing for the opener's choice; Premeditate stays at 2 % (B51, open choice 8 of combat v2.1).
