# UI/UX & Visual Engineer hand-off, 2026-11-10d (combat tempo, impact, status readability)

Reason: owner feedback docs/feedback/2026-11-10-1043-owner.md [stated] and the Lead's two additions (larger statuses; animation weight per class and spell). Presentation only: no rule, number or engine file changed.

## What changed
| Area | Change | Files |
|---|---|---|
| Tempo | Rounds play as beats from one timeline (`combat-feel.js` `timeline`), used both to play and to plan the round. Average round at 1x tuned to 1.4 × the M2 v2 budget (2.1 s); round cap 2.94 s (a round with many actors is compressed to it). If the engine's PACE_ROUND_ANIM_S is raised above 2.1 s, every beat scales with it (`roundPace`). Beats wait on the step's own clock, so a slow frame or late timer no longer stretches a round (same times at 1366x768 and 1920x1080 headless). | combat-feel.js (new), director.js |
| Weight per class and spell | Weight 0..1 from the engine's spell entry: max(p, w) × hits (0.75 light, 2.0 heavy), +0.15 for damage with cooldown ≥ 3, +0.3 for AoE, interrupts 0, combo finishers by points spent. Rogue: blade lands 80-130 ms after the key, short hit-stop. Hunter: bow draw 180-380 ms, arrow flight 210-320 ms with a trail (heavy shots glow). Mage: cast 70-520 ms with a charging orb (rune circle when heavy), bolt 110-400 ms. Examples: Frost Lance 0.07, Ember Bolt 0.30, Keen Shot 0.12, Heartseeker 0.75, Pyre Comet / Glacier Spike / Starfall Arrow 1.0. No UI-side table was needed. | combat-feel.js, director.js, scene.js |
| Impact | Each hit: anticipation (wind-up pose stretched to the beat), contact hit-stop (actor clock frozen, target flashes white), recoil away from the attacker, white-hot core, streak sparks, ring, slash, starburst on crits, damage number that slams in (crits larger, slower, shaking), screen shake scaled to the share of max HP (none under 3 %, up to 11 px on the hero), readable pause (380 ms) after a telegraphed heavy hit. DoT ticks of a round pop together as one beat. | scene.js, director.js |
| Telegraph | An enemy whose intent is heavy holds an overhead wind-up (new `smash` pose) with an orange ring and glow; a cast intent shows a turning rune circle in its school colour. It stays through the enemy's wind-up and ends at contact. | app.js, scene.js, art-actors.js |
| Input | Space or a click in a round skips to the end of the hit on screen; the next hit plays normally. An action key or button during a round queues one next action (gold ring + "Next: X" tag on the bar); the same key again is ignored, another replaces it; it plays when the round ends and lapses if the fight ends. Other steps still finish at once on any input. | app.js, hud.js, style.css |
| Reduce motion | New setting (follows the system's prefers-reduced-motion until chosen): no shake, recoil at 30 %, numbers without the slam. | settings.js, windows.js |
| Statuses | Icons 2.2 → 3.4 rem on unit frames, 1.7 → 2.9 rem on nameplates; rounds left in a lower-right badge (gold on the last round), stacks upper-left; buffs first, a divider, then debuffs. Buff: round green frame; debuff: square red frame with a corner notch (shape and position, not colour alone). Buff/debuff from the engine's `DEBUFFS`. Tooltip states the exact effect from the engine's status object and rules table (e.g. "Takes 6 damage at the end of each round. 12 more over the 2 rounds left. From Thorn Lash (Reed Stalker)"). The pet frame now sits under the (taller) player frame. ENRAGED shows as a status from the engine's `enraged`. | hud.js, tooltip.js, ui-text.js (`statusEffect`), engine-bridge.js, style.css |
| Fixes found on the way | Player WARD absorb was always 0 in the view (bridge read `value`, engine uses `amount`): the ward overlay and tooltip now work. Log named enemy DoTs "Rend@0": now the ability name. The hit flash painted a white rectangle over the scene (source-atop on the main canvas): it now tints only the actor. Pyre Comet arced into the ground: now arcs over. Enemy self-heal/shield casts no longer fire a bolt at the player. | engine-bridge.js, scene.js, director.js |

## Notes for the Lead and the designer
- rules.md 3 and D22 say the UI budget is PACE_ROUND_ANIM_S (1.5 s) and that input during enemy animation finishes it. Both no longer hold: proposal for the pacing rebalance: PACE_ROUND_ANIM_S = 2.1 (the measured mean); the UI then keeps its beats (it only scales up if the constant goes above 2.1). Input: a click skips one hit, keys queue one action. I may not edit specs.
- Headless measurements: the renderer runs at about 30 fps (1366x768) and 12 fps (1920x1080). Round times no longer depend on it, but animations on the scene clock run slower than real time at 12 fps in headless only.
- Combat v2 (docs/specs/combat-v2.md) will change events; `combat-feel.js` `timeline` is the one place to add beats for new event types.

## Known issues
- Mock mode (`?mock=1`) spells carry no engine entry, so every mock spell plays as light.
- Starlance Barrage plays one bolt and three impacts, not three bolts.
- Status duration shows rounds left as a number only (no sweep); a status without an expiry shows no number.
