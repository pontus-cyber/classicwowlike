## 3. UI needs
1. Learn levels: replace engine-bridge.js `learnOf` with `R.learnLevel(s, spellId)` (or `R.learnLevel(s, lvl)`) wherever a
   spell's or the trainer's learn level is shown; outside a Legacy run it returns the engine level, so no branch is needed.
2. Zone danger lines in a Legacy run: an enemy deals × `zoneDanger(level, variant)` × `tierDmg`, so the line's extra damage
   is `zoneDanger × tierDmg - 1` (zoneDanger is below 1 at the early levels, EARLY_DMG_MULT, so the product can be 1 or less). Zones: `mapView.tierDmg`; the Camp:
   `R.zoneTierDmg(s)`. Oruvath and his Shards do not take it. (The engine-bridge's `dangerOf` and `convZone`'s Camp
   `danger` are where it fits.)

   wording under GATES.legacy); it stays harmless. prep.js's fallback literal (line 254) still says Echoes.
