## 2. How it behaves
- No AudioContext before the first trusted `pointerdown` or `keydown`; until then the log is written and nothing sounds.
  Suspended while the tab is hidden.
- Buses master > sfx, ui, music (reserved) > compressor; the player's death on its own path past the duck.
- 8 voices, 2 of a cue, 50 ms retrigger gap; the lowest-ranked, oldest voice fades in 20 ms; Deathblow cues and the death
  are never dropped. Tails 60 % at 2x, 40 % at 4x and Instant; at 4x one light hit or tick per beat. Instant, or a step
  skipped as a whole, keeps its top cue and its protected cues; clicks, errors and windows always play.
- Settings window: "Mute sound" and one Volume row with three sliders (Master, Effects, Interface); saved in
  `tbwcr.ui.settings.v1`. Screenshot checked at 1366x768: the rows fit, the window scrolls as before.

## 3. Deviations to decide (Lead)
1. **Size**: the plan's budget is 16 KB for audio.js and +20 KB for game.html. Built: 299 lines, 22.6 KB (all must cues
   and 12 of the 13 should cues), game.html about +25 KB. Options: accept and raise the budget in the spec, or drop
   should cues, or minify in the build.
2. **windows.js** carries the settings rows (13 lines in its settings builder): the settings window lives there. Not prep.js
   or screens.js.
3. Pitch variation is ±0.4 % for bell cues (±3 % for the rest), so bells stay on the D pentatonic when two overlap.
4. No Dev-menu switch: D127 has sound on by default (after the first gesture) with mute in the settings, so the README's
   stage-2 "behind a Dev switch" was not built.
