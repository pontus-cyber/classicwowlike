## For the UI Engineer (rules the UI shows from a copied number)
1. **Ricochet Bolt** (row 7): src/ui/mock-data.js SPELLS text "Hits the target, then two others at 70 %." is used in
   engine mode too (`TXT.spellText` fallback, trainer list); it is now 40 %. Take the numbers from
   `R.C.SPELL.ricochet_bolt.bounces` / `.bounceFrac` for static text, or from `R.spellInfo(state, 'ricochet_bolt', t).amount`
   (`bounces`, `bounceFrac`, `bounceAmount`) in the tooltip. The bridge's `describeSpell` says "Hits every enemy for
   about X" for aoe-tagged spells, which is wrong for Ricochet Bolt (target, then 2 others at `bounceAmount`).
2. **Second Breath** (row 2, "not in the final fight"): `R.resurrectCharges(state)` = `{ boon, secondBreath, ember, now, boss }`;
   `now` lists what a death in the current fight would use, `boss` what works in the final fight. The bridge's
   `convPlayer().resurrect` list counts `p.res.meta` in every fight; use `resurrectCharges(s).now` where it is shown.
3. **Echo lines** (row 12): `summary.echoes.lines[i].echoes` is now the rounded value; the bridge already shows it.
4. No UI copy found of zone danger, boss HP, enrage round, class HP, Thornback damage or meta numbers: they come from
   the state, intents, telegraph or `C` (`enrageInRounds` reads BOSS_ENRAGE_ROUND). A future catalogue screen should
   read `C.META` and `metaOffers`. Mock-mode numbers in mock-data.js are the mock's own.

## Known issues
- Old profiles keep their stored ranks above a new maximum (e.g. Keen 3); they count as the maximum and nothing is
  refunded. Browser profiles hold no unlocks yet (the UI has no catalogue screen), so no player is affected.
- The default autopilot (`autoAction` outside combat) is a baseline, not a bot: 90 runs on real content end
  44 in zone 1, 30 in zone 2, 11 in zones 3-5 and 5 at the boss (no wins). The sim pass is the measurement that counts.
