## Cause and change

- **Cause (a), the sim's bar.** `rawDamage` in sim/lib/policy.mjs multiplied Ricochet Bolt by 1 + bounces × bounceFrac (1.6) on every cast. Autoplay casts it only with 2 or more enemies, and other AoE spells count one target. From hero 18 on, Ricochet Bolt (514 at hero 20) beat Deadfall Trap (436 raw) in the Camp AoE step and Savage Order (415) in the zone fill. Beastcall Horn (utility value, 161) never reaches a bar.
- **Change.** In Legacy runs (`legacyRules(s)`) the bounces are not counted: one target, like Volley Rain, Cinder Storm and Deadfall Trap. Level-60 runs are unchanged. balance.md 1.2 carries the rule (D166, draft).
- **Not the cause:** the technique numbers or learn levels (at hero 10 the techniques add +7.5 pp to the Hunter, 61.0 against 53.5 %), and Stormhawk (no effect in the sim: the planner always plays the Ridgewolf).
