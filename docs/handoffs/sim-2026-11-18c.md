# Hand-off: Simulation & Balance Engineer, 2026-11-18c (Camp bar template)

Status: **draft** (not approved). Task: D157 open item "sim Camp bar template (raw damage only)" (report legacy-classes-2026-11-18, Verdict 6). Only sim/ and qa/sim changed; src/, specs and game.html untouched.

## What changed
- `sim/lib/policy.mjs`: under `C.GATES.oruvathLegacy` the Camp bar (`templateBar(s, { camp: true })`) values a spell as (expected damage + damage prevented) × its usage share (`campSpellValue`). Damage prevented = `preventedRounds` × the main spell's damage per cast. `preventedRounds` reads the engine's spell data: CHILLED (rounds × (CHILL_PCT + Deep Chill), as the engine's chillValue), STUN / FROZEN / HEXED rounds × `SIM.CAMP_CC_SHARE`, SILENCED rounds × `SIM.CAMP_SILENCE_SHARE`. The exchange rate is one prevented enemy round = one main-spell cast [assumption].
- The fill step treats values within `SIM.BAR_TIE_BAND` (10 %) of the best remaining spell as tied, and the earliest-learned spell wins the tie. A small number change no longer flips a near-tie.
- The AoE step stays on raw damage. With Rime Burst's freeze counted, it took the AoE slot from Cinder Storm, and the Mage's Oruvath bench fell from 25 % to 0 % at H14 (and from 20 % to 0 % at H18, 17 % to 0 % at H22; 150 fights each). So the AoE slot is chosen by damage, which clears the Shards and adds.
- `sim/lib/params.mjs`: `CAMP_BAR_PREVENT` (true; set it to false through `SIM_OVERRIDE` to get the raw template back), `CAMP_CC_SHARE` 0.5, `CAMP_SILENCE_SHARE` 0.5, `BAR_TIE_BAND` 0.10. All four are assumptions.
- `sim/campbars.mjs`: prints the Camp bar of each class's hero at given hero levels, raw against new (native engine, both Legacy switches on).
- `qa/sim/campbar-value.test.mjs`: 6 tests. Off the switch, the bars do not depend on the flag. The prevented values match the spell data. At 2.4.1's +10 % (Glacial Tomb learned) Frost Lance leaves the raw bar at H14 and stays on the new one. Living Flame at ±4 % of Frost Lance does not flip the slot (same layout), and at +20 % it takes it. The AoE slot stays Cinder Storm. The bars at H6/10/14/18/22 are unchanged.

## Scope
- Non-Legacy runs: identical (the new path runs only when `C.GATES.oruvathLegacy` is on). Road bars are unchanged in every run, Legacy included.

## Results (native engine at a1c4f18, the 2.4.2 build, `C.GATES.legacy` + `C.GATES.oruvathLegacy`)
- Built Camp states (`node sim/campbars.mjs --hero 1-25` as a list): **no bar changes** for any class at any hero level 1-25, including 6, 10, 14, 18 and 22.
- Planner runs (60 per class at hero 6, 10, 14, 18, 22, seeds 6000-6059, legacy-hx band tables): **0 of 16 962** Camp bar settings differ between raw and new (Rogue 5 579, Mage 5 332, Hunter 6 051). On 7f808eb (before the build) it was also 0 of 17 078, and an H14 cell's results were identical seed for seed.
- Margins are now wide: at H14, Frost Lance is worth 503 against Living Flame's 284 (raw: 296 against 284). On 2.4.2, both spells fit the bar from H11 to H17. In 2.4.1's layout (Glacial Tomb learned, one slot for the two), Frost Lance at +10 % now stays on the bar (qa test). The Mage 11-15 cells need no re-read for this change.
