    creatures (variant shardspawn: HP x1.2, damage x1.3, XP x0.25), e.g. "starts a fight with this zone's creatures, made
    stronger by the dark".
16. Opening strip "no hits on you" for past rounds: tracks.js `createRuler` builds it from the current tracks, which no
    longer hold past turns. Item 16 removes the strip; if it stays, past columns should show nothing.
17. Loot "404 experience Earned in this fight" vs "+1555": the spoils window sums the step's `xp` events except why
    quest, story and contract (those show on their own windows), while director.js floats and logs every `xp` event of the
    victory step at once (site 1 on the route: kill 569, quest 2 196). Float the quest's and contract's XP when their
    windows open, or show the step's total on the loot row. The engine's events are right (`why` on each).
18. Naming rule in UI strings: director.js 515 banner "Oruvath, the Unlit Star" (use `C.BOSS_TEXT.name`, now "Oruvath");
    heat.js "the Star's line", "ahead of the Star", "the Unlit Star gains on you", "the Star burns high", "(Ascendancy)",
