## 1. Blind round 2 leftovers (ui-2026-11-14e 2.3)
| Item | Cause found | Change | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Packed Satchel potions never reach the item bar | `placeNewItems` only placed consumables new to the bags; the Satchel's are there from START_RUN. Not class-specific: the tester's Rogue had no Satchel | The run's first step out of the camp counts the start bags as new (app.js) | V-satchel (Mage and Rogue) |
| Vendor card "potions, rations and gear" vs "sells only consumables" | The hub vendor does sell 3 gear pieces; they were the last rows, below the list's fold at 1366x768 | Gear rows first (`data-i` keeps the stock index), scroll cue on the list; the card's line comes from the hub stock (`vendorMeta`, `hub.gearLeft`): "Potions and rations (gear sold out)" once the gear is bought | V-vendor (2) |
| "Train to 4" vs "Rank 2 · level 4" | Two numberings | "Train to rank N", "Rank N"; spell tip "Rank N. A trainer can raise it to rank N+1."; Train all tooltip by rank; spellbook "Rank N". The D150 subtitle is kept | V-trainer |
| Target ring after Tab | The pointer's hover ring stayed under the old target | Tab puts the hover ring away until the pointer moves (`window.__ui.rings` test hook) | V-tab |
| Bag gaps after an equip | stableBag keeps slots put (D144) | Slots stay put while the bags are open; closing (or a fresh open) forgets the layout, so they open compact | V-bags; qa/ui |
| Treasure gold before "Open the chest" | The engine opens an unguarded chest on entry; the log lines were already held, the purse was not | The view holds the chest's gold (and trap damage) out of the purse and health bar until OPEN_CHEST | V-chest |

Also fixed: the summary heat card showed literal `${HEAT_NAME.hot}` placeholders (heat.js).
