### 1.2 Contract tooltip (s0255: "repeats itself")

The held contract's tooltip had the region three times, the zone and band twice and the kind's name twice. Now the title
names the target, the subline the zone and band, the sentence the task, and the site line only the rule.

| Where | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
