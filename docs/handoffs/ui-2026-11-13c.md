## 2. Why the Rogue's "Press 1" did nothing (reproduced in the browser)
Reproduced at 1366x768 on a fresh guided profile, seed 7, as the playtest played it (the engine's autoplay choice for every
step outside the line's key). In round 1 of the first fight, key 1 sent `CAST severing_flourish`, and the bridge refused it:
`[ui] action CAST refused: Severing_flourish needs a combo point`. The refusal toast was queued behind "You set out with 1
contract..." and "Healing Draught is on item slot 8", so nothing showed.

Causes, in order:
1. **Slot 1 held Severing Flourish, a finisher.** The engine refuses a finisher at 0 combo points (src/engine/spells.js
   `castError`, line 100: `${id} needs a combo point`), and a Rogue starts every fight at 0 combo points unless it opens
   with Premeditate [source: src/engine/combat.js line 474, the fight's `p.cp: 0`]. So slot 1 is refused in round 1 of
   every fight.
2. **The finisher got into slot 1 through the engine's autoplay, out of combat.** The class's start order is
   `['gutting_jab', 'severing_flourish']` (src/constants.js `CLASS.rogue.startSpells`; src/engine/nodes.js line 115 puts them
   on the bar in that order). The playtest takes the engine's autoplay choice outside fights (the bridge's `AUTO` helper).
   Autoplay's out-of-combat policy (src/engine/autoplay.js `oocPolicy`, lines 325-326) sends `SET_BAR` until the bar equals
   `desiredBar` (lines 286-298), which orders the known spells by `AUTO_BAR_ORDER[classId]` (src/constants.js). For the
   Rogue that list has `severing_flourish` (position 11) before `gutting_jab` (position 16). Engine log of the route:
   `SELECT_CLASS START_RUN BUY_PREP TAKE_CONTRACT BUY_PREP SET_OUT SET_ITEM_BAR SET_BAR(0:severing_flourish) ...`, at the
   zone 1 hub. This is by the rules: slot order is autoplay's priority (rules.md 7.9 rule 5; rules.md "Slot order is also
   the autoplay priority"), and the finisher comes first so autoplay casts it when its combo-point condition holds.
   The Mage (Frost Lance before Ember Bolt) and the Hunter (Barbed Arrow before Keen Shot) are swapped the same way, but both
   their spells can be cast in round 1.
3. **The UI's line named a fixed key** (`key_spell1: '1'`) and pointed at slot 1, whatever the bar held. Fixed (1 above).
4. **The refusal was invisible** for up to 2.7 s, and its text showed the spell's id. Fixed (1 above).

A new player who never takes autoplay's choices outside a fight keeps the start order (Gutting Jab in slot 1), so for him
"Press 1" worked; the fault showed on the test route and would show for anyone whose bar starts with a finisher (a drag, or
autoplay's bar). [inference: from the engine log above and the start order]

Engine follow-ups (described, not changed; for the engineer and the Designer):
- `castError` and its neighbours build reasons from the spell id (`${id} is on cooldown`, `not enough resource for ${id}`,
  `${id} requires STEALTH`, `${id} needs a combo point`, ...). The bridge now maps ids to names; the engine could use
  `SPELL[id].name` itself.
- Optional, a rules call: whether autoplay's out-of-combat `SET_BAR` should leave the class's main attack
  (`CLASS.startSpells[0]`) in slot 1. Today slot order is also autoplay's priority (rules 7.9 rule 5), so changing the bar
  order means separating the two. The UI no longer depends on it.
