Character, enemy, prop and backdrop paint (art-actors.js, art-world.js) has no token role yet and stays literal: there
the exact matches were a cloud white (`--ink-bright`), the mage's staff orb (`--school-frost`) and wood trim
(`--map-ring-edge`), which would tie clouds to text and a staff to the FROZEN window. That paint is named by the art
slice (R5, the proof slice). The same rule kept `#9fdcff` in "Deathblow answered" floats literal (telegraph answer,
not frost).

## 4. Bug found and fixed during the step
`Director.play` has its own local `token` (its SkipToken). Importing theme.js's `token` into director.js was shadowed
there and threw "token is not a function" in a round. director.js imports it as `themeToken`; the canvas runs caught it
(console error), the final build has none.
