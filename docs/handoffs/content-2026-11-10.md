# Content hand-off, 2026-11-10: content for combat v2

Role: Content Designer. Branch wt-content3, pushed to main. Only src/content/ and this note changed.

## Changes
1. `zones[i].addPool` for all six zones (3 fighters each: a brute, a skulker, a beast), a row in src/content/SCHEMA.md (2.1), and the SCHEMA text for `enemies` and "How the engine uses content" updated.
   z1 hedge_ogre, hedgerow_cutpurse, barn_wolf; z2 mire_brute, reed_stalker, mist_hound; z3 cairn_brute, reiver_scout, crag_prowler; z4 dune_juggernaut, sand_stalker, dust_jackal; z5 hollow_oak, wraith_stalker, ghoul_hound; z6 slag_brute, ash_stalker, magma_hound.
2. Quests: 36 of 54 quests had a single-template or single-archetype enemy list; each now has two templates of two archetypes with at least one of caster, mender, skulker, beast, and no guardian-with-menders pair (checked for every 2- and 3-mob draw). The 18 quests that were already valid are unchanged. Text of the 36 was reworded by one clause so it names the added enemy (two sentences at most, under 240 chars). No new enemy templates.
3. Dungeon bosses: the `once` summon ability could not be a turn-5 signature, and bosses have exactly two abilities, so it was replaced by a usable one.
   - z1_belk: `ab_z1_belk_summon` replaced by `ab_z1_belk_rend` "Distraint Chain" (rend, pct 0.65, tickPct 0.2, cd 3). Ledger-Breaker (heavy, pct 2.0) stays the turn-2 signature; Distraint Chain is the turn-5 one.
   - z5_father_vael: `ab_z5_father_vael_summon` replaced by `ab_z5_father_vael_curse` "Writ of Excommunion" (curse, cd 4). Black Benediction (dark_cast 2.4) stays the turn-2 signature; the curse is turn 5.
   - All values are inside rules 12.4 ranges. Cost: these two bosses no longer summon adds. The other four dungeon bosses are unchanged. If the Lead wants the summon back, a boss would need three abilities, which the validator forbids.
