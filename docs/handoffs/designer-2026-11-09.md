## What I decided (summary)
- 6 zones of 10 levels; each zone = hub + 8 rows × 3 lanes (visible map, Slay-style adjacency); 1 dungeon per zone, 1 guaranteed rest.
- Leave a zone once you hit its exit level (speedrun lever) or after row 8.
- In-game hours per node; hours over a par raise the boss's HP and damage (Ascendancy). This is the run's time pressure.
- XP rewards are fractions of the level's requirement, so nodes-per-level stays constant from 1 to 60.
- Spells learned and ranked only at trainers (hub every zone).
- 26 talent points (every 2 levels from 10), 3 trees × 9 talents, one capstone per run.
- Single boss attempt at the end; death without a resurrect charge ends the run.
- Meta currency "Echoes", catalogue about 6 700 Echoes, target 25-40 runs to complete; raw power from meta is capped (B10).
- Starting constants were sanity-checked with a scratch steady-state calculation (not committed): about 2.5-3 rounds per normal mob and 20-30 % HP lost per 2-mob fight at levels 10-60 for all three classes. [inference: from that calculation; the simulator must confirm]

## Five decisions with the biggest effect on feel
1. **Enemy intents shown every round** (rules 7.1, 7.6). Each enemy telegraphs its action, target and damage; casts can be interrupted. This is what makes manual fights a decision instead of clicking the top button.
2. **Hours and boss Ascendancy** (rules 1.7). Every extra node makes the boss stronger, so "one more dungeon for loot" is a real trade-off and the speedrun route has a reward.
3. **Leave the zone at exit level** (rules 1.5). Creates the normal vs speedrun split (about 45 vs 26 min) without a separate mode.
4. **Bar order = autoplay priority, 7 slots for 12+ spells** (rules 5.2, 7.9). Building the bar is a build decision; the boss punishes a bar without interrupt or cleanse (balance B26, B27).
5. **Boss tests the build, not only stats** (rules 10): interrupt (Litany), cleanse (Brand), AoE/target choice (Shards + Linked), burst and sustain (Eclipse, Pulse), hard enrage at round 50.

## Owner review (defaults the team builds on)
1. Zone layout: 6 zones, hub + 8 rows × 3 lanes, whole zone map visible on entry.
2. Time pressure via in-game hours and boss Ascendancy (+4 % per 10 h over 130 h, cap +40 %).
3. One boss attempt per run; death at the boss without a resurrect charge ends the run.
4. Resurrect sources: Ember of Return (vendor 15 % stock, elite 4 % drop, max 1 carried), Shrine Boon of Return, meta Second Breath. Resurrection happens in place, mid-fight.
5. Enemy intents are always visible (telegraphs), including the target and estimated damage.
6. Talent pace: 1 point every 2 levels from 10 (26 total); one capstone per run.
7. Spells only learned and ranked at trainers, which costs gold and 1 hour; a hub with a trainer at the start of each zone.
8. Hunter picks one of 3 pets at creation (Ridgewolf, Thornback Boar, Duskcat); a fourth pet is a meta unlock.
9. Level-up does not heal; Rest, rations and potions do.
10. No armor types, no durability or repairs, no professions in v1.
11. Meta: currency name "Echoes", ~6 700 total cost, first-kill bonus per class, Oaths (difficulty modifiers) after the first kill.
12. Boss name "Oruvath, the Unlit Star" and raid name "the Sunken Observatory"; zone names left to the Content Designer.
13. PACE_MANUAL_ROUND_S = 2.5 s per manual round is an assumption to verify in the first playtest.
14. Autoplay can be toggled at any time, including mid-fight.
