## Results (details in the report)
- **One-button play is not enough to win**: 2 end-boss wins in 7 800 onebutton runs (both Mage). Reach the boss, onebutton / autoplay: classic P0 46.9 / 82.6 % (rogue 24 / 81, mage 60 / 79, hunter 56 / 88); classic PALL 68.8 / 92.8 % (51 / 93, 89 / 90, 67 / 96); expedition P0 34.6 / 74.4 %; expedition PALL 68.0 / 84.1 %. Autoplay on the slot-1 trees: 70.2 % (the button alone -23 pp).
- **Grind easy (fine)**: same fights, classic P0: onebutton dies in 1.3 % of normal fights (opener line 0.7 %), 0.3 % of grind waves, 0.4 % of dungeon trash, HP lost per normal fight 19.5 % of max HP (autoplay 19.7, opener 12.1).
- **Special fights hard (wanted)**: elite 12.6 % deaths (opener line 1.1 %), dungeon boss 32.9 % (1.4 %), rare 42.3 % (19.7 %); wind-ups answered 4.8 % (autoplay 73 %). PALL and expedition the same.
- **Think fights**: onebutton every 4.2 nodes (classic P0; 6.8 per run; four in five not special); a rotation player (masher) every 6.1 nodes, special fights 58 % think, normal and grind 10 %. Expedition: 4.6 and 6.6 nodes.
- **Misses against the owner's target**: Z1 special fights mashable (22 % think, Z2-Z5 63-89 %); a first-met enemy is no harder than a familiar one (16.0 vs 16.3 %); potions carry one-button play (nopot 31.3 % reach on classic P0).
- **Where it fails / passes**: dungeon bosses (Ammeset, Father Vael: 90-94 % think fights), elite and rare wind-ups, caster-only (dies 15.9 % vs 0.2 %) and brute-only fights (9.6 % vs 1.4 %) fail; beasts, skulkers, guardians, treasure guards and most trash pass (think 0-5 %).

## Known issues and readings
1. The think fight (20 % of max HP or a death against the opener line) is an assumption of "clearly more HP"; the report gives 10 % and 30 % too.
2. The opener's full runs are small (100 per class classic P0, 50 per class PALL and expedition P0, none on expedition PALL): about 2.3 s per run. The paired think replays carry the opener comparison.
3. The onebutton bot plays the slot-1 trees, the others the default trees; the planner on the slot-1 trees (classic P0) separates the two: the trees cost 12 pp of reach under autoplay, the button 23 pp.
4. fc at 3e16499 wrote '?' for dungeon trash and shardspawn enemies; fight kinds come from the fight, so no table changes (aa33d22 writes 't' and 's').
5. Not measured: a one-button human may still pick a target or press another key in a dangerous fight; the bot never does.
6. **fightClone bug (fixed in 43d8537):** it shared `exp` and the zone graph, so on the expedition a replayed fight (B50 replays, `--pairs`, the think replays) or a tactician look-ahead (sim/bots/tactician.mjs uses fightClone on the live state) could change the live run. The opener's look-ahead was affected the same way (playerView copies the state shallowly, then fightClone shared `exp` and the zone graph). Earlier expedition cells with replays, the opener or the tactician should be re-read or re-run; planner, greedy, speedrunner, masher and onebutton cells were not affected (no look-ahead).
