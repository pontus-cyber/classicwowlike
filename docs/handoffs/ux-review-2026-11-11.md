# UX review and readability pass, 2026-11-11 (UI/UX & Visual Engineer)

Task: the owner's 08:31 playtest note [stated: docs/feedback/2026-11-11-0831-owner.md]: "with more UI/UX improvements and a
more intuitive HUD that is easier to follow, it will be very good soon". Added during the task by the Lead: the owner's
10:12 request (the heat meter at the top centre, a calm end and a hell end, a moving pointer) and the designer's names and
unit [source: docs/specs/changes-m2b-v7.md table D, draft]: **the Furnace**; **Calm, Smouldering, Burning, Infernal**;

## 1. How the review was done
A first-time player's path in the built game at 1366x768 (Playwright, seed 1, Rogue): class select, the hub, the map, a
two-enemy fight from its engage round, an elite with a wind-up (Sisel Mistweaver, level 12 Rogue from the Dev menu)
answered with Throat Jab, loot, talents, character sheet and bags, the Camp and the end boss (Dev menu jumps). Screens
were taken at fixed times inside the answer round (300 to 2100 ms) to catch the floats, and read with the Read tool.
The same script ran on the final build ("after"). Scratch script and every shot: /tmp/uxr/ (not committed).

## 2. Top 10 readability problems
Ranked by how much each one stops a new player from following a fight or making a decision.

| # | Problem (what a first-time player meets) | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The answer moment cannot be read.** The damage number, "Interrupted!", "Off-balance!", "Silenced" and "Staggered!" all start on the same spot of the enemy within about a second. The number, the result of the player's most important decision, is hidden under "Interrupted!" (shot 1). | Words get their own lane under the numbers: 2 rows, a row is held 0.55 s, then the next word takes it and the old one fades in 0.16 s; a word waits for a free row (at Instant speed too). Numbers keep their place on the body. | **fixed** (shot 2) |
| 2 | **No "what to do now" on the player's turn.** The cue to act is spread over five places (the ruler's NOW, the target frame's Now row, the wind-up line, the bar's tags, the engage panel). Nothing says it is your turn or which key matters most. | A turn prompt over the action bar's right half, only while the game waits for the player. It names the most urgent thing the bar already marks: the opening move (Q W E); "Wind-up now: answer with [1] Throat Jab"; "Tab to it" for a wind-up on another enemy; "nothing on your bar can stop it this round"; a potion key at low health; an off-balance window with its bonus; else "Choose 1–7, A attack or X pass". The bar gets a gold frame. Hidden while a round plays and under autoplay. | **fixed** (shot 4) |
| 3 | **Nameplates jump during a round.** The off-balance chip, the stack badges, the Unaware tag and new statuses were inserted between the track and the bar or under it; the plate grows upwards, so the track and the wind-up line moved 20 to 50 px in the middle of the moment. | What comes and goes hangs under the health bar, in one wrapping row out of the flow; plates stand one row higher, so that row sits over the head, not on the body where numbers pop. The track, the wind-up line and the name never move. | **fixed** (shot 2) |
| 4 | **The time pressure competed with the fight and spoke in hours.** The heat meter sat in the middle of the combat area above the action bar; time showed as "19 h", decimals under the Oath of Haste and a day clock ("Day 2, 02:00 · 20 h"). | The Furnace at the top centre, always in view, over windows: still water on the Calm side, molten red with rising embers on the Burning and Infernal side, a white pointer moving between them, the Star's line in red. Target frame, ruler, boss frame, zone map and the in-fight toasts moved down by its height; windows open below it (the settings window scrolls on a short screen). Every name, state and unit from one table (ui-text.js `HEAT_WORDS`), table D's texts, whole tolls, no day clock. | **fixed** (shots 4, 5, 6) |
| 5 | **The same intent is shown in four places** (the opening ruler, the target frame's "Next actions", the nameplate track, the wind-up line); a wind-up in up to six. There is no single "read here" place. | Proposed: the nameplate is the one place for each enemy's next actions; the target frame keeps the target's detail (statuses, stacks, tooltips) and drops its three track rows while the nameplate is on screen; the ruler collapses to one line after the engage round ("Round 4 of 5 · ≈ 89 to you"). | not yet (combat-v2.md 10 defines all three: needs the designer) |
| 6 | **The first fight is the busiest screen of the game**: the engage panel, the first-fight hint, the target frame, the ruler, nameplates and toasts at once; the hint repeats the panel and covers the hero. | Proposed: fold the hint into the engage panel's header (one highlighted paragraph and "Got it") and dim all but the panel and the nameplates for that one round. Eased now: the turn prompt says "Open with Q W E (left panel)". | not yet |
| 7 | **The zone tracker floats over the sky in fights**, with no backing ("Story 0/3 quests · Travel on at level 19 · Row 0 of 7"), where it is not needed. | In a fight only the node's own lines stay (waves, fights left); every line has a dark backing. | **fixed** |
| 8 | **Colour meanings collide.** Red is damage to you, enemy health, the HEAVY tag, the target ring, close buttons and the Healing Draught icon; gold is NOW, the off-balance window, level badges and every panel edge. | One colour per meaning, written at the head of the UX block in style.css: ember = a wind-up and what answers it; gold = an off-balance window and your turn; frost blue = answered or staggered; red = damage to you and danger; teal = the calm side; green = healing. Applied to every new element. Not yet to the older ones (the potion icon, HEAVY, panel edges). | partly |
| 9 | **Toasts land on open windows** ("Target killed" and "3 talent points given" over the Talents title). | Proposed: while a window is open, toasts move to the left column under the unit frames, as on the map. | not yet |
| 10 | **Low-contrast floats on dark scenes**: "Silenced", "Dodge" and other words in grey-beige, and the end boss's grey damage numbers, on the night backdrop. | Proposed: a minimum brightness and one outline weight for every float, the word colours from the table in item 8. (The word lane already keeps them apart.) | not yet |

## 6. Interpretations (draft: please confirm or change)
1. **Word lane under the numbers, not above.** Numbers rise; words under them never meet a rising number and stay clear
   of the nameplate. Two rows and 0.55 s per word are my choice: at 1x the moment's four words show in about 1.1 s,
   inside the round.
2. **The turn prompt reads the bar's marks**, so it can never disagree with the bar; its priority is engage round,
   wind-up, low health, window, plain. It sits where the meter was, so nothing else moved on the bar.
3. **The Furnace stays above windows** (z-index over them) and windows open below it: part I asks that the meter is
   uncovered with every window open.
4. **The meter's main number is the zone's standing** ("20 tolls in hand" at a zone's start on schedule; 0 to 20 in a
   normal zone), as the Lead asked; run totals are in its tooltip ("Tolls so far: 47 · the Star's line: 60"), the camp
   panel, the tracker at the Camp and the summary.
5. **Whole tolls from today's engine**: the line is shown rounded up (A row 2), the standing is floor / ceil of the
   engine's heat against it. A state never disagrees with the number shown (A row 2's proof).
6. **Removed run totals beside places**: the map footer no longer shows the hours; the hub footer shows "Tolls so far".
