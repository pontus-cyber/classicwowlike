## Commands
- Play: `node sim/play.mjs --class mage --seed 42` ; scripted: `node sim/play.mjs --class mage --seed 42 --script run.txt --transcript out.md --finish bot:planner`
- Batch: `node sim/run.mjs --bot planner --class all --runs 2000 --seed-start 1000 --profile fresh --out sim/out/x`
- Pass: `node sim/pass.mjs --out sim/out/pass-2026-11-09 --half-runs 300 --style-runs 300 --variant-runs 500 --campaigns 20 [--resume]`
- Report: `node sim/report.mjs --in sim/out/pass-2026-11-09 --out docs/reports/balance-2026-11-09.md`
- Tests: `node --test qa/sim/` (24 pass, about 30 s)

Same seeds and options give identical records for any `--workers` (tested): runs are processed in blocks of SIM_RISK_REFRESH seeds and every run of a block uses the table built from earlier blocks. sim/out/ is git-ignored.

## Throughput (B37)

- Bots, ms per run on one worker of a 2-core machine shared with other agents' browser tests: random 2-3, greedy 8-50, planner 30-130, speedrunner 60-330, tactician 300-1 600 (search; gated and raced, see report). A 2 000-run planner cell of three classes takes 2-5 min on 2 workers.

## Engine bugs found
None that blocks the simulator. Two API notes for the Engineer:
1. `damage` events report the raw amount on a killing blow (e.g. 41 damage to an enemy with 20 HP left), but engine-api.md 5 says `amount` = HP lost. Repro: any kill, e.g. `node sim/play.mjs --class rogue --seed 3 --script` with `{"type":"START_RUN"}` then `auto 40`; compare the last damage event with the enemy HP before it. The simulator caps it itself.
2. Post-fight regen and level-up HP have no event, so HP at fight end can only be rebuilt from damage/heal events plus maxHp deltas (sim/lib/recorder.mjs does this).

## Spec readings and simulator assumptions (for the Lead and Designer)
All in sim/lib/params.mjs (`SIM`); BAL mirrors balance.md 6 and a test compares them.
1. Value function 1.1 has no term for spell ranks; without one the planner skips every Trainer node and plays Z1 with rank-1 spells. Added a trainer term in item-score equivalents (SIM.TRAIN_*).
2. XP_in_levels is the real fractional level gain, with the rested pool counted as banked XP (XP / xpToNext(L) valued a level-1 dive into a level-7 dungeon at 36 levels).
3. RISK_PRIOR does not depend on CL - L, so the first 500 runs of a cell dive into dungeons 6 levels up and die. Added: a 1 000-run planner warm-up per profile (seeds 9 000 000+), unseen (type, CL - L) cells shrink to the prior (not the type mean), P(death) monotone in CL - L and HP, quests keyed by size (1-3 mobs) and elites by the visible add.
4. Bar template: "finisher or second nuke" excludes situational spells (execute, elite-only, openers); usage shares differ at the Camp, where elite-only spells always qualify. The bar order puts conditional spells before always-castable ones (the hand-off fix); at the boss Open Vein (armor-ignoring bleed) is the rogue's finisher: with Severing Flourish instead the rogue's boss win rate fell from 25 % to 0 % in a test.
5. Greedy's default build "tree with highest B5 win rate" read as the B7 winner.
6. Consumable kit beyond HEAL_STOCK_TARGET: rations 5, mana draughts 3 (mage), tonics 3 and one Elixir at the Camp; purchases in two passes (2 of each, then to target).
7. Speedrunner cost in hours: node hours + P(death) × VAL_DEATH/VAL_HOUR, plus missing levels × VAL_XP/VAL_HOUR when a zone ends under its exit level. Avoids Vendor and Trainer nodes.
8. Tactician: pruned candidates (interrupts only while an enemy casts; potions below 35 % HP or when the best action dies in a sample; FLEE only then), search only in rounds with a decision, successive halving over the 8 samples, autoplay rules 1-2 kept unless a cast is pending, potion heals not counted as HP gained. Even so, the specified score (Shard HP counts like boss HP; Brand and the round-50 enrage lie beyond 3 rounds) plays the end boss worse than autoplay: on paired rogue seeds it reached the boss more often (122 vs 102 of 150) but won fewer boss fights (10 % vs 22 %). With autoplay in the boss fight only, it won 27 vs 22. A diagnostic cell `tactician-P0-bossAuto` is in the report.
9. Metric readings: B14, B15, B25 over runs that reach the boss; B16 "normal" = Quest, Grind and Treasure fights; Camp-wave deaths count as Z5-Z6; B26 removes interrupt-tagged spells only (stun spells still interrupt the Litany); B30 per-win Echoes include the first-kill line (every P0 win is a first kill); B33 at hub and Camp trainer visits.
10. PALL sets reachedBoss, bossKills 1 and firstKill for all classes. Campaigns use the P0 planner's final table, frozen.

## Known issues
- The planner's model ignores mana; the mage's long fights are partly mana-bound.
- With 20 campaigns per class, 5-run blocks hold 60 runs, so B32 dips of a few points are noise.
- The tactician runs at 300-1 600 ms per run; 2 000 runs per class take about 30 CPU-minutes.
