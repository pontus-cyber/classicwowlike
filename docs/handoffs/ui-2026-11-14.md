## 2. Findings for the Lead, the Designer and the Core Gameplay Engineer (draft proposals)
1. **The joint line outlives the engage round.** The engine keeps G8 on `G8.withEngage` until the wind-up is answered or
   lands or the fight ends (D141's yield rests it after ONB_LINE_STEPS when another beat has a line). Measured on seed
   8736, Rogue, Instant, scratch script outside the repo: on autoplay, Ambush kills the Hexer in round 1, and round 2 still
   shows "Round 1 is yours: open with W on the wind-up ..." with the Hexer dead and nothing to point at (screenshot 3); in
   round 3 G12 takes the top line and the coach line clears. Following the line (Sap on the Hexer), round 2 shows it with
   the ring on the wind-up badge, and it goes in round 3 when Sap cancels the wind-up (G8 learned). Proposal (engine,
   draft): after the engage round, G8's line becomes `G8` (its own line) while the winding-up enemy lives, and null once it
   is dead, as G7's line goes at round 2. The UI needs no change for it. D149 (S3's wind-up on the Hexer's turn 1, not
   built yet) ends the line in round 2 for a W opener, but not on the Rogue's autoplay route (Ambush, D49).
2. **Long toasts on the hub.** `placeToasts` keeps the stack clear of the banner and of windows, not of the hub's panel.
   Unwrapped, the 84-character cap line ran over the panel's top-left corner at 1366x768 (seen on the first run's
   screenshot), so it wraps (`.toast.long`, at most 40rem): measured at x 483-883 px, y 200-238 px, clear of the panel, and
   inside the window at 1920x1080 too (R(2b)). Other long toasts on the hub could meet the panel; none did in the regress.
3. Not done (optional in the engine hand-off): the rescue banner could say how many rescues are left
   (`tutorialView.rescues`, `rescueMax`); it needs a content line.
4. Texts (draft, UI): `SKIP_LINE` "You skip the guide. New things get one hint each." is the old log line, unchanged and
   now exported; content could own it as it owns `rescueCapLine`.
5. The cap line's toast is up for TOAST_MS (2.7 s, two lines); the log keeps it.
