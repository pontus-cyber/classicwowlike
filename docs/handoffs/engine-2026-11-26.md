## What changed
1. **Reinforcements are announced (engine, every build).** A fight of 2 or more enemies with a skulker calls 1 more enemy (Signal, rules 7.12) at the end of round 4 (engage round, then the skulker's script turn 3), once per fight, unless the caller dies or is stopped first. `signalPlan(content, tids)` gives `{ count, round }` before a fight; the map view carries it as `calls` on quest, grind (wave 1) and elite nodes and `fightCalls` per dungeon trash fight (rows view too). `R.fightSignal(state)` gives `{ count, round, by, name }` for the fight under way. View only: no state, no draw.
2. **Map node badges (UI).** A seen, reachable fight node shows its level (the existing colour rule, `conColor`; red only at "deadly") and its real enemy count in words, "3 foes", the called enemy included, on a neutral ink chip (it was "×2" on a red chip: red is danger's alone, D120). Quiet nodes keep D128 (a level pip; count on hover). The Key panel's hover rows add "[1] Fight level" and "[3 foes] Every enemy", and its tooltip says what the level colours mean.
3. **Tooltips and panels list every enemy (UI).** Node tooltips: "Foes: 2 enemies" with role chips, then "Calls 1 more in round 4 unless stopped."; a dungeon reads "Fight 2: 3 enemies, calls 1 more in round 4". Quest panel: each enemy at its level, then "Hedgerow Cutpurse calls 1 more in round 4 unless stopped." The grind panel the same; the elite panel now lists every enemy at its own level (the escort's lower) with the call line; the dungeon panel lists each fight. All draft.
4. **No self-contradicting tooltips (UI).** An elite with an escort reads "A powerful enemy at level 16, 4 levels above you, and its escort." (was "Foes: 2 enemies" then "One powerful enemy"). A seen chest reads "It may be trapped." ("Unguarded" then "may be guarded or trapped" before); a guarded one "watched by a guard. It may also be trapped." A node lost to the Dimming shows only its Dimmed line (no enemies, no type text, no "it can no longer be done" beside "starts a fight").
5. **Demo quests (engine, demo runs only: `state.demo`; demo.md 4.4, draft).** Each Quest node of a demo zone draws (one draw, as before) from the zone's quests not yet set up; once all 9 are out, a Quest node is set up as a Grind node, so no quest (same giver and text) is offered twice in a run. A demo quest's level is capped at the zone's band top (outer ring's top, the view's `bands`): QUEST_LEVEL_OFFSETS' +1 no longer lifts an outer-ring quest above "Levels 1-2" (a level 3 quest in that zone was the skipper's death). Zone 1 of a guided run keeps its draws (its scripted roles); its levels take the cap.

## Notes for the Lead
- **D50 question for the Designer.** An elite's `calls` follows from all its enemies, its own hidden archetype included. In zones 1-2 the only skulker elites are rares (Hobb Tollwright, Skerrow), so an elite with an escort that "calls 1 more" while its escort is no skulker tells a rare. I kept the call (the brief asks for every enemy that will appear); the alternative is to drop `calls` on elites whose own archetype is the caller.
- **Quest panel names, map does not.** Who answers the signal is drawn in the fight (fight stream), so no panel can name it; the line says "1 more".
- **Rule reading.** "No quest more than 1 level above the zone's band": capped at the band top itself, which also removes the cited case (engine level 5 shows as 3 in a band shown as 1-2). The +1 offset still acts inside the band.
- **Grind instead of a repeat.** About half the demo zones had more Quest nodes than the zone's 9 quests (up to 17). Those nodes are Grinds now. More quests in content would let them stay quests (Content).
- **Guided zone.** Zone 1 of the guided run keeps its quest draws (scripted roles re-bind quests at play), so it can still name one quest on several nodes before they are entered (2026-11-15 behaviour). Not touched.
- **Not mine / not done.** Dimmed encounters show no enemies before the fight (unchanged). The template summon (zone 4 rare, at half health) is not announced; not in the demo. Quiet map nodes keep D128 (pip only) to keep the owner's clutter rule; the count is on the reachable ones and in every tooltip.
- **demo-campaign build path.** My first campaign run did not rebuild /tmp/tbwcr-wt-engine/demo.html (its mtime stayed); the run quoted below used `--file` on a fresh `--demo` build. Worth a look by QA.
