**What scene.js keeps** (955 to 680 lines): layout, actors and where they stand, poses (`poseP`, `TELE_POSE`), anchors,
the effect records and their lifetimes, the projectile flight path, particle and ambient motion, the floats' lanes,
slam, rise and fade, the hit flash's scratch canvas (`drawFlashed`), and the effect spawners (`impact`,
`windupResult`, `heal`, `aura`, `levelUp`, `burst`), which build records with their colours.

**Interface** (documented at the top of each file). An art function takes the canvas context, then geometry in CSS px
(an actor's body box `an` from `anchor()`, the ground line, `u` px per art unit, the clock `t`, an effect's progress
`k`), then paint (a zone's `THEMES` entry, a colour, or tokens read through theme.js). It reads no game state, keeps no
state and queries no DOM. A function that throws sparks (telegraph, off-balance, burn, charge-up, bolt) **returns**
them as particle records and scene.js adds them; each calls `Math.random` in the same order as before, which is what
keeps the seeded canvas checks pixel-equal.

**Rule U6 test**: `qa/docs/ui-rules.test.mjs` scans every `src/ui/art-*.js` for static imports, re-exports, `import()`
and `require()`; only `./util.js` and `./theme.js` pass. A third test checks the scanner on the forms it must catch.
Today all five art modules pass (art-actors, art-fx, art-icons, art-props, art-world).

**Canvas fonts** (exact values only): the weights 700, 800 and 900 now read `--fw-bold`, `--fw-heavy`, `--fw-black`
(floats, sleep letters, quest marks). The families stay written: `FCT_FONT` differs from `--font-num` only by
`-apple-system`, and `Georgia, serif` matches no type token. Merging them is the art slice's call (tokens.css comment
updated).

**Contact sheet**: `tools/art-sheet.mjs` draws, straight from the art modules and tokens.css (Playwright serves src/
to the page; nothing is built, no game runs): heroes and pets, the six enemy archetypes in all seven zone tints, ranks
with their auras, every beast kind and the wolf in every zone, the boss's three phases, the shard, portraits, every set
piece, effects on an actor (three telegraphs, off-balance, rings, sleep, ward, stun, frozen, hexed, charge-ups),
effects in the air (every bolt kind, core, starburst, rings, slash, pillar, particles, floats, every mote kind), all
84 icon glyphs, all 19 icon backgrounds and three special icons, and all eight backdrops plus the farmland hub. 11
sections, 246 cells, 1600x4598, about 2 s. `Math.random` is seeded and every drawing gets the same clock, so the same
sources give the same bytes; console errors fail the run. Default output `/tmp/art-sheet.png`; `--theme lantern` draws
the proposal block. The committed reference is `docs/art/sheet-current.png` (current theme, 3.5 MB; sha256 913ab48c...).

## 4. Findings and open items (draft: please confirm or change)
1. **The stormhawk's portrait is empty.** `drawPortrait(..., 'beast')` sends a hawk through `drawBeast` to `drawHawk`,
   which draws it 70 units above its feet, above the portrait's frame. hud.js `petPortrait` and the pet choice in
   screens.js call it that way. Seen on the sheet (Portraits row), not checked in the game, not changed here (R5 is
   behaviour-identical). A one-line fix in `drawPortrait` (a hawk case) for whoever owns the next art step.
2. **Telegraph colours are in app.js** (`HEAVY_COL`, `WINDUP_COL`, `TELE_COL`), not tokens; the sheet repeats their
   values. Candidates for `--tele-*` tokens in the readability pass.
3. **The effect spawners' colours stay in scene.js** (17 literals): impact sparks and the slash in white, the heal
   plus, the wind-up result's frost and ember, the level-up flash, float outlines, the hit-flash tint. A next step can
   move the spawn recipes into art-fx.js as functions that return records, with the same `Math.random` order.
4. **Not on the sheet**: NPC looks (`npcLook` is in app.js) and the actor poses (only the idle frame at one clock).
5. **Fonts**: `FCT_FONT` and `--font-num` differ only by `-apple-system`; merging them is a visible change on no
   platform I know of, but it is not exact, so it waits for the art slice.
