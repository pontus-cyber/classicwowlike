# QA hand-off 2026-11-25: full-run twin mirrors the junk auto-sell

Role: Playtest & QA Lead. Status: **draft**. Test code only (qa/browser/full-run.mjs); no file in src/ or sim/ changed, game.html not touched.

## Cause
`qa/browser/full-run.mjs` failed 0/3 at 1366x768, with and without `--legacy`, on one divergence per run (player.gold and the bag list, e.g. rogue Legacy step 76: gold ui 469 vs engine 430; mage and hunter Legacy step 47 and 54: gold 242 vs 166; without Legacy gold 184 vs 100, 340 vs 196, 232 vs 88). The twin's bag held extra items and its gold was lower in every case.

Cause: since 8ded336 (ui-2026-11-20b item 1) the UI bridge (src/ui/engine-bridge.js `step`, after the action's switch) sends the engine's `SELL_JUNK` in the same step whenever a vendor is open and the bags hold junk. The twin did not, so it kept the junk and had less gold. The first vendor visit with junk in the bags is the first divergence. The D169 Hunter package and the Lantern art commits are not involved: the divergences are only gold and bag contents, and they disappear with the twin change alone. No game bug found.

## Fix
`twinStep(s, a)` in full-run.mjs: `R.step`, then, if `s.service.kind === 'vendor'`, the bags hold junk and `SELL_JUNK` is legal, `R.step(SELL_JUNK)`. Same action, same point as the bridge (after the action, before the next comparison). It replaces the plain `R.step` for the policy's action and for the UI's own actions (`mirrorOwn`). The policy's own junk sale in policy.mjs is unchanged; it simply finds no junk left.
