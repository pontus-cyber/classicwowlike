## 4. Interpretations (draft: please confirm or change)
1. **Crimson for the Deathblow.** The UX colour table keeps red for "damage to you and danger". The Deathblow is the most dangerous thing in a fight, so it gets the deepest red with a skull. That keeps it apart from the ember of an ordinary wind-up and the gold of "your turn".
2. **Which answers are named.** The line names those usable now (`now`). If there are none now, it names those ready by its round, as "R3: 1 Throat Jab". At most three, sure ones first. A chance gets "?" and the tooltip says why. The prompt picks the first sure one.
3. **Bar answers before the round.** windupView lists a bar spell whose cooldown is over by the Deathblow's round. The bar marks it "R3" (dashed ring) in the rounds before and "Answer" in its round. An off-balance window's tag takes precedence over "R3" on the same button.
4. **The autoplay hold.** 1.6 s at 1x in an engage round where autoplay answers a wind-up (a presentation pause, scaled by speed, none at Instant). Without it, autoplay's default 0.35 s would show the hint too briefly to read.
5. **MAIMED replaces its status icon** with the large badge, so it is not shown twice. The potion shows the cut amount only while MAIMED. The un-cut amount is not shown: potionInfo gives only the cut one, and the UI does not compute it.
