## 4. Interpretations (draft: please confirm or change)
1. "Within 2 hops of the front" = nodes not yet Dimmed whose hop distance from the Shardfall is at most front + 2
   (before the grace ends: the Shardfall and its neighbours). The count shown is the node's `dimsIn` (so the Dungeon's
   hold counts once its type is known).
2. Fog states: "seen" = in full view now (hops within the vision radius), "remembered" = seen before, "type known" and
   "unknown" as mapView's `know`. The map's key names them.
3. Region tints follow the nodes whose region is known, so without charts the player sees the regions of the land he
   has seen; names stand at the middle of a region's known ring 2-3 nodes.
4. Signposts name the next zone (the spec's "next zone's name"); the Road's own name (D51) is in its tooltip.
5. Main attack = the class's first start spell. Its average includes crits; misses, procs (Twin Fangs, unique procs),
   off-balance windows and conditional bonuses (Riposte, poison stacks, an elite target) are left out, and the foe is
   of the player's level with no armour, said in every tooltip. "Damage bonus" is the % that holds for every attack;
   the main attack's own % (school and spell talents) is in its tooltip.
