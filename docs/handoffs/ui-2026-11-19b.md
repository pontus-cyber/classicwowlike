## 4. Known issues and open items
1. Content lines with "Reserve" remain in the tutorial table (H22 "pay from your Reserve", H23 "Points you keep become
   Reserve"): content's lines, not touched here; with hints on they can show in a Legacy run. For the content role.
2. The content lines are written for plural counts; the UI makes "1 contracts", "1 camp points", "1 spells" and "1 Legacy
   points" singular (ui-text.js `econLine`). Content may prefer its own singular forms.
3. D160 refunds Packed Satchel's price too (the old 4.6 migration kept it unrefunded); a refunded profile's next summary may
   show the Satchel nudge once more (its own cap holds). Old purchases made with the switch off after the mark are not
   refunded again (the mark is once per profile).
4. The second camp line keys on the profile's run count (runs 1, not a first run): a reload in that camp shows it again;
   a profile whose first run was not lean (guide skipped before D152) would see it at its second camp too.
5. The level-up banner in a fight waits for the fight's end; the frame's level and the XP bar change at once, as before.
6. The sound cue is the existing `levelUp` cue at the banner's moment; no new cue (audio spec).
7. Draft lines (owner): the refund note's hall line, the wing's names and texts ("Second talent tree", "Third talent tree",
   "The Armoury", "Not yet open", "From hero level 20"), "Every entry in the hall is yours.", "Camp points" as the tooltip's
   title, "Take it for camp points".
