## Guesses and notes
- Elites carry exactly 1 ability (rules 12.2 / SCHEMA); rules 2.3 says "1-2". I followed 12.2 and dropped the second ability of five authored elites; they remain in the draft only as the first listed ability.
- Quest `sizeWeights` use tens (e.g. [40,40,20]); mean sizes lie between 1.6 and 2.4. `enemies` subsets are not set, so quests draw from all zone normals.
- Unique `affix` and `effect` pairings are my choice (spread over crit, power, armor). Epic uniques avoid armor. Slot `weapon` for class weapons; ilvl 62 comes from the engine.
- Quest "Rescue" and "deliver" are flavour only. Zone ids in enemy ids are prefixed `z1_`..`z6_`; abilities `ab_<enemy id>_<template>`.
- `texts` (spell/talent/pet/boon flavour) is left empty: optional in SCHEMA.md and out of the task scope.
- Affix suffix keys follow SCHEMA 2.7; the draft's `sta+spi` pair has no engine key and is not used.
- Camp name, boss intro text and hub texts are mine; the boss name Oruvath is not used in content text.
- Tone check: all names are original; none was copied from a game, book or film. [assumption] A quick similarity review by the owner is still advisable for "Barrowdeep", "Mournwood" and "Greywater Fens", which are plain compounds.
