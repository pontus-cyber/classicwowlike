## Owner review (only what changes the game for you)
1. **Full unlocks are a smaller edge.** A fully unlocked profile now wins about 2 runs in 3 (was 9 in 10); a fresh profile about 1 in 4. Vigor and Edge stop at 4 ranks (+4 % HP, +4 % Power); Keen, Quick Study and Prospector give a quarter less.
2. **Wayfinder is gone.** Travelling to the next zone always takes 2 hours. With it, full unlocks let you reach the boss before its time pressure starts, which made the boss about 12 points easier to beat. Swift Boots no longer needs it.
3. **Greater Healing Draught heals 37.5 %** (was 50 %); a plain draught heals 35 %.
4. **The end boss** has 13 900 HP (was 13 500) and Final Dark starts at round 57 (was 55).
5. **Dungeons are worth entering again.** In zones 3-6 dungeon enemies hit less hard than the open world (at level 60 ×1.5 instead of ×2.0), and dungeon bosses deal a bit less damage. Expect about one dungeon per run.
