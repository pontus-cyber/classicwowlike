## 2. Findings (draft)
1. **The rescue banner's title overlaps the hub's panel for its 2.2 s** at 1366x768 (screenshot 1): `placeBanner` only moves
   banners in fights, and the rescue banner ends as the hub mounts. It predates this work (the second line does not cause
   it). Option: let `placeBanner` keep clear of `TOAST_KEEP` panels outside fights too, or a smaller title for kind 'rescue'.
2. The wrap step needs no content limit, but a toast is still one stack: a long toast moved left takes the shorter toasts
   below it along.
