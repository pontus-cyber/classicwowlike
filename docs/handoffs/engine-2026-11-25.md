## What changed

1. **Legacy Ferocity** (h_a2): `ORUVATH_TALENT.h_a2.petDmgPct` 0.10 -> **0.045** a rank. Same getter as D157: Legacy runs with `C.GATES.legacy` and `oruvathLegacy` both on; otherwise 0.18.
2. **Lost Technique I (Hunter)** in a Legacy run with both gates on: the entry (sp_h1, price unchanged) teaches no Ricochet Bolt. `nodes.js startRun` drops it from `s.meta.spells` and sets `s.meta.keenRicochet` (the field exists only then, so every other state is as before). `spells.js keenRicochet(s)`: while that is set and the hero is at power 20 or more (Ricochet Bolt's learn level, hero 9), every Keen Shot cast also hits up to two other living enemies for 30 % of its hit (`C.KEEN_RICOCHET`: the arm `ksb`'s rule; Keen Shot's talent bonus applies, no crit bonus; the opening shot of the engage step does not bounce, as in the arm). `spellInfo('keen_shot')` then carries `bounces / bounceFrac / bounceAmount`, so the tooltip gives the bounce line Ricochet Bolt had. Level-60 runs, and Legacy runs with oruvathLegacy off, keep Ricochet Bolt.
3. **Hall card (draft)**: under ?legacy=1 the bridge's sp_h1 entry carries `passive`; the card reads "Keen Shot also hits up to two other enemies for 30 %", its tooltip "A passive for the Hunter: from hero level 9, every Keen Shot also hits up to two other enemies for 30 % of its damage. No spell to learn, no bar slot.", the card face still "From hero level 9", the icon is Keen Shot's, the next run's lines name the passive. Outside Legacy the card is Ricochet Bolt's as before.
4. **Sim templates**: the D166 one-target rule in `sim/lib/policy.mjs` stays; it now acts only on states that hold Ricochet Bolt (level-60 runs), and Keen Shot keeps one target's value (comment and balance.md 1.2). A Legacy Hunter with the v3d hall at hero 20 and 25 knows no Ricochet Bolt and its zone and Camp bars equal the bars without the technique (qa/sim/hunter-tech.test.mjs). `sim/hunter-arms.sh`: `ksb` is built in, so the arm leaves the copy as it is; `fer<NN>` edits the built 0.045; the rb* arms now act on level-60 runs only.
