## 1. The rule (legacy.md 4.7 E7, D161)
In a Legacy run under the switch, every XP award of a Camp wave (its kills and its wave bonus; at the Camp, outside
Oruvath's fight) is the E2 award × `min(1, diffMod(Z - L))`: Z the Camp's level (`state.hero.camp`, fixed at arrival), L
the hero's power level when paid, diffMod the rules 4.4 level-difference modifier. In full up to the Camp's level, then ×
0.95, 0.90, 0.70, 0.50, 0.30 at 1-5 power levels past it, × 0.10 (XP_GREY_MOD) from 6. Oruvath's fight (Shards, adds),
zones, contracts and every other award keep E2. No new constant, no field or event change.

Engine: progress.js `campFalloff(s, Z)` applied in `eventXp`'s Economy v2 branch; legacy.js `campWaveXp(s)` (at the Camp,
not in the boss fight; the wave bonus is paid after `s.fight` is cleared, so the test is the zone, not the fight kind).

## 2. Numbers (switch on: legacy, oruvathLegacy, economyV2)
Bench: `node sim/campgrind.mjs` (new). A hero built at the Camp at hero level H (debug.build: spells trained, rare gear at
the power level, default talents, 5 potions; Camp at power + 3), grinding as the QA bot did before its fix (the Inn once
under half HP, autoplay in fights). Seeds 1-10, three classes (30 grinds a row). Pace minutes = the engine's
`summary.minutes.manual`, the same measure as the run cells. Runs: planner v2 cells, `sim/legacy-run.mjs grid --native
--oruvath --econ --levels 1,5,10,15,20,25 --runs 60 --seed 9000` (180 runs a level), hero XP a run / manual minutes.

**A. Long grind from arrival at hero 5** (`--hero 5 --waves 700 --at 5,6,7,8,9,10,15,20`; the QA bot's case): hero XP a
pace minute while the hero is at that level.

| Hero level during the grind | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 15 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before (Camp XP / pace min) | 154 | 183 | 203 | 221 | 237 | 242 | 258 | 266 |
| After | 154 | 183 | 182 | 110 | 25 | 25 | not reached | not reached |
| Runs, planner v2 (XP / pace min) | 176 | | | | | 198 | 189 | 191 |

Whole grind: before 247 XP a pace minute, Rogue and Hunter reach hero **25** in 700 waves (249-271 pace minutes); after
53 a pace minute, they end at hero **11.2** (Mage: 8 of 10 die early either way, end 9.6 before, 6.8 after).

**B. A Camp the hero has just reached, at hero 5 / 10 / 15 / 20** (`--hero 5,10,15,20`):

| Arrival hero level | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First 20 waves, before = after (XP / pace min) | 154 | 158 | 159 | 166 |
| Up to 200 waves, before | 211 | 197 | 196 | 164 |
| Up to 200 waves, after | 110 | 125 | 131 | 164 |
| Runs, planner v2 | 176 | 198 | 189 | 191 |

The 200-wave rows at hero 10-20 are thin: the debug-built Rogue and Mage die within about 15 waves there (deaths 20, 21,
30 of 30), so most of those grinds end before the hero passes the Camp; at hero 20 every grind dies before it, which is why
before and after agree.

**Reading.** The farm is closed: past the Camp's level the waves fall to 110 and then 25 a pace minute (13 % of a run), and
a 700-wave grind gains 6 hero levels instead of 20. But in the first one to two hero levels after arrival (while the hero is
at most 2 power levels past the Camp), a Camp wave still pays 154-183 a pace minute, 79-100 % of a run's 176-198. The pace
model gives a Camp wave no travel or decision time (about 0.4-0.6 pace minutes a wave), so a wave at the hero's level is
about as fast as the XP in a run. "Clearly below at every hero level" therefore holds from 3 power levels past the Camp, not
in that first window. **QA's "48 XP a pace minute"** (qa-2026-11-19b 2) is not the engine's pace: summary.minutes.manual
counts 0.4-0.6 minutes a wave, not 2, and before this change an outlevelled grind paid 200-290 a pace minute, more than runs.
