## Interpretations (draft: please confirm or change)
1. **Which bar spells answer a wind-up.** The engine does not say it per spell. The UI reads the engine's spell entry as autoplay rule 3 does (tracks.js `answerKind`): tag `interrupt` or `stun`, or a spell that hexes or freezes, is "stop"; against a heavy wind-up, tag `defensive` or a taunt is "avoid". I checked this against src/engine/spells.js: control on a CC-immune enemy goes to `interrupt` (so Hexbind stops a dungeon boss's wind-up), Snare Trap stuns at once, Feign Stillness and Beastcall Horn send a heavy hit to the pet. "Avoid" is a hint, not a promise: a ward that does not take it all or a dodge that fails does not answer it, and the tag says "Avoid", not "Answer". **Engine request:** a per-spell field for the wind-up playing now, for example `spellInfo(...).answers` or `legal.spells[id].answers` = `'stop' | 'avoid' | null`, so the UI stops reading tags.
2. **Send the Pet.** engine-api.md says Send the Pet answers a heavy wind-up among its `pet` turns while the pet lives, but the preview lists it under `pet` only. The engage card combines the two (heavy wind-up in `pet` turns = "Answers the wind-up"). **Engine request (small):** list it in `engagePreview` `stagger` too, or as its own field.
3. **One line over each enemy.** It shows the first wind-up that still needs an answer, else the first one at all. On a dungeon boss whose round-3 wind-up was answered, the line moves on to the round-5 wind-up in round 3; the stopped one has its float, log line and badge.
4. **Toasts.** 2 at once, 6 waiting, merging and the two places above are my choices for "queue or merge them".
5. **Greyed-out Disrupt in play.** With the real content every dungeon boss shows its turn-2 wind-up in the engage round (SIGNATURE_TURN 2 ≤ INTENT_TRACK_LEN 3), so the greyed state cannot appear in play today. Part J checks it on the real engage round loaded with the boss's `windup` flags removed (`loadState`).
