## 5. Open items
1. **For the designer (not changed here):** the tester's hall points. The 70-point stat ranks (+1 %) are too small to
   notice; Deeper Pockets (bags full by fight 4-5 of zone 2) and Cartographer are not noticed either; Stormhawk 150, Second
   Breath 200 and the level-20 wing were never affordable in four runs. These are prices and effects in C.META_HERO
   (legacy.md 4.7 E5) and need a design decision.
2. Junk: the tester also asked to auto-sell or stack junk and saw the same junk twice in one loot window (s0426). This is
   not in this task's list and is left for the Lead's triage.
3. A technique of another class counts toward "N unlocks within reach", the same as its enabled Buy button (the hall is
   account-wide). If the owner wants the count limited to the current hero's class, both the line and the Next entry would
   change together.
4. Off-class detection reads the item's stats only. A future class whose main stat is Spirit or Stamina would need its
   stat added to `MAIN_STATS` in engine-bridge.js.
