## 3. Choices made (draft, for the Lead)
1. The Homeward Bell stays legal while the Roads are closed: the emergency exit (once per run). D142 names the Roads only.
2. The no-lock rule of section 1 (Gorrak lost to the Dimming or the gate unable to open), so zone 1 can always be left by a
   Road. Without it a slow player would be held until the Bell.
3. The pointer is a line of G13, not a new beat: G13 already owns "how to leave zone 1", and its lowest priority keeps it
   under every urgent line. The line is cleared at the start of his fight because D141's yield would otherwise rest G9 after
   2 steps in favour of "Face Gorrak first" in the middle of his fight (seen in a first version of this change).
4. ONB_LINE_RETURNS is a new named constant (1) rather than ONB_BEAT_RETRIES, which is G8's extra wind-ups.
5. G10's "in view" is the map's own site-mark rule, so the Tracker's Notes radius counts as it does on the map.
