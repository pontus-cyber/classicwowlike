## 2. Engine notes (item 4)
- rules.md 7.12: answered includes "a heavy one from which the player lost 0 HP, 7.13 (3)"; landed is "played, and for a
  hit or rend the player or the pet lost HP to it". 7.13 (3) counts a hit that "went to the pet because of TAUNT or feign"
  and excludes "a hit the pet takes by its own aggro draw". combat-v2.md 17.1: landed Deathblow = DEATHBLOW_EMPOWER and MAIMED.
- src/engine/combat.js `enemyAct` does exactly that: `avoided` stays true for a pet target only when `redirect` (TAUNT or
  FEIGN), and `lost` is set by any HP lost, the pet's included; `landWindup` then MAIMS the player. So: **no contradiction,
  no engine change.** A heavy Deathblow drawn at the pet that hits the pet maims the player; one the pet takes under TAUNT
  is answered.
- Pinned by a new test in qa/engine/s07d-v13.test.mjs ("aimed at the pet by its own aggro draw ...", 6 seeds on the fixture
  elite): windupView target 'pet', maims true, Send the Pet an answer; a pet hit lands it (empower `deathblow`, `maimed`;
  MAIMED on the player), a miss is neither, never a stagger; with Send the Pet: stagger `avoided`, no MAIMED.
- For the Designer (not changed): a Deathblow aimed at the pet costs the player nothing but MAIMED; whether a pet-drawn
  Deathblow should maim at all is a design question. The screen follows the engine.

## 4. Interpretations (draft: please confirm or change)
1. **"If you wait" on the compact marks.** The chip ribbon and the ruler say "IF YOU WAIT" (the full "answered if you
   wait" does not fit a small chip or a ruler column); the row and the line say "answered if you wait" in full; each mark's
   title says it too. Where waiting is not the answer (an add awake), a soft answer reads "If not hit".
2. **Which actions are marked "Breaks Sap".** The attack when its target is the sapped Deathblow enemy; bar spells that deal
   damage (the spell entry has damage, hits or a DoT) and hit it (single target on it, or an area spell). Read from the
   spell entries, as `answerKind` does (a stopgap until the engine says per action whether it breaks Sap). No confirm step:
   the warning shows before the key is pressed.
3. **LETHAL** compares the estimate with current health (no shield or heal assumed) and only for hits on you; a soft
   cancel keeps it (attacking still lets it land); a hard cancel (trap, freeze, interrupt) removes it.
4. **Headline lane.** Above the hero's head (nothing floats there) and under an enemy's word lane (its nameplate is
   above). A second headline on the same actor takes the next row.
