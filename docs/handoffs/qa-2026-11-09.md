## Top five playtest findings
1. **Autoplay does nothing in the visual build** (BUG-QA-01). Every round of every fight must be played by hand (a run that reaches the boss has about 370-390 rounds), so "turn on autoplay" and the speedrun route from the brief cannot be played today.
2. **Zone 1 is played with the two starting spells.** Spells of levels 4, 6 and 8 (Shadowstep Strike, Arcane Ward, Mend Companion, Throat Jab, Mark Prey) are learned only at a trainer; zone 1's hub trainer is visited at level 1, and trainer nodes are rare (weight 4). For all three classes the median bar leaving zone 1 holds 2 spells and the first new spell is learned at level 9-10, at the zone 2 hub [source: qa/browser/playtest.mjs, 40 seeds per class]. Manual fights in zone 1 are "press 1 or 2", and the Mage has no Arcane Ward through its deadliest zone.
3. **The deadliest early node is the one the map hides.** Zone 1 quests are the most common non-boss death even with careful play (Mage 13, Rogue 11, Hunter 4 of 40 runs). All 28 fatal ones had 2 or 3 enemies (17 had 3), 22 were 1-4 levels above the player (zone 1 rows 5-8 are levels 6-10 while the player is 4-8), and 25 started at 60 % HP or more [source: playtest.mjs]. The map tooltip does not show the enemy count (BUG-QA-02), so the player cannot see the risk before spending the hours.
4. **Two of the brief's big choices collapse.** (a) "Leave the zone at its exit level" never comes up on a normal route: the player reaches 10 × z only at rows 7-8 (median level leaving zones 1-6: 9-10, 18-19, 27-29, 38, 48-49, 58), so Travel on is the forced end of every zone, not a speedrun lever, and the player enters each zone 1-3 levels under its band. (b) Dungeons and elites sit above a player who follows the zone (zone 1 dungeon: trash level 7 and boss 9 in rows 4-6, where the player is level 4-7; elites at row level + 2). The engine's default policy, which enters them, dies in the zone 1 dungeon in 19 of 100 Rogue and 21 of 100 Hunter runs and reaches the boss in 4 % (Rogue) and 15 % (Hunter) of runs. The QA player, which waits until it is within a level, entered 2-9 dungeons and 1-9 elites in 40 runs per class (a typical boss run: no elite, no dungeon) and reached the boss far more often (Rogue 25 %, Hunter 62 %). Risk versus reward becomes "skip" [source: playtest.mjs and a 100-seed run of autoAction per class at a4ed861].
5. **Manual pacing at 1× will overshoot 45 minutes.** A manual round stays on screen about 2.3-2.7 s at 1× before the player can act (medians of three samples of 14-30 rounds; 1.65 s at 2×; travel to a node 3.4 s) [source: qa/browser/pace.mjs on a busy 2-core machine, so a little high]. The pacing model allows 2.5 s per round including the decision (PACE_MANUAL_ROUND_S, an assumption balance.md asks QA to measure). With about 380 rounds in a run that reaches the boss, the fights alone take roughly 8-10 minutes more than the model's 48-49 min estimate at 1× [inference: from the round count and the measured floor plus 1-2 s per decision]; at 2× the run lands near the model.

## Playtest notes
How I played: the QA player (qa/browser/policy.mjs) chooses as a careful player does from what the screen shows: trainer and vendor at each hub, potions on the item bar, a route scored with one row of look-ahead (rest when hurt, quests for the story, elites and dungeons only near their level and with potions), the standard talent build, upgrades by item score, interrupt and cleanse first on the boss bar. Combat is manual in zone 1 (the autoplay rules plus earlier potions and a flee from a lethal round) and autoplay's choice after. It played 40 seeds per class through the engine and 3 classes × 2 sizes through the browser, where I also read the screens.

Pacing
- A run that reaches the boss: about 48 nodes, 52 fights, 370-390 rounds, 127-131 in-game hours (at the par, no Ascendancy); the rules 3 model estimates 48-49 min manual and 37-38 min on autoplay. Each zone takes 19-22 hours.
- Real time at 1×: finding 5. At Instant speed the floating combat numbers pile on top of each other when several land in one frame.

Decisions that felt empty or forced
- Zone 1 combat with two spells (finding 2).
- Travel on is always the end of row 8 (finding 4a).
- Quest rewards: 9 % of the two-item choices hold no upgrade (175 of 1 926); otherwise the upgrade arrow makes the choice obvious.
- The trainer is "Learn, then Train all" every time: gold paid for everything at 272 of 278 hub and camp trainer visits, so "spells versus gold" (rules 1.8) is not a trade-off yet.
- Grind waves: once healthy, 2 or 3 waves is always right; 1 wave is only for a hurt character.

Difficulty spikes
- Zone 1 quests with 2-3 enemies above the player (finding 3); for the Mage also zone 2 quests and grinds: 0 of 40 Mage runs reached the boss, 26 died in zone 1-2 quests. The Mage's early fragility is known from the engine hand-off; the missing Arcane Ward (finding 2) is a large part of it.
- Dungeons and elites in zones 1-3 (finding 4b).
- The boss: almost every attempt ends in phase 2 (Shards and Linked); the Hunter mostly runs into Final Dark at round 50 (15 of 25 boss deaths), so phase 3 is almost never seen. Known in the engine hand-off; balance is M2.

Confusing UI
- Autoplay (BUG-QA-01); enemy counts (BUG-QA-02); the camp's "Face Oruvath" button below the fold of its panel (BUG-QA-10); potions that never reach the item bar unless the player opens the bags (BUG-QA-04); "You buy item." (BUG-QA-03); non-combat map labels in red (BUG-QA-08); the victory banner over the summary (BUG-QA-05).
- The first hub says "Visit the trainer, then set out", but at level 1 the trainer has nothing to teach; the visit costs an hour for nothing.
- The Autoplay toggle, class cards, hub service cards, reward cards and shrine boons are not buttons (no role, no keyboard focus). Fine for a mouse-first game; worth knowing for keyboard play.

Moments that felt good
- The zone map: all 8 rows at once with levels coloured against mine; planning a path to a rest before a hard row works.
- Intents: nameplates and the target frame show what each enemy will do and how hard; an interrupt shows "Interrupted!" on the enemy.
- The boss frame: phase, Brand counter, "Litany in 4", "Crush next round!", round and Final Dark countdown. It reads like a raid fight.
- Level-up banner and the story reward (three rare items with upgrade arrows).

Against the brief
- Classic MMO levelling feel: present (hub, zones by band, quest givers, dungeons, rarity colours, talents, unit frames, action bar, XP bar, minimap, bags, quest log).
- "Fights are manual by default; the player can turn on autoplay": manual yes; autoplay broken.
- About 45 minutes: the model says 48-49 min for runs that reach the boss; at 1× it will be longer (finding 5). The speedrun needs autoplay and an early Travel on, and neither is available in practice today.
- "Every choice spends time and has a long-term effect": true for rest versus quest and for the route to the story; weak for Travel on, trainers, and elites and dungeons (findings 2 and 4).

## How to run
```

node tools/build.mjs --out /tmp/qa-game.html
node qa/browser/ui-checks.mjs                                    # about 1 min
node qa/browser/full-run.mjs --seeds rogue:1,mage:117,hunter:1    # all three reach the boss; 30-60 min per size on 2 busy cores
node qa/browser/playtest.mjs --seeds 1-40                         # a few seconds
node qa/browser/pace.mjs --speed 1                                # about 2 min
```
Playwright comes from /home/claude/tools-ui (the scripts fall back to a local install). Once BUG-QA-01 is fixed, full-run.mjs plays the later zones on the toggle and waits for each fight; until then it records the problem and presses autoplay's choice each round, so the rest of the run is still checked.
