## What changed
- `sim/legacy-patch.py`: the designer's scratch patch of legacy.md 6.1 (applied unchanged to 5c8dfef) plus environment fields `learn` (per-spell learn levels), `bdmg` (Oruvath's damage by hero level) and `pdmg` (a class's damage in Oruvath's fight; sensitivity arm). Without them it plays arm T2 as before.
- `sim/legacy-run.mjs`: `build` (scratch copy + patch), `warm` (band risk table: chained cells over the band's hero levels, two rounds, from RISK_PRIOR), `grid` (one planner cell per hero level, each with its band's table).
- `sim/legacy-boss.mjs`: Oruvath's fight alone per class and hero or power level (diagnosis bench).
- `sim/legacyreport.mjs`: band and per-level tables, B6 per band; `--tables` summarises the band tables.
- `sim/tables/legacy/risk-H01-05.json`, `risk-H06-10.json`, `risk-H11-15.json`, `risk-H16-20.json`, `risk-H21-25.json`: the warmed band tables (300 warm-up runs per class each; read with `--risk-in`).
- docs/ARCHITECTURE.md: rows for the four sim files. docs/specs/legacy.md: two notes marked "(sim, 2026-11-17)" in 5.8 item 2 and 6.6 reading 3 (measured numbers only; no rule changed).
