## What the spec says
- **Guided first run** (2): the first run of a profile is guided in zone 1. Zone 1 uses the fixed seed TUTORIAL_SEED, with an overlay in `src/content/tutorial.js`: a fixed zone-1 Cull on the board, two site fights, scripts S1-S4 on the first four other fights, and Gorrak on the nearest Elite node. From zone 2 the run is normal and hard. There are 13 beats (G1-G13): contracts, moving, first fight, loot, the Furnace, intents, engage round, wind-up, Deathblow with its class answer, contract sites, potions, the Dimming, the Homeward Bell. Each beat has a trigger, one line, an anchor, a learned-when check from existing events and views, and a fallback. The player can skip the guide at class select or from any guide line. A death in the guided zone is a rescue: back to the last node, full health, 2 tolls.
- **First-time hints** (3): one coach line at a time, one line each, once per profile (per class for four of them). Settings gets "Hints On/Off", "Reset hints" and "Play the guide again", and every line has a "Hints off" link. There are 37 hints, H01-H37. The existing engage hint panel and the Furnace note fold into H01 and H16, with a migration of their localStorage keys.
- **First-run nudge** (4): the first non-dev summary pays a one-time welcome of 60 Echoes (the Packed Satchel's price). The "Spend Echoes" button glows, the Hall highlights the Satchel with one line, and "Later" closes the nudge. Nothing is blocked. If meta v2 is approved later, its ECHO_FIRST_RUN uses the same grant (4.4).
- Engine needs E1-E9 (section 5), UI needs U1-U8 (section 6), QA automated checks and the new-player playtest checklist (section 7), targets ONB1-ONB5 (section 8), constants (section 12).
