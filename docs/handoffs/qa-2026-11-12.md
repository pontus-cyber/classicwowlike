with the start camp), 13 special fights: 9 answered, 4 landed. Checked in play: the telegraph (line, chip, target row,
ruler, panel alert), the answers and their keys, the turn prompt, "No answer ready" in red, the autoplay hint (Hunter Lay
Trap, Mage Binding Glyph, Rogue "Sap, then wait a round", Disrupt on a dungeon boss), the Rogue's wait (Sap, Pass ringed,
"you stay hidden", Deathblow answered, still in STEALTH, Shadowstep Strike into the window), MAIMED (badge, potion "+N" =
half, tooltip), "Deathblow +100 % dmg", the map tooltips of named elites, dungeons, a hidden rows-map rare ("An elite ·
Deathblow: a cast") and hidden expedition elites ("Deathblow: a heavy blow"). All behaved as the specs say.
