## 4. Interpretations and open items (draft: please confirm or change)
1. **Exact value, one token per value and scale.** Near-values stay apart (1.15rem and 1.18rem are `--fs-body-1` and
   `-2`; 52 sizes where a designed ramp would have about 8). Merging them is the art slice's decision, as with the
   colours. "Body text one step up" is now a change to the six `--fs-body-*` tokens.
2. **Band names are by size, not by component.** A 1.3rem title in a small widget is `--fs-body-6`. Where a band and
   a role disagree, the art slice can give that rule its own token.
3. **Z-index.** Each of the 14 global layers has a token, `--z-scene` (0) included, though no rule sets it today.
   Values used inside one component's own stacking context are `--z-local-N` (0 to 6 and 25, the map's leave
   warning inside its window). `#engage` and `#heat .ht-row` share the value 6 under two names: the first is a
   layer, the second is local.
4. **Spacing.** Only lengths used at least twice are tokens; one-offs (`8.4rem`, `3.4rem`, ...) and negative offsets
   stay literal (24 uses). Rule U5's ratchet can start from those counts.
5. **Not done here.** The canvas fonts in scene.js are named inline (the review's "canvas fonts from theme.js"). Inline
   font sizes in screens.js and other JS are also untouched; the task limited JS to durations. Both are small next steps.
