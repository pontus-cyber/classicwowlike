## 5. Known issues

- The node tooltip's count follows the Dimming's schedule. In the guided zone a held corridor node is not counted; its release is the guide's doing and can add places the preview did not name. A guided rescue moves the Dimming back. Neither happens outside the guided first zone.
- A Grind whose waves are chosen inside gets no places line (its preview covers one wave).
- Under the Autoplay switch the director does not play zone travel (pre-existing: the step's action is the engine's), so the arrival log line, like "You arrive in ...", only appears when the Road or the Bell is taken by hand. The hub-foot line shows either way.
- When the Dimming sets the state, the tag and the line both name the Dimming ("The Dimming is near" and "◆ Set by the Dimming here"). The owner may prefer to drop one. The line is D152's wording and is kept as it is.
