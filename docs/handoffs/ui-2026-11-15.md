## 6. Known issues and open questions
- U01's tolls include the Road's own toll ("4 tolls" for 2 hops); the Road's sign tooltip still names the Road's toll alone.
  If the owner reads M as the walk only, it is one edit in `nearestRoad`.
- On a run with the guide off U01 shows at the first map (the Roads are open from the start there), next to the other first-map
  hints; it waits its turn (one hint a step).
- U02 waits behind any beat line or hint already up, so "the first time a red label appears" can be a step or two later.
- The "+N items" note counts items, not stacks: a potion stacking onto one in the bags counts as one.
- Draft wording: "The Dimming is near", the diamond's line, "Nearest Road: N hops, M tolls.", "Nearest: ...", "Pass through a
  place for 1 toll [X].", "+N items", the Key row "Dims in 3 tolls".
