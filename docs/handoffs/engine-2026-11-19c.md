  ranks above META_HERO's count as its top rank, retired entries (Keen Lineage, ...) have no effect in a Legacy run under the
  switch. With the switch off nothing changes. The Lead may want a refund rule before the owner's profile plays v2.
- The level-20 wing is priced, not sold (phases 4-5 of 7.1).
- 4.7 E6.1's example "280 XP" is illustrative: at no mods a Delve pays 252, a Cull 336, a Bounty 420.
- The top-end drop of 4.7 Open 1 is not addressed (designer's lever BOSS_HP_LEVEL); the H20 / H25 win rates above are the
  cells' numbers, not tuned.
- In a rows-map Legacy run (not a Legacy target) zone bands are not fixed at arrival, so xpRef follows the hero's level.
