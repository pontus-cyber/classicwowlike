# QA hand-off 2026-11-19b: why every full-run --legacy ends "death level 8 at zone 6"

Role: Playtest & QA Lead (with the Sim engineer's tools). Build: `node tools/build.mjs --out /tmp/qa-b/game.html` from
8aabfd4 plus the commits below. Root game.html not touched. Sources: docs/handoffs/ui-2026-11-19b.md, D154-D160,
docs/specs/legacy.md 1.2, 2.1, 4.7, 5.8.

## 1. The cause (no balance wall)
Reproduced without the browser: a Node copy of full-run's loop (same `newRun`, policy.mjs, autoplay after zone 1, seed 7)
gives the browser's result for all three classes in 0.3 s a run (death, summary level 8, zone 6).

1. **"level 8" is the power level, not the hero level.** `summary.level` is `player.level`, the engine's 1-60 power
   level (legacy.md 1.2). Power 8 is **hero level 4**. A run without Legacy levels 1 → 49-51 inside the run; a Legacy run
   moves the hero about three levels a run by design (the first run ends at hero 4-5, 4.7 E4). The two numbers are not
   comparable. **Report quirk: fixed** (full-run now prints "hero level 1 → 4 (power 8)").
2. **Every class plays a fresh hero 1.** The Rogue's profile is new; the Mage's and Hunter's `later` profile is
   `newProfile()` with runs 2 and no hero. So every run is a first run of a level-1 hero: zones are set from power 1-8
   (bands on seed 7: 1, 1, 3, 4, 8, 11) and zone 6 sits at power 11 with x 1.50 damage (2.1-2.2). This is intended, not a
   bot bug.
3. **Seed 7, not the classes.** XP and map choices do not depend on the class, so on seed 7 all three classes walk the same
   path, arrive at zone 6 as hero 4 (power 8), clear its first Grind (power 12) and die on its first ring-3 Quest:
   Rogue and Hunter against 2 Pyre Magus + Magma Hound at **power 15** (7 over the hero, about 3 hero levels), the Mage
   against 2 Forge Priest + Pyre Magus at power 13. Rogue and Hunter carry no healing draught by then (gear item level
   8-9). Autoplay's Legacy map policy takes that quest.
4. **Across seeds it is not a wall** (Node, the same bot, Economy v2 on, `later` profile, seeds 1-40 per class):

| Class | Reach Oruvath | Die in zone 6 | ... of which its first fight | Zones 1-5 | End hero level (power) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rogue | 22 / 40 | 3 | 1 | 15 | 3.60 (7.2) |
| Mage | 15 / 40 | 10 | 4 | 15 | 3.73 (7.6) |
| Hunter | 26 / 40 | 2 | 0 | 12 | 3.85 (7.9) |

   The sim planner at hero 1 (`sim/run.mjs --bot planner --class all --runs 60 --seed-start 9000 --profile later --map
   expedition --prep --hero 1 --oruvath --econ --risk-in sim/tables/legacy-hx/risk-H01-05.json`): Oruvath reached 68 / 72 / 82 %
   (Rogue / Mage / Hunter), zone 6 deaths 2 / 0 / 0 of 60, end power 6.9 / 7.4 / 7.5, wins 1.7 / 11.7 / 0 %. The QA bot
   (engine autoplay in fights, no risk table) dies in zone 6 more often than the planner, the Mage most (25 %).

## 2. A real bot bug found on the way: the Camp grind
policy.mjs `campAction` kept the level-60 rule (Camp grind while `level < MAX_LEVEL`) in Legacy runs; autoplay does not
(autoplay.js `campPolicy`, legacy.md 5.8 item 4). Under Economy v2 a Camp wave pays the same XP at every hero level (4.7
E2), so a run that reached the Camp ground on: 600-670 waves, about 1 200 pace minutes, **hero 1 → 25 in one run**
(seeds 1-10, 30 runs: 16 reached the Camp, arriving at hero 4.7 and leaving at 23.8 on average, 601 waves; none won Oruvath). Seed 7 never reached the Camp, so the browser runs did not show it.
**Fixed:** in a Legacy run the bot goes on to Oruvath (inn first if hurt). Checked in the browser on hunter seed 1: hero
1 → 5 at the Camp, Oruvath fought, PASS.

Numbers for the Lead (no tuning done): XP a Camp wave, the QA bot (2 waves a step, Camp level fixed at arrival):

| | Hero 5-6 | Hero 10 | Hero 15 | Hero 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Economy v2 on | 96 | 96 | 96 | 96 |
| Economy v2 off | 113-161 | 5 | 2 | 0-2 |

With v2 a hero can still level 5 → 25 by Camp grind alone at about 48 XP a pace minute (runs pay 145-211, legacy.md 1.4),
on waves set at the arrival level (power 9-16) that the hero outgrows. It is slow, but it has no end. If that is not
wanted, the lever is the Camp's reference level in `xpRef` (or a grind cap); a design question, left open.

## 3. Also fixed
- **Chest after the waiting loot** (full-run, seen on hunter seed 1): the guardian's loot with full bags waits in the loot
  window; the bot clicked "Open the chest" first and timed out. It now clears the loot (the twin's `s.loot`) first.
- **Second camp line once per profile** (ui-2026-11-19b open item 4): START_RUN decides it (bridge `local.secondCamp`) and
  marks the profile `secondCampSeen: true` (stored at once and in the run's copy, so zone checkpoints and END_RUN keep it).
  A reload in that camp no longer shows it. Still only for a profile with `runs` 1 (the half of item 4 about a non-lean
  first run is unchanged). Hints H22 / H23 and all wording not touched.
- **reloadKeep** (ui-regress): the first part Y run failed Y6a "after a reload: no note, no second refund" (the note came
  back; the balance was right). A reload had kept localStorage but lost sessionStorage, so openY's init script seeded its
  old profile again. The sentinel is now in both stores; the next S,Y run caught one such drop and recovered.
