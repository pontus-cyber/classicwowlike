## Spec questions (reading implemented; easy to change)
1. **STEALTH and openers (7.4)**: "removed by any non-opener action" read literally: an opener keeps STEALTH, so a Rogue at full energy can open with up to 3 Shadowstep Strikes. PASS, FLEE and items are non-opener actions and remove STEALTH before enemies act, so the +0.50 miss bonus only helps after an opener or a mid-fight STEALTH. Flip `ENGINE_ASSUMPTION.OPENER_BREAKS_STEALTH` to make openers break it.
2. **Elite abilities (2.3 "1-2" vs 12.2 "1")**: 12.2 followed (exactly 1), per the Lead.
3. **Elite and dungeon gold (2.0 vs 8.5)**: elite/rare kill pays ELITE_GOLD_V × V(level) instead of the mob coin; a dungeon boss kill pays the mob coin and the clear adds DUNGEON_GOLD_V × V(boss level).
4. **Boss actions per phase (10.2)**: Umbral Strike in every phase when not channelling (phases 2-3 do not list it); Gravity Crush in phases 1-2 only ("Litany and Brand continue" in phase 3), also during a channel. Interrupting Litany does not give the boss a Strike that round.
5. **Crit cap (4.2)**: CRIT_CAP caps the always-on crit; situational bonuses (Pyre Comet vs BURN, Heartseeker, Searing Focus, Shatter, Conflagrant Core) add on top, else Heartseeker and Conflagrant Core would do nothing.
6. **Template ability intents (12.4 has no weights)**: weights heavy_blow 30, dark_cast 25, cleave_all 30, rend 40, curse 15, self_heal 50, self_shield 20 (mirroring the archetype actions); self_heal only below 70 % HP; self_shield only without a shield (lasts until broken or fight end); enrage is passive; summon is a forced, telegraphed intent at <= 50 % HP; summoned mobs are zone normals at the node CL (dungeon: trash level).
7. **Talent spells have no autoplay condition (6)**: Thousand Cuts CP <= 3, Rupture Venom CP >= 4, Conflagrant Core and Wild Communion vs elite/boss, Glacier Spike and Starfall always, Hourglass at mana < 30 %, Deadfall at >= 2 enemies or a cast.
8. **Bar template (balance.md 1.2)** puts the main builder before the finisher; with 7.9 rule 5 the "always" builder then blocks every finisher. The default autopilot orders conditional spells before unconditional ones (`AUTO_BAR_ORDER`). The sim's template should do the same.
9. **Final Dark** cannot miss (else a 5 % miss survives the enrage); it ignores WARD and IMMUNE as specified.
10. **Brand** ticks BRAND_DMG × stacks × (1 + asc) × level mod at each round end; the new stack is added after that round's tick.
11. **Shards**: armor of a level-60 normal enemy (900); their attack is named "Dimming Shard Strike" (rules name none).
12. **Glacial Tomb**: the skipped turn is a forced PASS (only legal action) so the UI can show that round.
13. **Hit-dependent effects**: combo points only on a landed hit; finishers spend CP even on a miss; statuses from damaging spells (CHILLED, FROZEN, interrupts, SILENCED, POISON) need a hit; pure status spells (Temple Blow, Hexbind, Snare Trap, Expose, Mark, DOT spells) always land.
14. **Death's Sidestep** triggers on any damage event, DOT ticks included.
15. **Mirrorshade**: one extra draw before each enemy attack on the player (hits, Litany, Pulse; not DOT ticks or Final Dark); an image skips the 3 damage draws.
16. **Node-counted buffs**: map nodes, each camp wave and the boss fight count as nodes; a boon or Elixir covers the next 3 nodes entered, not the current one.
17. **Treasure trap**: the first draw of loot(z,r,l), so Scout's Eye can show it on the map.
18. **Stream keys**: hub vendor node(z,0,0); camp = zone 7, its vendor node(7,0,0); camp wave w: node(7,w,0), fight(7,w,0,0), loot(7,w,0).
19. **Unique items**: deterministic stats (class primary, STA second, content affix); each unique drops at most once per run; Starforged weapon: epic ilvl 62 at the camp vendor.
20. Smaller readings: consumable sell value = round(price V × V(L) / BUY_MULT); Trainer's Ledger only at hub and camp trainers; Prospector on gold found, not on sales; soft enrage also in dungeon boss fights; Hourglass resets spell cooldowns, not the potion cooldown; event XP at CL 60 uses the curve value (83 000) although `xpToNext(60)` is 0; Spark Flask is generic magic (no fire/frost talents); boss phases for Echoes count 2 and 3 (enrage is not a phase); junk names come from a new content field `zones[].junkNames` (12.1 has none); `SET_SEED(random)` is resolved by the front end.

## Known issues and notes
- **Default autopilot is weak by design**: outside combat it is a simple deterministic policy. Observations for the Designer and Sim Engineer (starting numbers, unsimulated): 3-mob dungeon trash at +1 level and elites at +2 are often lethal before gear; the Mage rarely gets past Z2 (130 HP, mana); at the boss, low-burst builds mostly reach Final Dark in phase 2 because Linked (-60 %) lasts while new Shards keep coming.
- `node --test` on a directory needs qa/engine/index.js on Node 22 (it does not expand directories).
- There is no package.json; Node 22 detects ES module syntax in `.js` files. A root package.json with `"type": "module"` (Lead's call) would skip the detection pass.
- Events are copies (safe to keep); the state must be treated as read-only by callers of `step`.
- The UI currently runs on its mock engine (src/ui/mock-engine.js) with its own view shape; the adapter to src/rules.js is the UI Engineer's. Helpers added for it: `spellInfo`, `fleeChance`, `mapView`, `talentPointsFree`, `xpToNext`, `itemScore`, `whyIllegal`.
- qa/ is QA's area; engine tests live in qa/engine/ as the task asked.
- All numbers are spec starting values; `ENGINE_ASSUMPTION` in src/constants.js lists the engine's own numbers (autopilot thresholds, template weights) for tuning.
