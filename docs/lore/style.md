# Style: tone and naming rules

Status: draft (not yet approved by the owner)

Rules for anyone writing names or text for the game: the lore chat, the Content Designer and the Lead.

## 1. Tone
- Grounded and weary, with hope underneath. People along the road are tired, practical and stubborn. They ask for help plainly and pay what they can.
- Dark at the edges. The Dimming, the restless dead and the cults are serious threats. Horror is shown through small concrete details (a latch a wolf has learned to open), not gore.
- Humour is dry and rare, and comes from characters. No jokes that wink at the player, at other games or at the real world.
- No modern words or ideas (no "okay", "team", "boss" in in-world text, no clocks or engines). "Hours" are fine.

## 2. Text formats
Matches the existing content [source: src/content/enemies.js, src/content/zones.js].
| Text | Length | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| Enemy description | 1-2 sentences, under 25 words | Third person, present tense |
| Zone theme, hub text, dungeon text | 1 sentence | Present tense, no "you" |
| Shrine and treasure lines | 1 sentence | Second person allowed ("when you nudge it") |
| Quest text | Up to 3 short sentences | Spoken by the quest giver, first person |
| Item flavour | 1 sentence, under 15 words | Any |

## 3. Fixed terms
Always written exactly like this: the Unlit Star, Oruvath, the Dimming, the Shardfall, Shardspawn, Dimming Shards, Homeward Bell, Echoes, the Roads, the Sunken Observatory, Observatory Steps. Proposed (world.md): Calvenmark, Veth, the Sunscribes, the Bellwrights, Wayfarer.

## 4. Naming by zone
Each zone has its own sound so a name tells the player where it comes from.
| Zone | Sound | Existing examples [source: src/content/zones.js, enemies.js] |
|---|---|---|
| Amberfold Downs | Plain English farm words, compounds of field, mill, hedge, stone | Millbrook Rest, Barleyreach Verge, Mother Thistlewick |
| Greywater Fens | Wet, soft words: reed, mire, gloam, stilt; short gruff personal names | Stilt Haven, Sisel Mistweaver, Bargh |
| Highcairn Reach | Hard moorland words: cairn, barrow, heath; clan names with a by-name | Windbarrow Hold, Mawra Ironbrow |
| Sunscoured Wastes | Short names of two or three syllables with hard consonants and doubled letters | Veth, Kharun, Nesret, Ammeset, Tazem |
| Mournwood | Church and grave words: chapel, lantern, penitent; titled names | Lanternfall, Father Vael, Lord Varis Dunmere |
| Cindercrown Peaks | Forge and fire words; harsh names with K, R, TH | Emberwatch, Skarn, Kaelith, Karth |

## 5. Originality check (every new name)
1. No names of characters, places, gods or creatures from games, books, films, myths or religions. Real first names are allowed only for minor folk, never for named elites or bosses.
2. Search the exact name on the web before using it. If it is a known character, place or brand, drop it.
3. Avoid terms coined by a single famous author, even if they sound generic.
4. Genre words (wolf, ogre, wraith, cultist, crypt) are fine.
5. Record any name that failed the check in docs/feedback/ so the Lead can replace it in content.
