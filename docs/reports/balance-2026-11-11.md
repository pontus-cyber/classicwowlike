## Summary
1. **The planner now prices heat** (expedition.md H.6). Its value function pays the HEAT_GAIN multiplier on XP and gold and the HEAT_RARITY_UP step on drop-table items of the state a node is entered in (the first node of a choice from `R.heatPreview(...).pays`), and charges a boss term: the node's change of projected Ascendancy × SIM.VAL_ASC. Hours in hand cost no Ascendancy; hours past the line do. The greedy and the speedrunner are unchanged.
2. **Ascendancy is expensive.** On the same runs, removing Ascendancy lifts the planner P0 from 34.2 % to 54.5 % (+20.3 pp). A run reaching the boss loses 15 pp of boss win at +4 % and 32 pp at +8 %, about 3.9 of win probability per 1.0 Ascendancy [measured]. SIM.VAL_ASC = VAL_DEATH 2 000 × 3.9, rounded to 8 000: 32 value units per hour past the line, against 12 per hour of today's hour weight.
3. **Hour weight: 3 is best.** With heat valued, planner P0 on 300 seeds per class: weight 1 38.3 %, 2 40.6 %, 3 49.1 % (+8.6 pp over 2, 4.5 - 12.6), 4 47.6 % (+7.0 pp). At 3 and 4 the planner reaches the boss under par (117 / 115 h, Ascendancy 0) and never enters Hot. The planner before today (no heat terms, weight 2) wins 33.7 % on the same seeds.
4. **B58 fails with avoid ahead**: heat-seek 36.6 %, heat-avoid 46.2 % (−9.6 pp, −11.7 to −7.6; 11-10e approximation −12.7 pp). **B56 fails low**: 6.8 % of the planner's wins had a Hot node (11-10e: 76 %). Once the planner prices the Ascendancy a Hot node costs, it stays out of Hot. **B57 passes**: +0.5 pp.
5. **The heat rewards cannot close B58 on their own; a cheaper Ascendancy step does.** Following H.6's order (HEAT_RARITY_UP first, then HEAT_GAIN), on 300 seeds per class: rarity step 0.50 / 0.70 −10.0 pp, 1.0 / 1.0 −9.0 pp, 1.0 / 1.0 with HEAT_GAIN 0.70 / 1.0 −6.4 pp (today's constants −10.3 pp on the same seeds). Entering Hot costs one Ascendancy step, about 15 pp of boss win. With ASC_PER_STEP 0.02 and today's rewards B58 passes (−1.9 pp, −5.9 to 2.2), B56 is 40 % and the planner gains 2.6 pp. A seeker that discounts the boss term at today's constants runs Hot in 74 % of runs and wins less (33.1 %).

## Findings
1. **The boss term changes how the planner plays more than the rewards do.** With heat valued at weight 2 the planner reaches the boss at 131 h (141 h before) with Ascendancy 0.04 (0.08), and wins 6.9 pp more on the same seeds (40.6 against 33.7 %, 300 per class) [measured: sweep hm2 vs noHeat]. Its nodes are 82 % Clear, 18 % Warm, 0 % Hot: it spends the hours in hand and stops near the line.
2. **The heat rewards are small next to one Ascendancy step.** The rewards only pay in the nodes a run plays Hot, and a Hot run has paid an Ascendancy step to get there. B57 (+0.5 pp) shows the rewards add almost no power to the planner because it seldom collects them; the rarity step at 1.0 moves heat-seek by +1.4 pp and the planner by 0 pp.
3. **Heat-avoid is the planner with the better hour weight.** heat-avoid (weight 4, no heat rewards valued) wins 46.2 %, the same range as the planner at weight 3 or 4 with heat valued (49.1 / 47.6 % on 300 seeds). heat-seek (weight 0.5) spends its in-hand hours early, crosses the line more often (25 % of its nodes Warm, 1 % Hot, 19 % of runs with a Hot node) and meets a +4 % boss at 133 h. So B58 still measures the hour weight: the seek side has the worse weight and nothing on the reward side pays for it.
4. **Forcing heat makes it worse.** heat-seek with its boss term scaled like its hour weight (VAL_ASC × 0.5 / 2, SIM_OVERRIDE, diagnostic) runs Hot in 74 % of runs, reaches the boss at 143 h with +8 %, and wins 33.1 % on 300 seeds per class (honest seek 37.2 %, avoid 47.6 % on the same seeds) [measured: sim/out/h11/m/diag].
5. **B1 consequence.** The planner P0 band is 20 - 35 % (WR_P0_PLANNER). The heat-aware planner wins 38.5 % at weight 2 (1 000 per class) and 49.1 % at weight 3 (300 per class). The bot plays the time trade-off better, so the planner band and the boss's strength need a decision before the weight changes.

## Proposals
1. **PLANNER_HOUR_MULT 2 -> 3** (balance.md 6, a bot constant), together with the boss term: +8.6 pp planner P0 on paired seeds, the best of 1-4. Re-measure B1, B6 and B21 with it, since every planner-based bot (planner, tactician, opener, masher) inherits the weight. B1's planner band (35 % max) fails at weight 2 already (38.5 %).
2. **B58: lower the price of heat, not the rewards (ASC_PER_STEP 0.04 -> 0.02, for the Lead Game Designer).** Measured on 300 seeds per class with today's heat rewards: heat-seek 45.8 % against heat-avoid 47.7 % (−1.9 pp, inside ±5), the planner 43.2 % (+2.6 pp over today on the same seeds), 32 % of planner runs and 40 % of its wins with a Hot node (B56 inside 30-85 %), the planner at 134 h with +2 %. The reward levers of H.6 do not get there: doubling the rarity step closes 0.3 pp, a certain step 1.3 pp, a certain step with HEAT_GAIN 0.70 / 1.0 3.9 pp; B57 stays within 0.5 pp in those rows because the planner rarely goes Hot. Costs of the change: every over-par run meets a weaker boss (the owner's 148 h run: +4 % instead of +8 %), so B1 for the greedy and the speedrunner moves up; a 1 000-per-class measure and B1 / B6 / B21 are needed before adopting it. ASC_PER_STEP is a rules constant (rules.md 13), outside the heat block.
   - Alternatives not measured: redefine the B58 policies around the planner's own weight (seek = values heat rewards; avoid = never enters a node past the line), so B58 measures heat rather than the 0.5 / 4 hour weights [assumption]; or judge heat in expedition runs, where the Dimming's edge pays Hot without hours past the line (H.5; B43 is the check there) [assumption].
3. **B56 band**: with honest pricing the planner's wins rarely touch Hot (6.8 %; 17 % with the strongest reward variant, 40 % with ASC_PER_STEP 0.02). The band held in 11-10e only because the planner did not price Ascendancy; keep it and judge it with proposal 2.

## Method notes
- Value function (sim/lib/model.mjs): `state` = R.heatPreview `pays` for the first node of a choice and state.node.heat inside a Grind; later nodes of a path take the run state of `heat0 + model hours` (rows runs: the line is fixed within a zone). XP and gold × (1 + HEAT_GAIN[state]) after rested and meta, as the engine; drop-table gains with the rarity step (each source rarity steps up with HEAT_RARITY_UP, epic stays, ilvl of the final rarity); vendor stock and the trainer get no step. qa/sim/heat.test.mjs checks the model's state and projected Ascendancy against heatPreview / heatView in all four states.
- Boss term: `(ascProjected(heat after) - ascProjected(heat before)) × VAL_ASC`, ascProjected = ascIfPar without the floor (SIM.HEAT_ASC_FLOOR 0): ASC_PER_STEP per ASC_STEP_HOURS past the line, capped [assumption: the expected step count when later zones' hours are not known; the stepped form makes path values jump at the step boundaries]. The term is charged on top of the hour weight (balance.md 1.1); heat-avoid and heat-seek pay it in full.
- Policies (sim/bots/planner.mjs): default = heat rewards valued, boss term, PLANNER_HOUR_MULT; heat-seek = the same at HEAT_SEEK_HOUR_MULT 0.5; heat-avoid = no heat reward valued, boss term, HEAT_AVOID_HOUR_MULT 4. Diagnostics: `noHeat` (the planner before today), `hm<x>` (hour weight x).
- Calibration: the planner before today does not read Ascendancy and the risk table has no boss fights, so the base and ASC_CAP 0 cells are identical up to FIGHT_BOSS (3 000 of 3 000 runs); the per-1.0 loss is the sum of paired win differences over the sum of Ascendancy, runs reaching the boss. VAL_DEATH stands for a lost run [assumption: the value function's exchange rate].
- B57's arm is a scratch engine (cf53a9b) with HEAT_GAIN, HEAT_RARITY_UP and ECHO_PER_HEAT_FIGHT 0; the planner there values no reward (it reads the engine's constants) and still pays the boss term.
- The proposals' cells hold the first 300 seeds per class; the first 300 seeds of a 1 000-run cell are the same runs (the risk table refreshes every 500 runs), so they pair with the measure.
