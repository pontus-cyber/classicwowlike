## Bar-template follow-up (changes-m2-v5.md Notes)
The rogue's boss bar put Severing Flourish ahead of Open Vein at high weapon damage because the template valued a finisher at 4 × its 1-point amount, weapon term included; the engine adds the weapon term once (rules 5.3: (4 + tl + 0.30P) × CP + 0.5WD). Fixed in sim/lib/policy.mjs (d0d29f7) without a spec change: balance.md 1.2 leaves the choice inside "finisher or second nuke" to the template's damage estimate, and the estimate now matches the engine. Open Vein now stays ahead up to weapon damage 416 + 0.8 × power (the Starforged epic was 191). Paired seeds on the M2 v4 engine: PALL rogues holding the class weapon won 56 of 92 boss fights before the fix, 88 of 92 after; every other run identical (P0 rogue: 2 of 600 runs changed, both with weapon damage 155-176). No spec change needed.
