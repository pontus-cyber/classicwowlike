## Spec readings (M2 v2 tactician and pass)
1. T1 damage: the est of this round's intents on the player ('player' or 'all' targets) from heavy actions, casts the player cannot stop this turn with an interrupt or stun-tagged spell, Collapse Litany 2/2, Gravity Crush and Starless Pulse; an active WARD is subtracted. Hits on the pet do not count.
2. T1 responses, first usable: (a) interrupt, then stun-tagged spell on the biggest stoppable source (a cast: interrupt or stun; a non-cast hit: stun on an enemy that is not CC-immune); (b) a defensive-tagged spell that is not active and covers one of the hits: Smoke Veil physical hits only, Feign Stillness (live pet) and Beastcall Horn single-target hits only, the others every hit; (c) a healing item on the item bar.
3. T2: with >= 2 Shards the usable aoe-tagged spell with the highest base damage (Ricochet Bolt aimed at the lowest-HP Shard); with 1 Shard, the player's expected damage of one action is the mean over TACTICIAN_SAMPLES cloned streams of the damage of each usable direct-damage action (and ATTACK) on that Shard; the best is used when the Shard's HP is below it.
4. Search score: HP removed and lost use the damage events' hpLost (WARD absorption is not HP lost); summoned adds (enemies from the fight's starting count on, and Shards) count TACTICIAN_ADD_WEIGHT. The search budget assumptions of the first pass stand (candidate pruning, search gate, sample racing).
5. The planners' travel level is the engine's R.travelLevel (exit level - TRAVEL_EARLY_LEVELS, Swift Boots one lower).
