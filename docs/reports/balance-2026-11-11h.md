## Row 4: why the specified speedrunner plays worse
balance.md 1's cost prices one level at VAL_XP / VAL_TIME = 16.7 tolls and a death at VAL_DEATH / VAL_TIME = 333 tolls. The v11 policy valued a toll at VAL_TIME × 9 (PLANNER_TIME_MULT × STYLE_SPEED_TIME_MULT) against VAL_XP + EXP_UNDER_LEVEL_VALUE per level, about 4.6 tolls per level [computed: params]. So the row-4 bot buys XP with 3.6 times more tolls: it stays in each zone longer for quest XP (17.1 against 15.6 tolls per zone) and meets the front more (Dimmed deaths 81 against 37). With no gear term it never enters a dungeon (0.00 per run; it still buys 2.3 Delves per run at the camp, speedPrep unchanged, and completes none) and reaches the Camp with ilvl 44.9, where 80 of 900 runs die in the Camp grind. On the rows map the same cost is harmless because every row costs one node and the zone cannot be left early; on the expedition the level price decides how long the bot stays. B12 cannot be read on 200 wins with this bot: at 3.4 % it needs about 5 900 runs [inference: 200 / 0.034].

## Readings and known issues
1. **Measured engine:** 199168c includes eb0ee5f (the Lead's note 2026-11-11). Planner P0 (19.3 %) and PALL (50.1 %) sit within 1 pp of their edges; the cells were already on the current engine, and a paired arm on src 2e74169 isolates the change: P0 fails either way (19.7 % without it); PALL and the row 5 gate pass only with it (49.4 % without).
2. **Planner search time after a Dimmed encounter:** planFog does not advance the search's zone time by an encounter's tolls, so it sees the front 1-2 tolls late. speedFog advances it (row 4: "the Dimming exact"); the planner was kept as in v11 so its cells stay comparable. A candidate sim fix for the next pass (it may move B43 and P0).
3. **The escape rule in the bots** follows the engine's autoplay: a step onto a resolved node, the hub or a node Dimmed at the step's start is a walk (MOVE), any other node is passed through (the bots' exitStep); qa/sim/v12.test.mjs checks every neighbour step's tolls against R.heatPreview in wandering god-mode runs.
4. B12's run-start reading counts the start camp's minute (as 11-11g); the SET_OUT reading uses `ct.ps`.
5. Cut for time: cell 6 at 100 per class, cell 9 at 300 per class, cell 12 (campaigns) not run. B31 / B32 keep 11-11g's readings.
6. P10 under the 8.2 order (spec item; not measured here, B2 is not a flip criterion): satchel, vigor 1, edge 1, quick_study 1, pockets 1, ledger, scout, cartographer, vigor 2, edge 2; sp_(class)1 is 11th [source: sim/lib/profiles.mjs standardOrder].
