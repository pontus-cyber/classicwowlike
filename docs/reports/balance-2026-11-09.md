# Balance report 2026-11-09: first simulator pass (M2 2.2)

From: Simulation & Balance Engineer. For: the Lead (tuning decisions next session). No constant, content value or spec was changed in this task; every proposal below is for the Lead to decide.

## Setup
- Engine, content (m1-content-1 with per-quest enemy subsets, 22faebe) and simulator: commit 38562b2 (sim/pass.mjs orchestration only changed after 29106f9; bots and engine unchanged).
- 2 000 runs per class per cell on seeds 1000-2999 for every bot on P0, P10 and PALL; except tactician PALL 300, B26-B28 variants 500 (paired with the planner's seeds 1000-1499), route styles 300, hunter pets 2 000, diagnostic tactician 300; 20 campaigns per class (balance.md asks 50; cut after two interruptions on a shared 2-core machine). 95 500 measured runs, 0 errors.
- Profiles: P0 fresh; P10 = first 10 entries of the standard order; PALL = full catalogue (flags reachedBoss, bossKill, firstKill set).
- Default trees from B7: rogue Venomcraft (2), mage Rimecraft (1), hunter Marksmanship (1); Hunter pet ridgewolf.
- Death-risk table: 1 000 planner warm-up runs on P0 (seeds 9 000 000+), 500 on P10 and 1 000 on PALL (both starting from the P0 table); every cell then refreshes its own table every 500 runs. Reading of the spec and simulator assumptions: docs/handoffs/sim-2026-11-09.md.
- Reproduce: `node sim/pass.mjs --out sim/out/pass-2026-11-09 --half-runs 300 --style-runs 300 --variant-runs 500 --campaigns 20` then `node sim/report.mjs --in sim/out/pass-2026-11-09 --out docs/reports/balance-2026-11-09.md`.
- Tuning experiments: planner, 300 runs per class after a 600-run warm-up, constants changed in a scratch copy only (not in the repo).

## Headline
- Only the rogue can win. Planner P0: rogue 39.1 %, mage 0.0 %, hunter 0.1 % (pooled 13.1 %, target 20-35 %). Planner PALL: rogue 96.7 %, hunter 50.3 %, mage 0.0 % although 90 % of PALL mages reach the boss.
- The end boss's phase 2 (Shards with Linked) is a wall for mage and hunter: their boss losses end with the boss at a median 65 % (mage) and 57 % (hunter) HP; the hunter mostly runs into Final Dark.
- Half of all P0 mage runs die in zone 1-2 (median level 7). QA's playtest shows the same: zone 1 is played with the two starting spells, and fatal quests have 2-3 mobs above the player (qa handoff, findings 2-3) [source: docs/handoffs/qa-2026-11-09.md].
- Nobody can leave a zone early: every bot plays all 48 nodes; the speed route is not a lever (QA finding 4a agrees). Speedrun wins take 33.9 min on autoplay (target <= 30).
- The tactician as specified does not beat autoplay (B5 -0.8 pp); its 3-round score loses the boss fight it reaches more often.
- Engine health is good: 1 002/1 002 replays identical, 0 stuck runs in 95 500, 11 900+ autoplay runs/min/core.
- 38 of 71 checks pass; P1: 22 pass, 22 fail.

## Five biggest misses and tuning proposals
Ranked by size and by how many other targets they drive (the generated ranking is below).

### 1. Mage cannot win (B6, B7, B1-B3, B32; QA findings 2-3 agree)
Mage: 0 wins in 27 000 runs on every profile and tree; half of P0 runs die in Z1-Z2; at the boss 96-98 % of losses end in phase 2, killed by Shard hits at round ~24.
| Constant | Current | Proposed | Measured effect (mage, P0, 300 runs, with proposal 2 applied) |
|---|---|---|---|
| CLASS.mage.hpPerLevel | 14 | 17.5 | |
| CLASS.mage.stats.STA growth | 1.0 | 1.25 | |
| MANA_REGEN_ROUND_PCT | 0.02 | 0.025 | |
| (together) | | | reach the boss 15 % -> 31 %, Z1-Z2 share of deaths 56 % -> 48 %, wins 0 % -> 0 % |

Within TUNE_LIMIT this halves the early-death problem but does not make the mage win: it still dies in phase 2 at 65 % boss HP. Proposal for the Designer (beyond TUNE_LIMIT): give the mage a phase-2 answer, e.g. Cinder Storm / Rime Burst damage against Shards, or a larger Arcane Ward, and let the zone 1 hub trainer teach the level 4-8 spells early (QA finding 2: Arcane Ward is missing through the mage's deadliest zone).

### 2. Boss phase 2 wall for hunter (and mage) (B6, B7, B9, B23, B21 boss share 54 %)
| Constant | Current | Proposed | Measured effect (planner P0, 300 runs per class) |
|---|---|---|---|
| SHARD_HP | 1300 | 1000 | rogue 35.3 % -> 47.0 %, hunter 0.0 % -> 4.0 %, mage 0 % -> 0 % |
| LINK_DR | 0.60 | 0.45 | (with SHARD_HP) |
| BOSS_ARMOR | 1200 | 900 | with both above: rogue 48.7 %, hunter 5.7 % |
LINK_DR 0.45 alone: rogue 40.7 %, hunter 0.3 %. These help the rogue far more than the hunter, so they widen the class gap unless paired with a rogue reduction (proposal 4). The hunter needs about twice its phase-2 damage on the boss [inference: from boss HP left at loss, 57 %, against the 40 % threshold]; a hunter-specific lever (pet damage PET_DMG_POWER 0.30 -> 0.375, Heartseeker/Keen Shot coefficients) needs its own measurement next pass.

### 3. Too many early deaths (B21 Z1-Z2 34 % of deaths vs <= 15 %; B22 quests 60 % of non-boss deaths; QA findings 2-3 agree)
Fatal early nodes are quests with 2-3 mobs 1-4 levels above the player, played with the two starting spells (QA: 28 of 28 fatal zone-1 quests had 2-3 enemies; simulator: planner P0 non-boss deaths 60 % quest, 21 % grind).
| Constant | Current | Proposed | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| QUEST_SIZE_W3 | 25 | 19 | fewer 3-mob quests [inference: 3-mob quests have 2-4 times the death rate of 1-mob quests in the risk table] |
| QUEST_SIZE_W1 | 25 | 31 | |
| W_TRAINER | 4 | 5 | more trainer nodes in zone 1, so level 4-8 spells arrive before zone 2 |
Not measured this session. Also proposed for the Designer: show the enemy count on the map (QA BUG-QA-02); the simulator's planner keys quest risk by size and is much safer for it.

### 4. Rogue trees and armor (B7 rogue: Bladework 12.7 %, Shadowcraft 29.4 %, Venomcraft 39.1 %; worst/best 0.32 vs >= 0.6)
Venomcraft's bleed and poison ignore armor; the boss has 1 200 armor (32 % mitigation for a level-60 attacker), and with Open Vein on the boss bar the rogue wins 54 % of boss fights, with Severing Flourish 6 % (greedy, which keeps its map bar). 
| Constant | Current | Proposed | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| BOSS_ARMOR | 1200 | 900 | raises direct-hit trees (Bladework, hunter) relative to DoT builds [inference; measured only pooled: rogue Venomcraft +1.7 pp on top of proposal 2] |
| TALENT.r_c2.pct (Deep Wounds) | 0.12 | 0.09 | trims Venomcraft DoTs [inference, not measured] |

### 5. Manual play does not matter in the simulator (B5: tactician - planner -0.8 pp vs 8-25 pp)
The tactician (balance.md 1) reaches the boss more often than the planner (P0 73 % vs 60 %) but wins fewer boss fights: its 3-round score counts Shard HP like boss HP and cannot see Brand stacks or the round-50 enrage. With autoplay in the end-boss fight only, it is +2.8 pp on the same seeds. This is a bot-spec issue more than a game issue; per balance.md 4 do not raise intents yet.
| Constant | Current | Proposed | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| TACTICIAN_DEPTH | 3 | 3 | unchanged |
| new TACTICIAN_ADD_WEIGHT (weight of summoned enemies' HP in the score) | 1 (implicit) | 0.3 | stops spending cooldowns on Shards [inference] |
| TACTICIAN_HP_WEIGHT | 1.5 | 1.5 | measured 0.5 and 3 in the boss fight: both worse than autoplay |

### Also measured
- Dungeon boss length (B16 14 rounds vs 8-13): DUNGEON_BOSS_HP_MULT 7 -> 5.6 with ELITE_HP_MULT 3.5 -> 2.8 gave dungeon boss 10-13 rounds, elite 10, and no change in win rates (rogue 35.3 % -> 38.3 %).
- Speed route (B12, B14): zones give about 10 levels in 8 rows, so no route reaches the exit level early. Next pass: QUEST_TURNIN_XP_FRAC 0.80 -> 1.0 with a check that B20 (level vs zone) still passes.
- Ember (B34 5 % vs 30-70 %): an Ember costs 30 × V and is in stock at 15 % of vendors; P3, left for later.
