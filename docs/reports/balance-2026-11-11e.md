## Summary
1. **The bots play the start camp and the hub contract.** Planner: contracts by estimated completion × reward value per point, Tracker's Notes when its completion gain pays per point, the Second Clapper at BELL_KEEP_VALUE, all only above SIM.PREP_POINT_VALUE per point; hub contract from Reserve or by a swap. Greedy: contracts by value per point, then Field Draughts; hub contract from Reserve. Speedrunner: Delves, Second Clapper, Field Draughts; Delve hub contracts. Random: random purchases. The fog bots value sites (10.3) and walk to known sites. Rows and expedition-without-prep runs are hash-identical to the simulator before (48 runs).
2. **Contract completion is under its bands:** planner P0 42.5 % (45-65), planner PALL 58.1 % (65-85), greedy P0 6.2 %. Culls complete (55 % / 69 %), **Delves do not (14 % / 23 %)**: the planner rarely enters a dungeon (6 tolls) under its time weight and the Dimming; without the Dimming Delves complete 42 %.
3. **The plan matters weakly:** planned prep beats "none" by +4.3 pp of planner P0 wins (-0.3 to 9.0; target >= 3 pp, pass on the point) but loses to random prep by -2.7 pp (-7.4 to 2.0; target >= +2 pp, FAIL).
4. **Contracts do not replace the story chain:** contract XP is 6.1 % of all XP (B46 8-20 %, FAIL), gear at the boss falls (item score 352 against 360, rare-or-better 0.42 against 0.48) and planner P0 wins 12.7 % (16.3 % without prep; classic 32.0 %).
5. **Run length still passes:** B11 42.4 min (+1.8 min: PACE_PREP_S and the hub contract decisions), B53 15.3 min; B12 29.2 min = 0.69 × B11 on only 11 speedrunner wins.
6. **B43 and B44 still fail as in 11-11d**; the greedy's close calls come into band (71.7 %).

## What the numbers say
1. **Delves are not worth a dungeon on this map.** A Delve pays 1.6 levels-worth (DELVE_XP_FRAC 1.0 × 1.6) and a choice of 2; the dungeon costs 6 tolls (× VAL_TIME × PLANNER_TIME_MULT, plus the boss term) and its death risk, and lies in rings 2-3 under the front. The planner enters about 0.26 dungeons per run (table pass); with the Dimming off Delves complete 42 %. EST_COMPLETE's 0.90 for Delves is far from the 14 % measured, so the start camp buys them first (cheapest per point) and the hub swaps them away (0.83 abandoned per run). Levers for the designer: DELVE_XP_FRAC, the Delve price, EXPED_TIME_DUNGEON; or count a Delve as complete at the dungeon boss with fewer trash fights.
2. **Completion is a Cull and Bounty matter.** Culls complete 55 % at P0 and 69 % with full vision; Bounties 40 % / 54 %. Without Delves the planner P0 would be at 52 % (623 / 1 192; B39 band 45-65). Vision adds +8.2 pp of completion (B42 needs 10): the Tracker and Region Charts help; the Local Guide is not bought by the planner.
3. **Contracts give less than the story chain they replace.** With 2.3 contracts completed per run (planner P0), contract XP is 6.1 % of all XP; the story chain gave about 0.9 levels and a rare choice in each of 6 zones. Gear at the boss falls and P0 wins fall 3.6 pp. Spec 12.3 item 5 names the lever: raise contract rewards first (CULL_XP_FRAC, the epic weights). B1-B3 on the expedition stay far under the classic bands.
4. **The plan barely beats no plan, and loses to a random one (B40).** Random prep buys an Ember (20 % of runs), Field Draughts, Region Charts and the Spyglass and takes 3.8 contracts; the planner's rule takes 6.2 contracts and supplies only the Tracker. Supplies and the Ember are not in the planner's prep column (expedition.md 10.2); with them the plan might win. The differences are inside their intervals at 300 runs.
5. **B43 and B44 are as in 11-11d:** the planner leaves zones before the front, with or without contracts (exit levels 11 / 22 / 32 / 42 / 53). Contracts did not lengthen zones (17.8 tolls, 11-11d 17.7).

## Readings and assumptions (designer to confirm)
1. Contract value (10.3) at the start camp: XP at the contract level, items against an uncommon of that level, best of n choices = SIM.CONTRACT_CHOICE_GEAR[n] × one roll (1 / 1.3 / 1.5 for 1-3) [assumption]; on the map: at the current level against what is equipped.
2. Site chance on unseen nodes: Cull CULL_SITE_IN / OUT × the chance of a Quest or Grind (type belief), the regions' mean when the region is hidden; Bounty BOUNTY_PLACED over the target region's expected ring 2-3 nodes; Delve 1 on the dungeon (a held Delve shows it). A fully seen node without the mark, or a node inside the Tracker's radius without it, is not a site.
3. Planner prep: SIM.PREP_POINT_VALUE 60 per point (a kept point pays hub contracts), SIM.EST_TRACKER_GAIN 0.20 as the Tracker's prior (prototype 12.2), no rerolls, no supplies but the Second Clapper. Hub swap margin SIM.HUB_SWAP_MARGIN 50.
4. "Keeps Bell charges" read as: the Second Clapper is valued at BELL_KEEP_VALUE per charge (never bought at 60 per point).
5. Random prep: TAKE_CONTRACT and BUY_PREP only (no rerolls); random hub contract once per zone at 50 %.
6. The completion table is keyed (kind, Tracker held, zone) and counts abandoned contracts out.

```
node sim/run.mjs --bot greedy --runs 100 --profile fresh --risk-in $O/obs-P0-ct.json $C

```
