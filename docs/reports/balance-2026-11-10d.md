## Summary
1. Headline, P0 reach the boss / win rate [measured]: autoplay (planner) 79.8 % / 35.9 % (before 80.3 % / 24.6 %); masher 78.7 % / 1.4 % (80.5 % / 1.7 %); opener 82.0 % / 38.2 % (83.4 % / 27.4 %); tactician 89.9 % / 46.3 % (87.6 % / 34.5 %).
2. On the spec defaults combat v2 does not yet do what it was built for. Mashing reaches the boss as often as autoplay (B49 gap +1.1 pp, floor 8). The planned opening adds +2.2 pp reach and 13 % fewer non-boss deaths (B48 floors +5 pp and 30 %), about what it added before v2 (+2.6 pp, 18 %).
3. Per fight the planned opening is clearly better: on the same 26 385 fights it ends with 82.2 % HP against autoplay's 77.3 %. It does not die less (4.2 against 4.1 per 1 000 fights). Deaths come from elites and dungeon bosses (about 30 per 1 000 fights whatever the opening), which outlast the opening.
4. Every bot wins more, because the end boss got easier: boss wins per boss reached 45 % against 31 % before. B1 fails for greedy (25.9 %), planner (35.9 %) and speedrunner (29.4 %). Removing the end-boss window damage (OFFBAL_BOSS_TAKEN 0) brings the planner to 30.2 % with the same reach.
5. Proposals [measured in scratch copies]: keep ENGAGE_DMG_COMP 1.15 (it holds autoplay's reach per class within 1 pp of 11-10c). For B49, raise HEAVY_SCRIPT_MULT and CAST_SCRIPT_MULT to 1.5 and OFFBAL_TAKEN to 0.50: masher 71.1 %, gap +9.0 pp (passes), autoplay 80.1 % (today 80.3 %). That change does not raise the opener's edge (-1.9 pp, interval -4.1 to +0.3), so B48 and B50 need a design decision, not a constant (Findings 2).

## Findings
1. **Mashing (B49).** The masher loses as little as it did before v2: 78.7 % against 80.5 %. It kills fast, and most fights hold no threat that only an answer survives. The mage masher reaches the boss 92 % against the mage autoplay's 80 % (91.5 % against 79.7 % before v2), so the mage's autoplay rotation is weaker than its biggest button in levelling [inference: from the per-class table]. Enemy damage does not open the gap, because it hurts both bots: planner minus masher is +2.0 / +1.7 / +0.4 pp at compensation 1.05 / 1.15 / 1.25.
2. **Planning (B48, B50, B5, opening pairs).** The opener (beam search on the visible tracks, below) beats autoplay's opening per fight on HP, potions and fight length, but not on deaths. B50: 39.0 % of the masher's deaths survive with the opener's first five rounds. The control, autoplay's first five rounds, saves 36.6 %, so B50 measures "not mashing" more than planning. The tactician's edge (B5 +10.4 pp, band 15-30) is the old per-round search; only 0.21 of it comes from the opener (target 0.6). Read-out [inference: from the kind table and the pairs]: deaths happen in elites and dungeon bosses after round 5. A 5-round opening plan cannot reach them, and the scripted threats in rounds 2-4 are survivable without an answer.
3. **Engage choices (B51).** The opener almost never picks a setup option: Premeditate 0 %, Gathered Power 0 %, Send the Pet 8 %. The Rogue Ambushes in 92 % of multi-enemy fights. Inside the plan's 8-round horizon the setup options score below a strike [measured: opener choice shares]. Either they are too weak, or their value lies past the horizon (designer call).
4. **Windows (B52).** The opener hits 57.5 % of its windows with a spell, the masher 79.5 %. The masher opens only 2.1 windows per run (autoplay 29, opener 18), mostly from heavies that missed it on the enemy it is already hitting. A share-based gap cannot pass while the masher's few windows are on its own target. Proposal: count windows used per 100 fights instead (opener 23.8, masher 3.9: 31 502 of 132 183 and 5 063 of 128 561 non-boss fights) [measured].
5. **Time (B53, B15, B16).** At v2 pacing combat takes 26.5 min per run (budget 21) over 51 fights (band 34-42). This needs the expedition's fewer fights (combat-v2.md 9). Fight lengths pass, end boss 40 rounds (before 42).
6. **Classes and stages (B6, B21).** The P0 spread is 8.5 pp (rogue 31.6 %, mage 40.1 %, hunter 36.1 %; limit 8). Deaths moved early: 16.9 % of runs die in Z1-Z2 (limit 12 %) and 0.6 % in Z5-Z6 (band 2-8 %). PALL passes (B3 73.6 %, B6 spread 5.8 pp).
7. **First target (B54) passes:** the opener picks another target than autoplay's default in 36.9 % of multi-enemy fights (before 17.9 %).

## Method notes and assumptions
- Opener (sim/bots/opener.mjs, combat-v2.md 11): beam search of width OPENER_BEAM 12 over action sequences to the end of round OPEN_ROUNDS 5. Candidates: every engage option, bar spells on pruned targets, the attack, items. Sequences are expanded on 1 sample. The chosen one is scored on the 7 held-out samples (OPENER_SAMPLES 8) plus OPENER_TAIL 3 autoplay rounds, with the tactician's score and potion heals credited. It is followed only if it beats autoplay on the same samples by 6 % of max HP; else autoplay plays until the next surprise. It re-plans on a new enemy, a replaced entry or a new heavy or cast entry. Autoplay rules 1-2 always stand. A routine fight (one normal enemy, no heavy or cast in sight, HP >= 70 %) is played by autoplay. [assumption: SIM.OPENER_* in sim/lib/params.mjs, compute budget.] Cost: 1.5 s per run; 61 plans per run, 60 % of them keep autoplay.
- Why the anchor: without it, the Hunter opener on the M2 v5 engine reached the boss 14.6 pp less than autoplay (150 runs). Its plans skipped setup (Mark Prey) whose payoff falls past the 8-round horizon [measured, sim hand-off].
- Leak test (qa/sim/combat2.test.mjs): the opener plans on `playerView` (fight and loot streams replaced from the bot stream). Its choice and plan do not change when the hidden streams change, and a proxy that throws on any read of them never fires.
- B50 replays: a snapshot at each fight's first decision; the masher replayed with the masher dies again in 639 of 639 replays (harness control). Opening pairs: autoplay's replay equals the live fight (tested).
- B52 counts OFF_BALANCE status windows in non-boss fights; FROZEN, which the engine also counts as a window, is not included. B53 uses the engine's v2 pacing (2.1 + 1.0 s per round, 3 + 6 s per fight).
