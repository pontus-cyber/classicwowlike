## Readings and known issues
1. **The sim's camp (items 1-4).** Every prep policy reads options through `campOffers` (the options in `C.PREP_CAMP_OPTIONS`). On the engine as built this is the engine's own filter (R.prepView lists only those), so no run changed (stage H). The speedrunner's Second Clapper now falls through to Field Draughts at P0 only: at PALL Swift Boots fill the Bell, and the PALL speedrunner bought Field Draughts in 100 % of runs before D128 as well. `--camp-options all` plays the camp's nine options on the D128 engine (board and reroll stay as built); not used in this measure.
2. **B42** now bans only Region Charts and Tracker's Notes (the camp's sight perks); the Local Guide and the Spyglass are not offered. The no-vision cell bought the Ember in 100 % of runs, as PALL did, so the win difference (+0.6 pp) is vision alone.
3. **The prep `none` arm is identical on both engines** (101 / 900 wins, every run the same): a run that sets out at once never sees the board or the options, as expected.
4. **B20** is read on medians of whole levels: zone 1's median moves from 11 to 12 on a +0.25 level shift of the mean (the mean was 11.33). The miss in zones 2-5 (1-2 levels over 10z + 1) is the expedition's, unchanged.
5. **B40 random** is inside its noise on both engines (intervals about ±3.2 pp at 900 runs); the point estimate crosses +2 pp. The lean camp has no weak options left for random prep to waste points on (it buys the Ember in 59 % of runs, Region Charts 82 %), so random prep gains more than planned prep.
6. Not run: classic cells (D128 is expedition only), one-button cells, B41 / B43 oracle cells, campaigns, the `--camp-options all` arm, the reroll-on arm (`-rr`; no bot rerolls).

## Levers for the Designer (draft; none measured)
The loss sits in the late contracts, so the levers that keep the owner's three simple choices act there [inference]:
1. Draw the camp's three offers across the run instead of zones 1-3 (for example zones 1, 3 and 5, or one each from zones 1-2, 3-4 and 5-6): a late contract held from the start brings back its XP and gear. An engine arm (`drawBoard` zones) and the cells of stage M and R: about 10 min of compute.
2. A second offer at each hub, or the zone 4-6 offers at the zone 3 hub: the PALL planner leaves 0.77 points per run unspent.
3. Raise the hub contracts' rewards in zones 4-6 toward what the old board paid.
4. Accept the lean camp's numbers: P0 is unchanged; re-base B46 and the PALL clause of criterion 2 for the lean camp.
"Take 1" (the Lead's first plan; D130 kept the four slots) would cut contracts further [inference].
