# Balance report 2026-11-12b: the M2b v14 package (the Rogue waits on Sap) and the gate defaults

Status: measurement, draft for the Lead and the Lead Game Designer. No rules constant, content value, balance.md constant or spec was changed; src/ untouched. The Engineer sets values after (decision rules below).

- **Engine measured: main 30d565a** (src/ identical at eb70678) = the v14 engine (engineer-2026-11-12): rows 1, 2 and 4 of changes-m2b-v14.md; `C.GATES = { rareHpMultZ1, firstMeetWindup, sapHold, passStealth }`, all on by default.
- **Simulator: eb70678.** A cell without `--gates` now plays the engine's own `C.GATES` defaults (the game as built); a cell with `--gates` plays the named switches on and every other off (the v13 rule); the cell name marks every switch played at another value than its default (`g5`, `g6`, `sap`, `pst`; `no<tag>` when off); cell metas hold `gatesPlayed`. Hash identity (stage H, `sim/hashgates.mjs` against the sim of 30d565a on the same engine): cells that name their gates **72 / 72 identical on classic and 72 / 72 on the expedition** (4 gate sets × planner, onebutton, greedy × 3 classes × 2 seeds); a plain cell equals the cell that names every default switch, 18 / 18 on each map. qa/sim **128 / 128** (v13.test's setGates check now covers sapHold and passStealth; new v14.test).
- **Script:** `bash sim/measure-v14.sh sim/out/pass-v14 "H C1 C2 C3 C4"` (7 min, 2 workers, the machine to itself). Tables: `node sim/v14report.mjs`. "+dirty" in the metas from the masher cell on is the untracked report script only (sim/v14report.mjs, written during the run).
- **Cells** (changes-m2b-v14.md "What the sim must run"): 1 classic P0, planner row 1 on (as built) and row 1 off (`--gates rareHpMultZ1,firstMeetWindup`), onebutton, and the masher (added for O14's second figure), 1 000 per class; 2 expedition P0 with the start camp, planner 300 per class, onebutton `--think --think-mash` 50 per class; 3 classic P0 onebutton `--think` 50 per class; 4 classic P0 planner, Rogue, row 1 (a) only (`--gates rareHpMultZ1,firstMeetWindup,sapHold`), 1 000. Same seeds (1000 on) and tables as the v13 measure, so every cell is **paired by class and seed** with its v13 cell (sim/out/pass-v13, the v13 engine: rows 5-6 and row 1 off), the mash cells (classic, before the Deathblow package) and the v12 cell (expedition planner, before the package).
- **Deaths** = the fight ended the run or needed a resurrect; in the replays, outcome 0 or a resurrect used. **Answered share** = Deathblows answered / (answered + landed), from the recorder's f2 fields. Rates: Wilson 95 %; paired differences: normal interval of the paired difference.

## Summary
1. **Row 1 stays.** Autoplay now answers **100 %** of the Rogue's zone 1 Deathblows on both maps (classic 285 / 285, expedition 72 / 72; v13: 67 and 61 %). The Rogue's zone 1 special deaths fall to **14.0 %** on classic P0 (v13 27.2 %, row 1 off 25.7 %, before the package 21.1 %) and **8.3 %** on the expedition (v13 22.9 %, before 14.1 %): under the pre-package values, as the spec expected. Rogue reach **+2.8 pp** (1.5 - 4.1) and win +0.6 pp (-1.0 - 2.2) against the row-1-off arm, paired. Mage and hunter runs are identical in both arms (rule H needs a SAPPED enemy).
2. **Row 1 (b): the rule as written drops (b) and keeps (a).** Cell 4 (a PASS removes STEALTH) gives the Rogue a reach of **82.9 %** against cell 1's **82.8 %**: +0.1 pp (-0.4 - 0.6), one run in 1 000. Win 36.5 against 37.0 % (-0.5 pp, -1.4 - 0.4); zone 1 special deaths and Deathblows answered are the same (14.0 %, 285 / 285). So (b) has no measurable effect on autoplay. Its other use, the manual Rogue who waits and stays hidden (the UI's "you stay hidden" and the Pass answer's `stealth`, which follow `passStealth`), is outside the sim. The rule was written to expect a lower reach without (b), and this result rests on a single run, so the designer or Lead should confirm it before the Engineer turns `passStealth` off.
3. **B60 expedition (replays, 50 per class):** elites **2.2 %** (pass; v13 5.4 % on the same seeds), rares 7.1 % (pass), dungeon bosses **6.7 %** (2 of 30; **still over the 5 % cap**; v13 6.5 %, 2 of 31). ## Readings and known issues
1. **Rows 5-6 alone barely move the Rogue's zone 1 line** (row 1 off: 25.7 % against v13's 27.2 %); row 1 makes the difference (14.0 %). For mage and hunter, rows 5-6 move zone 1 deaths 4.5 -> 0.6 % and 2.5 -> 0.5 %.
2. **Row 1 (b)'s value is small for autoplay.** The wait cancels the Deathblow either way (the Sap answers it at the start of round 3); STEALTH only adds the opener in the OFF_BALANCE window, and that does not change who survives the fight (zone 1 deaths equal, 285 fights). The decision rule reads reach only, and the difference is one run in 1 000.
3. **The expedition dungeon-boss miss is two fights against one boss** (Warden Belk of Harrow, n 30 dungeon-boss fights in the replays). It is the same small-sample reading as v13, and row 1 does not touch it.
4. **Samples:** B60 replays are 50 per class (expedition elites n 89, rares 28, dungeon bosses 30; classic rares 23). Cell 2's one-button full runs (50 per class) are the expedition B59 reading; v13's expedition B59 used 300 per class.
5. Not run: B62 (a) (one button and masher never Sap, so row 1 cannot move it; rows 5-6 were measured in v13 cell 7), classic PALL, expedition PALL, the flip cells and campaigns. None of these is in the v14 list.
