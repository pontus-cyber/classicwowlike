## Findings (ranked)
1. **The Deathblow is never taught on the autoplay route (Q3 missed, all classes).** Gorrak is optional, the route the engine picks
   skips him, and no Deathblow came up in zones 2-4 either (Hunter summary: 0 elites in the whole run), so G9 and H04 never showed.
   This is the owner's own complaint (he did not find Sap, 2026-11-11). Proposal for the Designer: make one scripted fight after S4
   (or Gorrak) the zone 1 exit condition, or put a Deathblow enemy into S4. Severity: high (onboarding goal).
2. **The first fight's screen says the same thing twice and shows five panels at once.** Coach line "Press 1 to attack" and the turn
   prompt "Open with Q W E (left panel) or any bar key" sit side by side, with the Engage panel, the target frame and the Opening
   planner (qa-2026-11-13-01). The owner hates clutter. Proposal (UI, small): while a coach line is shown in a fight, hide the turn
   prompt's "Opening move" text (keep Deathblow and wind-up prompts); in the guided zone's first fight, collapse the Opening planner.
   Not changed in this task: the turn prompt is game feedback, a Designer call.
3. **Hints repeat when autoplay or a fast speed is on.** A hint shown for less than ONB_HINT_MIN_MS (1.5 s) does not count as seen, so
   H02, H14, H31 and H05 came back fight after fight: 29 hint showings for 13 ids (Hunter), 48 for 14 (Mage), 36 for 11 (Rogue).
   Per spec 2.5 and 3.1 this is correct, but it reads as nagging. Proposal: a hint shown twice counts as seen. Severity: medium.
4. **Mage: "Open with Q" does nothing on its own.** Q is Pre-cast, which waits for a second key (choose spell 1 or 2); the line does
   not say so and the engine state does not change (qa-2026-11-13-02). Proposal: G7/H01 per class for the Mage ("Q, then a spell"),
   or Pre-cast takes bar spell 1 when Q is pressed twice. Severity: medium.
5. **Rogue: "Press 1 to attack" did nothing in round 1 of the first fight** (qa-2026-11-13-03, stealth on). Cause not found in this
   session (slot 1 may need a target or not be legal from stealth); G3's line should match what the Rogue can press. Severity: medium;
   to the engineer and UI.
6. **G10 "Contract site: win this fight to count it" shows at the zone hub**, where no site is in view and its arrow points at nothing
   (qa-2026-11-13-04), and then comes back twice more (D141 returns). The engine opens G10 at the hub; it should open when a site is
   first on the map. Engine follow-up (the UI cannot make it wait: the engine rests it before the map is shown).
7. **G12 (the Dimming) showed over a quest dialog** with no map in view (qa-2026-11-13-05). **Fixed** (src/ui/onboarding.js
   MAP_LINES): G12, G12.caught, G13 and G13.onRoad wait for the map; part R reads the same rule.
8. **G12 comes back four times in zone 1** (D141: it returns when the front is near again) and stays up to 19 steps at a time. It
   is the right line, but four returns in ten minutes is a lot. Proposal: return at most once (ONB_BEAT_RETRIES).
9. **The first summary is dense** (qa-2026-11-13-06): three columns of about 25 rows, and the Furnace chart is cut at the bottom at
   1366x768. The nudge itself works (glow, one line, 2 clicks to buy). Proposal: for the first run, fold Deeds and the chart.
10. **H13 "Drag your new spell onto the bar" stays up for 15 steps** at a hub (it is done only by the drag); fine for a player who
   reads it, but it sits over the hub screen through the trainer and vendor. Low.
11. **Full-run is long, not stuck:** Hunter 748 s, Rogue (1920x1080) 1142 s, Mage 1246 s (to the end boss), all with three browsers
   on two CPU cores. The guided zone 1 (manual) took about 3.5 minutes per class; the rest is autoplay. No stuck coach line,
   modal or barred node (0 divergences, 0 problems). Low.
12. **Skip paths** (guide off at class select, Skip guide mid-zone, Hints off): pass in ui-regress part R (3) and (4); not repeated here.
