# Playtest 2026-11-11: one expedition run with start camp (Playtest & QA Lead)

Status: draft. Build: main at 9ba9157 (`node tools/build.mjs`), headless Chromium at 1366x768, Playwright.
Run: Rogue, seed 4, `?prep=1`. At the camp: keys 1 and 2 (Delve: The Harrow Cellars, zone 1; Cull reedfolk, zone 2),
key A (Field Draughts), Enter. From the first hub on, the engine's own choices (AUTO), with a screenshot at each
milestone. The run reached zone 4 (Sunscoured Wastes) and died there at level 26 to a Dust Jackal in a quest fight
(round 7), 60 tolls (par 120), 11 fights, contracts 1 of 5 completed, 3 expired, Reserve left 1. No console or page
errors. A Dimmed fight was not reached: the autoplay leaves each zone before the Dimming reaches the player.

## What felt wrong or confusing (most important first)
1. **Heat toasts flap on a zone change.** Arriving at Stilt Haven showed five heat toasts at once: Burning, Smouldering,
   Calm, Smouldering, Calm. The stack ignores TOAST_MAX for heat changes, and the state goes up and down inside one
   step. The player cannot tell the current state from the stack; one toast with the final state would read better.
   Screenshot: screens/qa-2026-11-11-05-hub2.jpg.
2. **Under-levelled at the end of the run.** Level 26 entering zone 4 (levels 31-40) after 60 of 120 tolls: the run
   spent half its time budget and still arrived 5 levels short. On the engine's choices the expedition map pushes on
   faster than XP keeps up. For the Designer and the Sim role: compare with the balance targets.
3. **Contracts expire silently.** The zone 1 Delve expired when the run left zone 1 without the dungeon. The only
   signal is a combat log line ("Contract expired: ..."). There is no toast, and nothing at the Road or the Bell says
   "leaving now expires 1 contract". Of 5 contracts, 3 expired.
4. **Duplicate offers on the contract board.** Offers 2 and 7 are the same contract (Zone 2, Cull reedfolk in
   Rotplank Causeway, same pay). It looks like a bug to the player. Screenshot: screens/qa-2026-11-11-02-prep-taken.jpg.
5. **The contract card's numbers have no labels.** "9 ⚪ 56 ●" and "◆◆ 1 of 3" only make sense from the tooltip (gold;
   choose 1 of 3 items of these rarities). One word ("pay", "pick 1 of 3") would help.
6. **Heat log lines end in two full stops.** For example "Calm: you are ahead of the Star.." and "Smouldering: the
   Dimming draws near..". The toast text ends with a full stop and the log adds one more. ui-regress part I checks
   `log === toast + '.'`, so the fix and the check must change together.
7. **Tooltips cover the next action.** At the hub, the Vendor card's tooltip covers Set out. At the camp, a card's
   tooltip covers the card next to it.
8. **A contract's reward window comes after the node's own spoils** (+27 776 XP, choose one of 3). That works, but the
   two windows in a row after one fight feel like double handling. Screenshot: screens/qa-2026-11-11-05-contract.jpg.
9. **The fog map at 1366x768 is dense.** The disc is about 400 px wide. With labels now kept apart (this session), the
   hub's ring is readable, but after the Dimming spreads, clocks still crowd the outer rings. Labels may now sit on "?"
   markers. Screenshot: screens/qa-2026-11-11-04-fogmap.jpg.
10. **Death summary: "Rogue · level 26 Rogue".** The class name appears twice where a player name would stand.
    Screenshot: screens/qa-2026-11-11-05-summary.jpg.

## What felt good
- The camp reads at a glance: points left in large type, held contracts in slot order, keys on every card.
- The hub's contract offer at the top of the panel, with Take and the Reserve price, is clear.
