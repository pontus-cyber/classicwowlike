## Designer open item: the Packed Satchel anomaly is a bot bug (fixed in 1485fbb)
**Cause.** The bots keep HEAL_STOCK_TARGET (5) healing draughts and counted plain and Greater Healing Draughts together. Their item bar holds one healing item: the Greater draught while any is held (sim/lib/policy.mjs desiredItemBar; the engine autoplay's item bar uses the same rule). Autoplay drinks only what is on the item bar (rules 7.9 rule 1). With Packed Satchel (3 Healing Draughts at the start) and Alchemist's Notes (Greater draughts in every vendor), the 3 plain draughts counted toward the 5 but stayed in the bag, so every vendor visit bought only 2 Greater draughts. At the end boss the PALL rogue held 2.25 usable potions instead of 5 and drank 2.1 instead of 3.7 per boss fight [source: scratch diagnostic sim/out/satchel/diag.mjs on the designer's v3a copy, 300 runs]. Only profiles with both Satchel and Alchemist were affected (PALL and late campaign runs); P0 and P10 have no Alchemist. No engine rule is involved.

**Minimal repro** (sim before the fix, 934ab58): rogue, planner bot, PALL profile, tree 2 (Venomcraft), RISK_PRIOR table (`--warmup 0`), no actions needed beyond the bot's.
- Engine 0d663d1 (M2 v3), seed 1002: at bossStart the bag holds 3 Healing Draughts and 2 Greater; item bar slot 1 is the Greater draught. Both Greater draughts are drunk by boss round 17. In rounds 23-35 the rogue is at 23-30 % HP (5 % in round 25), below AUTO_POTION_HP 35 %, with 3 Healing Draughts in the bag and no usable healing item on the bar; it dies in round 36. Same seed without Satchel: 5 Greater draughts, all drunk, victory in round 49. With the fixed sim: victory.
- Engine 934ab58 (M2 v2), seed 1022: Greater draughts gone after round 23, 30 % and 28 % HP in rounds 33-34 with 3 Healing Draughts unused, death in round 35; without Satchel victory.

**Fix** (sim only): where Greater draughts are sold, only Greater draughts count toward HEAL_STOCK_TARGET. The Satchel draughts are extra; the bot drinks them once its Greater stack is empty, after the item bar switches between fights. Measured on the designer's v3a scratch engine (seeds 1000-1299, the designer's PALL table), PALL planner with / without Satchel:

| Class | Before the fix | After the fix |
|---|---|---|
| rogue | 59.0 / 83.3 % | 87.7 / 86.0 % |
| mage | 81.7 / 82.3 % | 84.0 / 83.3 % |
| hunter | 92.3 / 93.0 % | 91.3 / 91.3 % |

The B6 PALL gap is gone (pass: spread 5.8 pp, rogue 90.0 %). Note for the Lead (UI, not a rule): a human player who holds both potion kinds has the same problem unless both are on the item bar. The engine's default autopilot buys only plain Healing Draughts, so it does not strand them.

## Spec readings (M2 v3)
1. Hour weight (balance.md 1.1): planner and tactician value calls (map search, grind waves, Inn) use VAL_HOUR × PLANNER_HOUR_MULT, × STYLE_SPEED_HOUR_MULT for the speed route style (6 in total); the greedy uses 1. The speedrunner's value calls carry the weight, but its search minimises hours + P(death) × VAL_DEATH / VAL_HOUR + missing levels × VAL_XP / VAL_HOUR and has no value tie-breaks, so the weight does not change its choices. The other reading, dividing those two exchange rates by PLANNER_HOUR_MULT, measured 22.6 -> 23.9 % on P0 (paired, within noise); not used.
2. Resurrect awareness: the bots count R.resurrectCharges(state).boss (Boon of Return, Embers; none under the Oath of Frailty) when ranking Shrine boons and in the node model.
3. B24 and B38: the lowest HP of a fight is rebuilt from the step's events (damage after WARD and other absorbs, heals, resurrects), so a resurrect counts as 0 HP. It is stored in per mille rounded down, so "below CLOSE_FIGHT_HP" is exact. B38 uses won non-boss fights by the zone they are fought in (Z1-Z6); Camp waves count in B24, not in B38.
4. B21: Camp waves are in the Z5-Z6 stage (as before); stage bands are shares of all runs, the boss band a share of deaths.
5. B23 boss loss phases use the engine's BOSS_ENRAGE_ROUND (55) instead of a fixed 50. B23's combined check (phase 3 + enrage) does not change.
6. Tactician T2: for the Rogue the >= 2 Shards clause is skipped and autoplay plays the round; the 1-Shard clause stays.
