## What the numbers say
1. **The loot rows close most of the win gap.** Rows 1-2 measured +4.1 pp on planner P0 (boss ilvl +1.2); with the D2 value (+3.8 pp over 11-11f's D2 arm in total) the planner P0 reaches 22.9 % and PALL 50.4 %. Boss win given reach rose from 21.6 to 32.1 %. Gear is still under the classic map's (ilvl 47.7 against 50.1).
2. **Delves at price 1 bring back buy-then-swap.** The camp buys about one Delve per run (98 per point at the 0.35 prior), the hub swaps 0.61 of them away for hub offers, 0.11 complete. At price 2 none are taken and wins do not change (-0.6 pp). The Delve is not worth its dungeon to the planner at either price; the guard returns it to 2.
3. **The second Dimmed toll cuts encounters but chains them.** Caught zones fell 28.6 -> 21.7 %, encounters 2.9 -> 2.1 per run, but Dimmed deaths rose (18 with one toll, 52 with two): during a 2-toll fight the front moves on and the next step is deeper. The arm that raises the damage instead (9b) is worse on both parts. B43's caught band (8-18 %) is 3.7 pp away with the planner pricing the encounter as measured.
4. **B12 is a speedrunner pace miss, not the camp's minute.** From SET_OUT the median win is 30.8 min (over 30) and the ratio to B11 is 0.71 (over 0.70) on 278 wins; the speedrunner now takes 2.3 Delves per run and clears 0.84 dungeons, which costs minutes.
5. **The PALL hunter's lead is Ricochet Bolt.** Row 8 targets Beastcall Horn, which the bots never cast; without Ricochet Bolt the classic PALL hunter falls to 54.8 %, the other classes' level. B6 PALL on the expedition is 17.3 pp (hunter 61.3 %).

3. B12 from SET_OUT: the run's autoplay minutes minus PACE_PREP_S per start camp (record field `ct.ps`).
4. B42 row 6: the no-vision cell has Scout's Eye, Cartographer and Old Hand removed and the planner never buys Region Charts, Tracker's Notes, Local Guide or the Spyglass (`--meta-drop`, `--prep-ban`); points and Swift Boots kept.
