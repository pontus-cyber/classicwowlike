## Summary
1. **Fog-only bots and the leak test are in.** Planner (belief search, depth 4), greedy (threat rule), speedrunner and random decide the expedition map from `R.mapView` only. The leak test permutes the hidden nodes' contents at every map decision of real runs: every fog bot decides the same; the oracle changes its choice on 38 of 132 planner decisions, so the test sees a leak when there is one.
2. **Run length is met on the expedition map and missed on the classic map:** B11 40.6 min (classic 53.7), B12 27.5 min = 0.68 × B11 (classic 30.3 min), B53 15.0 combat minutes (classic 25.4). B11 passes by 0.6 min, at the band's lower edge.
3. **Calculability lands in its bands:** C_step 0.69 at P0 and 0.85 with the PALL vision kit; C_route 0.10 at P0 and 0.31 with the kit. The owner's "partly chance" holds at a fresh profile; vision buys it back.
4. **The Dimming shapes the greedy, not the planner.** Planner P0 is caught in 7.0 % of zones (band 8-18 %) and 1 of its 55 non-boss deaths is a Dimmed encounter (band 5-20 %); without the Dimming its zones are as long (B44 1.02, target >= 1.5), because its own time weight and boss term already end each zone. The greedy is caught in 31.5 % of zones, Dimmed encounters are 52 % of its non-boss deaths, and it wins 0.7 %.
5. **Win rates fall by half without contracts:** planner P0 16.3 % (classic 32.0 %), planner PALL 29.7 % (classic 58.7 % in 11-11c), speedrunner PALL 8.7 % (classic 20.7 %). The expedition has 28 fights per run against 51, so less gear reaches the boss (item score 360 against 403, rare-or-better slots 0.48 against 0.57). Contracts (rare and epic rewards) and prep supplies are meant to add it; B1-B3 on the expedition are not meaningful before 2b.5.

## Leak test (expedition.md 10.1)
qa/sim/fog.test.mjs, in `node --test qa/sim/`:
- At every map decision of real expedition runs (planner: 4 runs; greedy, speedrunner: 2 each; random: 4), the hidden nodes' contents (everything but id, ring, angle, neighbours, Road and Shardfall flags) are rotated among the hidden nodes on a copy, twice. The player's `mapView` must be identical (the scramble touched only hidden contents) and each bot's decision must be identical. Planner: 132 decisions, 264 scrambles, no change.
- Power check: the oracle (the same search on `R.debug.revealMap`'s view) changes its choice on 38 of the 132 states, so a bot that read hidden contents would fail.
- Structure: the bots' map code reads only `fogInput(s)` (= `R.mapView`, sim/lib/view.mjs) and the character model (`makeCtx`); the oracle's input (`oracleInput`) is the only reader of hidden contents, in a separate section of view.mjs; the oracle acts only on copies (C_route) and is compared, never played, in measured runs (C_step).

## What the numbers say
1. **Run length (B11, B12, B53, owner's 45 minutes).** The expedition map with the 9.5 budget (one Grind wave, two trash fights, tolls 3/3/2/6, XP × 1.6) brings the run from 53.7 to 40.6 minutes and combat from 25.4 to 15.0 minutes. It overshoots: 40.6 is at the band's floor, 28 fights per run is under FIGHTS_V2 (34-42), 33 resolved nodes under 44-56. Prep (PACE_PREP_S 60 s), hub contracts and contract detours will add minutes; the room to add fights is about 5-10 minutes.
2. **The planner over-levels and leaves early enough on its own.** It leaves zones at 12 / 22 / 33 / 42 / 53 (B20 10z ± 1 fails high from zone 2) after about 18 tolls, with or without the Dimming. The Dimming's schedule (first node at 6 tolls, hub at 14) rarely reaches it: 7 % of zones. Levers for the designer: XP_WEIGHT_MULT (1.6 gives 2-3 levels over 10z), or a later or faster front; B43 and B44 cannot pass while the planner's own value function ends the zone first. The prototype's planner wandered without the Dimming (84 min) because it had no boss term; this planner prices tolls past the line through projected Ascendancy (PLANNER_TIME_MULT 3, SIM.VAL_ASC).
3. **The greedy cannot live with the threat rule.** Caught in 31.5 % of zones, it loses 135 runs of 300 to Dimmed encounters and leaves zones 1-2 levels under 10z (9 / 18 / 28 / 36 / 46), then dies in quests of the next zone. Read "of arrival" as the end of the node's content (the engine checks the Dimming when the player is back on the map): with the literal reading (arrival = entry), it is caught in 61.6 % of zones and close calls are 88.5 %. A human sees "dims in N tolls"; the spec's greedy sees only the next step. Question for the designer: give the greedy the engine's default policy rule (enter only what leaves a clean way to a Road, rules 1.9.9), or accept a lower B1 greedy band on the expedition.
4. **Calculability is where the owner asked for it.** At P0 the planner's step matches the full-information step 69 % of the time and its zone route 10 % of the time; the vision kit raises them to 85 % and 31 % (bands 0.80-0.92 and <= 0.50) and cuts caught zones to 1.8 %.
5. **Gear is the missing part of the win rate.** Same level at the boss (60) and the same reach (82 %), but 47 % fewer fights mean fewer drops and a weaker boss fight. Contracts' rare and epic rewards (CONTRACT_RARE_W / EPIC_W, BOUNTY 60 / 40) are the designed source; measure B1-B3 again when 2b.5 lands, before tuning drops.

## Reproduce
```
O=sim/out/exp-2026-11-11d; P0=sim/out/pass-2026-11-11c/obs-P0.json; PA=sim/out/pass-2026-11-11c/obs-PALL.json
node sim/run.mjs --bot planner --class all --runs 100 --profile fresh --risk-in $P0 --warmup 0 --out $O
node sim/run.mjs --bot speedrunner --class all --runs 100 --profile all --risk-in $PA --warmup 0 --out $O
node sim/run.mjs --bot planner --class all --runs 100 --profile fresh --risk-in $P0 --warmup 0 --out $O --map expedition --oracle
node sim/run.mjs --bot greedy --class all --runs 100 --profile fresh --risk-in $P0 --warmup 0 --out $O --map expedition
node sim/run.mjs --bot speedrunner --class all --runs 100 --profile all --risk-in $PA --warmup 0 --out $O --map expedition
node sim/run.mjs --bot planner --class all --runs 50 --profile fresh --risk-in $P0 --warmup 0 --out $O --map expedition --dimming off
node sim/run.mjs --bot planner --class all --runs 100 --profile all --risk-in $PA --warmup 0 --out $O --map expedition --vision guide,charts,pathfinder --oracle
node sim/run.mjs --bot planner --class all --runs 100 --profile all --risk-in $PA --warmup 0 --out $O --map expedition
node sim/run.mjs --bot planner --class all --runs 50 --profile fresh --risk-in $P0 --warmup 0 --out $O --map expedition --oracle --route
node sim/run.mjs --bot planner --class all --runs 50 --profile all --risk-in $PA --warmup 0 --out $O --map expedition --vision guide,charts,pathfinder --oracle --route
```
The rows of the tables come from `expMetrics` and `runLength` (sim/lib/metrics.mjs) over each cell's runs-*.jsonl (`readJsonl`, sim/lib/output.mjs; combat minutes with `combatMinutes`, sim/lib/combatchecks.mjs). Wall time: about 8 minutes on 2 workers.
