## Findings (most important first)
1. **Fixed: the Rogue's engage prompt contradicted its own line.** In the engage round against a lone elite, the line said
   "Answer: W Sap, then wait" and the card "Sure if you wait", but the turn prompt said "answer with W Sap (a chance)".
   It now reads "answer with W Sap, then wait (Pass next round)". Check: ui-regress P (2b). Screen: screens/qa-2026-11-12-01-rogue-sap-prompt.jpg.
2. **The Rogue's wait reads as already done.** In round 2 after Sap, the line over the enemy says "sapped: answered if you
   wait · Wait: X Pass" (good), but the chip ribbon, the target frame row and the Opening ruler all say ANSWERED. A player
   who attacks in round 2 breaks Sap and the Deathblow lands. Larger fix (the track's predicted cancel shows as ANSWERED
   for every soft cancel): show "IF YOU WAIT" or a dashed ring while the cancel `breaks`. Screen: screens/qa-2026-11-12-02-rogue-wait-answered.jpg.
3. **No warning when a Deathblow is lethal.** A level-3 Mage (209 HP) entered an expedition "Elite" node at level 10 in
   ring 3 and met a level-11 rare: "Deathblow R3 ≈ 359", and round 3 sums to 447. Nothing on the line says it kills you,
   while DoT previews already have a lethal skull. Suggest a "LETHAL" mark when the estimate is at least current HP. The
   jump from ring 1 to ring 3 at level 3 is the player's (my policy's) choice, but the map tooltip's "Level 10" was the only
   warning. Screen: screens/qa-2026-11-12-03-lethal-deathblow.jpg.
4. **Floats collide when a Deathblow lands.** "Maimed: healing −50 %" sits on top of the pet's damage number and next to a
   "Dodge" float; "Deathblow landed: empowered" overlaps the damage numbers on the enemy. The most important line of the
   fight is the hardest to read. Larger fix (the word lane): give the Maimed float its own row above the hero. Screens:
   screens/qa-2026-11-12-04-maimed-floats.jpg, screens/qa-2026-11-12-06-boss-landed.jpg.
5. **Fixed: track ribbons were cut by the next chip.** On small nameplate chips the DEATHBLOW ribbon was wider than its
   chip: the next chip's round badge covered it ("DEATHBLO") and it covered its own round number; ANSWERED and OFF-BAL
   ribbons overlapped the same way. Ribbons are now drawn above neighbours, compact on small chips and shifted right of the
   round badge. An answered Deathblow's ribbon was still crimson on a frost-blue chip; it is now frost blue. Screen:
   screens/qa-2026-11-12-08-ribbons-fixed.jpg (before: 01 and 02).
6. **Fixed: heavy Deathblows had the cast icon.** A wind-up is also a cast (rules 7.12), so Threshing Blow, Tidefall Slam
   and Ledger-Breaker showed the arcane rune that marks a cast, next to the text "Heavy · hits you". Heavy versus cast decides
   the answers (the map tooltip says so), so the icon misled. Heavy now wins. Screen: screens/qa-2026-11-12-09-heavy-icon-fixed.jpg.
7. **Fixed: "your trap: answered" after a Mage's or Rogue's Disrupt.** On a dungeon boss the line said "your trap" for every
   class; it now says "Disrupt: answered", as the log does. Check: ui-regress P (4). Screen: screens/qa-2026-11-12-10-disrupt-fixed.jpg.
8. **A Deathblow aimed at the pet.** Gorrak's Threshing Blow in the Hunter fights: the panel's alert says "≈ 79 to your pet.
   MAIMS if it lands", and Send the Pet is listed as an answer. rules 7.13 (3) counts a heavy action from which the player
   lost 0 HP as answered, so a Deathblow that hits only the pet may already be answered, yet the screen calls it a threat
   to you. Not played to the end here (I answered it with Lay Trap both times). For the Engineer to confirm what windupView
   and the engine do in that case, then the text.
9. **Fixed: the fog map's node tooltip gave two instructions.** "Click to enter" was followed by "Pass through it from the
   list of moves." It now reads "Or walk through it without doing it (1 toll): Pass in the list of moves." Screen (before):
   screens/qa-2026-11-12-05-hidden-elite-tip.jpg.
10. **Answered elites stop being a threat.** After Binding Glyph the zone 1 elite Gorrak hit the Mage for 8 per round (the
    Deathblow estimate was 82), and the fight was over by round 6. The Deathblow decides the fight, as intended, but the rest
    of the elite fight has no stakes on the classic map in zone 1. For the Designer: a note, not a bug.
11. **Map node level and enemy level differ by one.** The tooltip's header says "Elite Level 4", the line under it "Old Marrow
    the Hexer · Elite level 5", the description "one level higher". On the expedition the node says "Level 9" and the fight is
    level 10. Readable once you know it; a first-time player reads it as an error.
12. **Toasts sit on the Opening ruler.** "Quest complete! ×2" and the Dev menu's toasts appear over the lower edge of the
    Opening ruler in the engage round, the moment the player reads it. Screens: 01 (toast under Round 3-4).

## What felt good
- The engage round reads at once: the crimson line over the enemy, the alert at the head of the panel, the tagged card and
  the prompt all name the same key. I never had to hover to find the answer.
- The Rogue's wait (Sap, then Pass) feels clever, and "you stay hidden" pays off: round 3 opens with Shadowstep Strike into
  the off-balance window.
- "No answer ready. Kill it first or brace for it" in round 2 of a landed fight is clear and, in red, alarming.
- A landed Deathblow is a real threat: both runs that let the dungeon boss's land died in that fight at level 8, with the
  MAIMED badge and the halved potion number in view.
