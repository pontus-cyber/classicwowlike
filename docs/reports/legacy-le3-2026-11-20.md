# LE3: what a bought hall does to Legacy paid, and two ways to fix the check (2026-11-20)

Status: **draft** (Simulation & Balance Engineer), for the Lead. Nothing here is approved by the owner. Question from the Lead after D167: LE3 (cumulative Legacy at hero 20 within ±15 % of the spec curve) fails with any bought hall (v2 +20.9 %, v3d +21.6 %) and passes without purchases. Which hall entries raise the Legacy paid a run, and by how much; then (A) a curve for a player who buys, (B) payouts at hero 15-20 cut so the buying chain meets today's curve. src/ and sim/lib/hall.mjs are untouched.

## Verdict

1. **Recommendation: A, and read LE3 at the hero level, not at the end of the run that crosses it.** Read that way the buying chain and the nothing-bought chain are the same curve: cumulative Legacy at hero 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 is **269 / 641 / 1 135 / 1 717 / 2 399** with the v3d hall against 267 / 626 / 1 120 / 1 721 / 2 537 with nothing bought (+4.1 % and +4.3 % over the 1 650 account wing at hero 20). No payout changes. The buyer earns more a run (+18 % over runs 1-7, +21 to +40 % a run at hero 14-19) and reaches hero 20 in 6.0 runs where nothing bought needs 6.8, and the two cancel.
2. **The +21.6 % is a reading artefact, not a payout surplus.** On the LH1 seeds run 6 ends at hero 19.9, so LE3 reads the whole of run 7 (hero 19 -> 22.5, 343 points) and gets 2 057. One tenth of a hero level the other way (run 6 ends at 20.0 or 20.1, as on seeds 9060-9119 and on both seed sets together) and the same hall reads **1 718 (+4.1 %), a pass**. The hours gate flips on the same 0.1 level: 6.03 h on the 9-run branch, **5.40-5.44 h** on the 8-run branch. No hall price can pass both gates until both are read continuously (section 5).
3. **Where the Legacy comes from.** Purchases add +309 to +319 points over runs 1-7. Boss kill 33-34 %, first kill 27-28 %, levels 16-17 %, dungeons 12 %, contracts 8-9 %. The first-kill line (100 points) is paid at every win in the sim's cells, because the cell profile has never killed Oruvath; the game pays it once per class. That line alone is 84-91 of the gain and 298 of the chain's 2 000.
4. **B works but is expensive.** The cheapest single constant is LEGACY_BOSS_KILL 120 -> 60 (hero 20 +24.7 % -> +13.2 %, 1.8 points inside the band, hours 6.02, LE4 3, 2, 3, 3). It cuts half the winner's reward to fix a number that passes when read at the hero level.
5. **The stored v3d chain predates D166.** Its cells were played at 534c864, before the Hunter bar fix (4e89291). At HEAD the Hunter wins 45 of 60 at hero 17 and 42 of 60 at hero 19 with the technique entries (were 27 and 20). Rogue and Mage cells are identical. D167's 6.03 h and +21.6 % are therefore not at HEAD; the HEAD chain is +24.7 % and 6.03 h on the same seeds (section 2).

- Cells are the v3d chain's stage cells (hero 1, 4, 7, 11, 14, 17, 19, 20, 22, 24 with the entries bought by then), the same heroes with nothing bought, and the **ladder** at hero 14 and 19: the buy order's entries added one at a time (11 and 16 cells). 60 runs a class (180) on seeds 9000-9059 (set A, the LH1 seeds) and a second sample on seeds 9060-9119 (set B); "ab" is both (360). Paired on seed. Noise [estimate]: about ±1 % on a cumulative at 360 runs, ±5-8 points on one ladder step.

## 4. Why LE3 reads +24.7 % and what it is made of

| Step | Cumulative at hero 20 | Against 1 650 |
|---|---|---|
| Read as LE3 reads it (end of run 7, hero 22.5), set A | 2 057 | +24.7 % |
| Same chain, read inside the crossing run (hero 20) | 1 722 | +4.4 % |
| Seeds 9060-9119: run 6 ends at 20.1, read at the end of run 6 | 1 722 | +4.4 % |
| Both sets: run 6 ends at 20.0, end of run 6 | 1 718 | +4.1 % |
| Nothing bought, end of run 7 (hero 20.3) / inside the run | 1 752 / 1 718 | +6.2 % / +4.1 % |

- **Run granularity: +335 of the 407 surplus.** A run is 2.5-3 hero levels and pays 245-365 points, so a reading at the end of the crossing run swings by up to a whole run (±20 % of 1 650), against a ±15 % band. The v2 hall (+20.9 %) and v3d (+21.6 %) read run 7 (run 6 ended at 19.9); v3c (+9.2 %) crossed 20 in run 6. v3 (+16.5 %) crossed in run 6 too and fails on its lower price (1 540) against 1 794. The hours gate depends on the same crossing (section 6).
- **The buyer is not ahead at a given hero level.** Per run the buyer earns 32 % more at hero 14-19 (runs 5-7: 1 026 against 779 points), but it needs 6.0 runs to reach hero 20 where the nothing-bought chain needs 6.8. The curves by hero level coincide to within 3 % up to hero 20.
- **First kill.** With the line paid once per class (expected value, per class, from the cells' win rates; `--fk once`) the buyer's hero 20 is 1 847 at the end of run 7 (+11.9 %) and 1 583 inside the run (-4.0 %); nothing bought 1 635 (-0.9 %) and 1 606 (-2.7 %). The harness overstates the income of every chain by 110-210 points at hero 20 and by 220-340 at hero 25; the bought chain more, because it wins more. Hero 25 with the line once: 2 400 (+2.1 %) on set A, 2 130 (-9.4 %) on both sets, where the hero reaches 25 after run 8 and the hall is not yet paid.

## 7. Option B: cut payouts so the chain meets today's curve

Reference: set A (LH1 seeds), the chain as D167 reads it (+24.7 %, 6.03 h). Lines recomputed from `ln`; play is unchanged (the planner does not read Legacy), purchases move with the balance, cells re-chosen by stage and level. "MISSING" cells were taken from the nearest level played; those rows (hours 5.93-5.96) are approximate.

| Cut | Hero 20 (end of run 7) | Hero 25 | LE4 | Hours to 25 | Nothing bought, hero 20 / 25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| As built | 2 057, +24.7 % | +16.6 % | 3, 2, 3, 3 | 6.03 | +6.2 / +5.8 % |
| LEGACY_BOSS_KILL 120 -> 90 | 1 956, +18.5 % | +10.6 % | 3, 2, 3, 3 | 6.02 | +2.4 / +1.7 % |
| LEGACY_BOSS_KILL 120 -> 72 | 1 903, +15.3 % | +7.3 % | 3, 2, 3, 3 | 6.02 | +0.1 / -0.7 % |
| **LEGACY_BOSS_KILL 120 -> 60** | **1 867, +13.2 %** | +5.1 % | 3, 2, 3, 3 | 6.02 | -1.4 / -2.3 % |
| LEGACY_BOSS_KILL 120 -> 60, runs from hero 13 | 1 928, +16.9 % | +7.7 % | 3, 2, 3, 3 | 6.02 | +2.1 / +0.2 % |
| LEGACY_PER_LEVEL 20 -> 15 | 1 954, +18.4 % | +11.4 % | 3, 2, 3, 3 | 6.02 | +1.3 / +1.4 % |
| LEGACY_PER_LEVEL 20 -> 10 (approx.) | 1 881, +14.0 % | +7.5 % | 3, 1, 4, 3 | 5.96 | -3.5 / -3.0 % |
| LEGACY_PER_ZONE_HERO 10 -> 7.5 | 1 944, +17.8 % | +10.5 % | 3, 2, 3, 3 | 6.02 | +0.2 / -0.3 % |
| Every line x 0.85 from hero 13 (runs 5-7) | 1 892, +14.7 % | +5.2 % | 3, 2, 3, 3 | 6.02 | -0.2 / -3.4 % |
| Every line x 0.90, all levels (approx.) | 1 852, +12.2 % | +4.9 % | 3, 1, 4, 3 | 5.96 | -4.0 / -4.5 % |

- **Which constant:** LEGACY_BOSS_KILL, because it is 34 % of the purchase gain, follows exactly the entries that raise the win rate, and moves the nothing-bought chain least (-1.4 % at hero 20). It must go to **60 (-50 %)** to land inside the band; 72 is 0.3 points outside. PER_LEVEL, PER_ZONE and a uniform cut also reach the band but at -50 % (PER_LEVEL) or on every payout (about -10 %); PER_LEVEL is in the level-up banner text ("+20 Legacy points").
- **Effect:** hours stay at 6.02-6.03 where the cells exist (purchase timing moves by a run at most; the 9-run branch stays). LE3 at 25 falls to +5 % and LE4 holds at 3, 2, 3, 3. The band is cleared by 1.8 points at best, inside the ±1.2 % noise, and the cut does not move the hero-20 crossing: on seeds 9060-9119 (run 6 ends at 20.1) the same cut takes a passing +4.4 % to -4.1 % and hero 25 from +3.0 % to -6.7 %, with hours still 5.4.
- **Cost:** half the winner's reward (D154: the player may optimise for winning), and a real-game income 10-15 % below the sim's, since the game pays the first kill once and the cells pay it at every win (section 4). B would double-correct.

## 8. Recommendation

**A.** Make LE3 read cumulative Legacy at the hero level, take the planner chain with purchases as the curve (table in section 5), and keep the ±15 % band. It passes at +4.1 % and +2.1 % with the hall and payouts as accepted in D159-D167. B reaches the band only by halving the kill payout, does not change what the hero-level reading shows, and would set the real income about 25 % under the sim's once the first-kill line is paid as the game pays it. Follow-ups, in order:

1. Sim (mine, next): the chain's LE3 read at the hero level in sim/lh1report.mjs and the first kill paid once a class in the chain model (the cells' profile has no kill).
2. Lead: decide the hours gate on the continuous reading. The buyer's 5.4 h is below 6.0 on every seed set; the 6.03 h of D167 is a ninth run. Options: accept about 5.4 h for the planner as the good-player floor (a human at 70 % of the planner's rate lands near 7.7 h [inference]), or raise the hours with the hall (not a price question: prices move the run in which the hall completes, not the XP pace; Quick Study I/II are the levers).
3. Lead: re-record the LH1 chain figures in D167 (+24.7 % and 6.03 h at HEAD, not +21.6 %); the stored chain predates D166.

## 9. Limits

- 180 runs a cell on set A, 360 on both sets (second sample on the chain, nothing-bought and ladder cells; the hero 22 and 24 cells and the hero 20 cell have both samples; the cut table runs on set A only). The v3d chain is bistable at the 20.0 line: three of four sample choices land on one side. The numbers of sections 2 and 7 are set A because D167 is on those seeds; sections 3 and 5 use both sets.
- Ladder steps carry ±5-8; only groups and steps of +10 or more are read. Quick Study's -10 and the Lost Technique steps are not explained.
- The planner is the good player; a nothing-bought reference by interpolation of cells at hero 1, 4, 7, 11, 14, 17, 19 (not a played chain).
- First kill once is an expected value from the cells' win rates (per class), not a played campaign. The chain's Hunter hawk, Oaths and the level-20 wing's effects are not played.
- Interpolation inside a run is linear in hero level; Legacy is paid at the run's end, so the balance in hand on reaching hero 20 is the run-start cumulative.
