## What the numbers say
1. **A1 and the cheaper contract camp fix B39 and B46.** Contract XP without the level penalty doubles the value of late contracts; with EST_COMPLETE at 0.35 for Delves and the Delve at 2 points, the planner buys Culls and Bounties only and completes 61 %. The cost: no planner takes a Delve any more, so B1-B3 (Delve XP, dungeon loot) and B4 cannot be read on Delves. The prior is close to what Delves taken by others complete (random prep 39.9 %, speedrunner 34.2 %); the price is what keeps them out. Lever for the designer: Delve price back to 1, or DELVE_XP_FRAC up.
2. **C2 overshoots B43.** The learned price of a Dimmed encounter (about 1.4 % death at depth 0 for the planner, HP lost about 0.1) is right for the encounter, and the planner now takes about three per run near the front (Hot nodes pay more). B43's bands were set from the prototype planner with a fixed price; the measured planner is caught in 29 % of zones and 22 % of its non-boss deaths are Dimmed. Either the band moves (the designer's call: the owner wants the dark to chase), or the encounter gets dearer (DIM_LEVEL_PER_DEPTH, DIM_PACK_SIZE: the learned table follows).
3. **The planned camp beats random only after D2.** With Field Draughts in every run, planned minus random is +1.6 pp; without them (the point goes to the Ember) +5.2 pp and planner P0 wins 19.1 %. The value is now set (SIM.POTION_VALUE 30).
4. **Winnable is still short.** Planner P0 15.4 % (19.1 % with D2) against 20 %; PALL 46.6 % against 50 %. Gear at the boss rose a little (item score 352 -> 367, ilvl 45.6 -> 46.5) and stays under the classic map's (ilvl 50.1): the loot side of the 9.5 budget, as the designer expected ("next package").
5. **B41 C_step PALL fell to 0.743** with prep and contracts: the PALL stand-in's fog decisions differ more from the oracle's when sites matter (the oracle sees every site). C_route passes. Reading for the designer: the band was set on 11-11d's runs without contracts.
6. **B12 misses the 30-minute cap by 0.6 min** (n=86 wins; 0.70 × B11 holds). The speedrunner now completes Delves (34 %) and enters more dungeons.

## Readings and assumptions
1. B20 read as the median level at each zone exit (zones 1-5) against 10z ± 1, as changes-m2b-v10.md does.
2. B43 "caught zones": zones with at least one Dimmed encounter (expMetrics, as 11-11d/e).
3. The D2 rule names forced arms; for an option bought in at least half of cell 2's runs the script ran a ban arm instead (sim-2026-11-11f, approved), so "the difference" is cell 2 minus the planner without the option.
4. Gear rows use the mean item score of the equipped slots (as 11-11e).
