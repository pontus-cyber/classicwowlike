# Balance report 2026-11-13c: the step from zone 1 to zone 2 (blind testers, D144)

Status: measurement, draft for the Lead and the Lead Game Designer. No rules constant, content value, balance.md constant or spec was changed; src/ untouched (tuning arms ran on scratch copies of src/ under sim/out/). Sim changes are listed under "Simulator".

**Verdict.** The blind testers' reading is confirmed in the sim, and the step is sharpest on the guided first run they played. Zone 1 takes about 5 fights at about 2 levels per fight. About half of zone 1's XP comes from quest turn-ins. Zone 1 never puts three enemies in one fight (QUEST_MAX_SIZE_Z1 2). Zone 2 starts with a three-enemy quest in 25 % of runs, and the first zone 2 fight is 1.7 levels above the player on average (3.2 on the guided run). Those first fights carry the zone 2 deaths: for the planner, the first zone 2 fight kills 7x more often than the later zone 2 fights (2.1 % against 0.3 % per fight). **Recommended option: a6, zone 2's inner ring (ring 1, levels 12-14) keeps the zone 1 cap of two enemies per quest.** Three-enemy quests then start at zone 2's ring 2. Measured on the current main (bbe83a8, rewardPrimary on), paired: the planner's deaths in zone 2 fall from 2.8 % to 1.4 % of entering runs, runs dying in zone 2's first 3 fights from 2.5 % to 1.0 %, and the one-button guided run's zone 2 deaths from 13.0 % to 8.8 %. Win and reach do not fall (planner win +0.9 pp, -1.2 to 3.0; reach +1.9 pp, 0.1 to 3.6). This is an engine change (one line, best as a named constant); exact change under "Options". The measured ways to make zone 1 harder with three-enemy quests (a2, a4) kill too much before level 10 and are not recommended. Zone 1 ease stays open as a Designer item. rewardPrimary (D144) does not change zones 1-2 (zone 1 lowest HP +0.3 pp, zone 1-2 deaths -0.3 pp, n.s.), so no zone 1 offset is needed for it. It moves planner P0 win from 27.0 % to 33.2 % (+6.2 pp paired, 3.6 to 8.8; B1 20-35 %: inside, 1.8 pp under the ceiling).

How this matches the testers [source: docs/feedback/2026-11-13-blind-casual.md, -tactician.md]:
1. **"Level 1 to 10 in about five fights":** confirmed. 5.1 fights on the standard map (level 11), 4.4 on the guided run (level 9-10). Every bot gains about 2 levels per fight in zones 1 and 2. A quest node pays about as much at turn-in as its fight: quest turn-in XP is QUEST_TURNIN_XP_FRAC 1.15 x XP_WEIGHT_MULT 1.4 of xpCurve(CL) [source: src/constants.js].
2. **"One contract payout gave 6 to 9":** confirmed. A zone 1 Cull pays 2.0 x 1.4 x xpCurve(8) = 8 176 XP, 3.0 levels at level 6 (the tactician's 8 176). On the guided run the fixed board Cull is a third of zone 1's XP (3.1 levels). On the standard map zone 1 contracts are 5 % of zone 1 XP, because the board's zone 1-2 offer is completed in zone 1 in only some runs.
3. **"Never below about 75 % health in zone 1":** confirmed for the guided run. The lowest HP per fight averages 87 %, no fight goes near death, and zone 1 deaths are 0 (rescue and Gorrak's level clamp). On the standard map the mean is 70 % (planner), with 2.8 % of fights near death.
4. **"Died in the first normal zone 2 quest (two level 16 enemies plus a third at round 4)":** this is the measured worst case. In the first zone 2 fight, three or more enemies kill 7.1 % (planner) and 17.1 % (onebutton), against 0.2-0.5 % and 0.5-1.3 % with one or two enemies. A gap of +3 or more kills 7.0 % and 13.7 %. A gap of +5 or more kills 19.5 % and 27.3 %. On the guided run 72 % of first zone 2 fights start at a gap of +3 or more, because the guided route leaves zone 1 at level 9-10.
5. **Where the step is, by bot.** For the planner (and the greedy, which enters zone 2 at level 8) the step is real: zone 1's last 3 fights kill 0.9 % per fight, the first zone 2 fight 2.1 %, later zone 2 fights 0.3 %. For the onebutton and masher, zone 1's end is already as dangerous as zone 2's start (zone 1 last 3: 4.3 % and 3.9 % per fight), because zone 1's elite, rare and dungeon boss bite one-button play (B59 d: 26.0 % deaths per fight, plus about 21 % survived falls through the Ember of Return). The guided run has no such fights (Gorrak is clamped to levels 4-6), so there the step goes from no risk at all to the zone 2 entry fights. That is the testers' "nothing in between".

## Readings and known issues
1. **The guided route in the sim is not the guide's route.** The sim bots (planner map policy; an action the guide refuses is replaced by the engine's autoplay action, in 41 of 900 runs per guided cell) leave the guided zone at level 9-10 after 4.4 fights, so Gorrak's gate moving to S3 changed nothing in the sim. The engineer measured the autoplay route leaving at level 12 [source: docs/handoffs/engine-2026-11-13d.md section 2]. A player who follows the guide and autoplays now enters zone 2 about 2 levels higher than the sim's guided bots and close to the standard map's +1.7 gap. The casual tester entered zone 2 at level 10 on build 30a5f30, before that change. The guided cells here are the low-level case.
2. **Bots do not learn.** The sim cannot measure "nothing taught proper play"; it measures where the risk sits. With option 1, zone 2's inner ring becomes the in-between step: two-enemy fights at +1 to +3 levels before three-enemy fights.
3. **B15 and B16** are judged against bands set for the classic map. Re-setting them for the expedition is a Designer item, not part of this report.
4. **The 30-minute limit on background jobs** stopped one stage on 1ce100b (a5 guided onebutton). It was re-run by hand with the same command (sim/out/pass-cliff-1ce100b/measure.log).
