## Readings and known issues
1. **Bot readings (cbed250).** planFog adds the passage XP (VAL_XP on the banked level) at a Road leaf reached at zone time <= `swift.tolls` (the search's zone time plus the way's tolls, checked before TIME_TRAVEL) and at a Road taken now (the view's `swift.xp`, equal to `heatPreview(TAKE_ROAD).swift`); speedFog books it as levels, capped at its exit level, at its level price. **The speedrunner's exit rule at 10z - 1 also counts it** (it sits outside both searches): a Road reached in the window without a Dimmed step is preferred to the Bell unless its way is longer than the channel by more than `swift.frac` × the bot's level price in tolls (v11 policy: VAL_XP / (VAL_TIME × 9) = 1.85 tolls per level, so about 1.4 tolls; v12 policy: 16.7 tolls per level). This is my reading of "value the XP as any XP" for the exit; it moved the v11 policy's Bell exits inside the window from 1 141 to 655 of about 10 400 zone exits. Designer or Lead to confirm. With the switch off every bot plays as before (hash identity above).
2. **B60 expedition elites 3.6 %** (3 of 84; cap 3 %; v14 2.2 %, 2 of 89): one fight over the cap, none in zone 1 and none against Belk (Sisel Mistweaver, Wenlith of the Veil, Kharun the Unburied). With row 2 on, the one-button runs follow other routes (the one-button bot uses the planner's map search), so the replayed fights are not v14's [inference]. Row 1's rule reads dungeon bosses only.
3. **Small samples:** B60 dungeon bosses n 30; Belk fights 26-31 per expedition planner cell (the Rogue's 8-9).
4. **Row 4 was measured on the classic map only** (the spec's cell 4). The as-built Mawgrim figures repeat the v14 cell (163 / 162 fights, 34.4 / 33.3 %).
5. The tables' "/ B11" column divides every speedrunner row by the as-built B11 (43.6 min). With row 2 off the same engine's B11 is 45.0 min, so the row-2-off v11 cell is at 0.711 × B11 (32.0 min from run start; line 31.5).
6. C3B (planner P0 and PALL with row 2 off) is not in the spec's list: it is the paired base for row 2's "planner win falls" clause and isolates row 1 against v14.
7. Milliseconds per run are not comparable with earlier passes (shared machine). Not run: classic PALL, opener full runs, campaigns; the ROAD_SWIFT_TOLLS 13 arm (its trigger, B11 < 40, did not occur).
