Targets: combat-v2.md 12 (B48-B55, the changed B5, B15, B16, B53), balance.md 3 checks combat touches (B1, B3, B4, B6, B21), expedition.md H.6 heat targets (B58, B56, B57; B58 was numbered B55 in section H and written B58 here until D61 renamed it). - Engine: main 94226e4 (combat v2.1 with D60). The src/ tree used differs from 94226e4 only in src/ui/heat.js (UI) [source: `git diff 94226e4 f247570 --stat -- src`].
- Simulator: stages W, P, M, O at 57976e2 (D59 opener, wind-up recorder, B50 gap / B52 per 100 / B55 checks); stages T, X, H at 177bce5 (heat variants; the default bots' code is unchanged: planner-P0-heat reproduces planner-P0 run for run) [measured].
- Interruption: a server restart stopped stage O after the Rogue and Mage classes. One tuning cell (opener e30s40) was lost and not re-run.
- Before: the 11-10d measure (sim/out/m2b-2026-11-10d, sim 21a0a00, combat v2 without v2.1).

## Summary
1. Headline, P0 reach the boss / win [measured, 1 000 runs per class]: autoplay (planner) 78.9 % / 34.2 %; masher 67.9 % / 1.4 %; opener 85.8 % / 38.9 %; tactician (300 per class) 91.7 % / 43.6 %.
2. **The opening now decides the fight.** B48 passes on the current constants: opener - autoplay +7.0 pp reach (5.4 - 8.5), deaths per 1 000 non-boss fights 0.61 × autoplay's (0.54 - 0.69). B49 (masher 67.9 %, gap +10.9 pp), B50 (71.0 %, gap to autoplay's opening +10.1 pp), B52 (27.4 vs 3.8 windows used per 100 fights) and B55 (opener 76.5 %, masher 5.5 % of wind-ups answered) pass.
3. **Proposal: keep EMPOWER_DMG 0.15 and STAGGER_DMG 0.30.** Every measured variant raises the opener's margin a little but makes autoplay 0.5 - 2.1 pp harder and the masher 5 - 9 pp harder; none is needed for B48 (table below).
4. Still failing: B51 Rogue (Ambush 74 %) and Mage (Gathered Power 7 %), B5 (+9.4 pp, band 15-30), B16 end boss 42 rounds (band 30-40), B6 P0 class spread 11.9 pp, B21 (19.2 % of runs die in Z1-Z2), B1 greedy 23.8 %, B53 26.3 min and B15 51 fights (the expedition's job).
5. Heat [measured, approximation]: B57 passes (+2.5 pp), B56 passes (76 %). B58 fails: heat-avoid wins 12.7 pp more than heat-seek (46.2 % against 33.5 %), because a planner that weighs hours × 4 meets a +0 % boss. The heat-seek bot does not yet price the heat rewards, so this measures the hour weight more than heat.

## Method notes
- Opener D59 (sim/bots/opener.mjs): at each depth the beam keeps the OPENER_BEAM best and the best sequence of every other first action; the held-out scoring takes the best by tail score, then the best of other first actions up to OPENER_ROOT_KEEP 6; stun and control spells are candidates on a CC-immune enemy whose now-entry is a cast. Same algorithm as the designer's prototype patch. SIM.OPENER_ROOT_DIVERSE 0 restores the v2 opener (diagnostic only).
- Recorder: f2 fields 10-13 per fight (answered, landed, killing wind-up, Disrupts); per-run `wy` (answered by kind and why) and `ht` (nodes per heat state).
- B50 gap is paired on the same replayed masher deaths; B52 is windows used per 100 non-boss fights (normal interval over fights); B55's interval is over wind-ups, not clustered by run [assumption: slightly too narrow].
- Heat: heat-seek / heat-avoid differ from the planner only in the hour weight (× 0.5 / × 4 instead of × 2) [assumption: approximation of H.6]. B57's arm without rewards is a scratch engine with HEAT_GAIN, HEAT_RARITY_UP and ECHO_PER_HEAT_FIGHT 0. The heat cells and B57's arm start from the warm-up observations, so they are paired with planner-P0.
