# Mash report 2026-11-11: is one-button play enough?

Status: measurement, draft for the Lead and the owner. No rules constant, content value, balance target or spec was changed; src/ untouched.

Question [stated: docs/feedback/2026-11-11-1841-owner.md]: the owner played Hunter runs by pressing key 1 over and over, without picking targets, and got through. Is one-button play enough, for every class, with the talents of that attack? Follow-up 18:46 [stated]: mashing a rotation on normal mobs is fine (classic grind); rares, elites, dungeon bosses and new enemy types must make the player stop and think, and that should happen every so often.

- Engine and simulator: main 3e16499 (engine M2b v12 as implemented in c0ab802, the onebutton bot and the think replays). Rotation replays (`--think-mash`) at aa33d22 (classic) and the expedition think and rotation replays at 43d8537, which fixes fightClone (below); src/ is the same in all three, and the replay cells' onebutton runs are identical run for run to the main cells' (all classic P0 runs, all 150 expedition think runs).
- Script: `bash sim/measure-mash.sh sim/out/mash` (stages A-D as measured), rotation replays with `--think-mash`; tables `node sim/mashreport.mjs --in sim/out/mash`. About 30 minutes on 2 workers plus 7 on 1.
- Seeds 1000 onward, paired by class and seed across bots. Starting tables: classic, the 11-11b planner warm-up tables; expedition, the 11-11f node-risk observations with the v11 table pass's `ct` and `dim` (as 11-11g).
- Rates: Wilson 95 %; paired differences: normal interval of the paired difference.

## Summary for the owner

**Short answer: no, one-button play is not enough to win, for any class, on either map, with or without meta. It is enough for the grind, which the owner says is fine, and it fails in the special fights, which is what the owner wants.**

| Class | Reach the boss, one button / autoplay: classic P0 | classic PALL | expedition P0 | expedition PALL | End-boss wins, one button |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rogue (Gutting Jab) | 24 / 81 % | 51 / 93 % | 14 / 73 % | 50 / 81 % | 0 in all cells |
| Mage (Ember Bolt) | 60 / 79 % | 89 / 90 % | 58 / 77 % | 86 / 83 % | 1 in 1 000 classic P0, 1 in 1 000 classic PALL, else 0 |
| Hunter (Keen Shot) | 56 / 88 % | 67 / 96 % | 32 / 73 % | 67 / 88 % | 0 in all cells |

1. **One-button play does not win.** One key, the talents of that attack and a potion under 35 % HP beat the end boss twice in 7 800 runs (autoplay wins 33 % of classic P0 runs and 61 % of classic PALL runs). The end boss is the skill check the game already has.
   - **Rogue: not enough anywhere.** Gutting Jab builds combo points the one-button player never spends. Pressing the biggest damage button instead (the masher, which also fires finishers) reaches the boss 59 % (P0).
   - **Mage: enough for levelling with meta.** On classic PALL one-button play reaches the boss as often as autoplay (89 against 90 %); on P0 60 against 79 %.
   - **Hunter (the owner's case): levelling partly.** 56 % (P0) and 67 % (PALL) against 88 and 96 %. [inference: from the PALL numbers and the owner's report] The owner's "I got through" matches the Mage and, with meta, much of the Hunter's levelling, not the end boss.
   - **Expedition with the start camp: the same.** One-button play reaches the boss in 34.6 % of P0 runs (autoplay 74.4 %) and 68.0 % of PALL runs (84.1 %), and wins none of 1 800.
   - Part of the gap is the tree: autoplay on the one-button trees reaches the boss 70 % (classic P0), so the button alone costs 23 pp of reach on the same character.
2. **The grind is easy: fine.** On the same fights (paired replays), one-button play dies in 1.3 % of normal quest fights, 0.3 % of grind waves and 0.4 % of dungeon trash fights on classic P0 (PALL: 0.7, 0.1, 0.1 %) and loses as much HP as autoplay (normal fight: 19.5 against 19.7 % of max HP). A planned opening saves about 7 % of max HP per normal fight, not runs.
3. **The special fights are hard for one-button play: wanted, and it holds.** On the same fights (classic P0; PALL in brackets) one-button play dies in 12.6 % of elite fights (9.8 %), 32.9 % of dungeon-boss fights (21.1 %) and 42.3 % of rare fights (32.6 %); the planned opening dies in 1.1, 1.4 and 19.7 % (0, 0, 8.4 %). Of the fights one-button play dies in, the planned opening survives 84 % (95 %), and 71 % (85 %) of those are special fights. One-button play answers 5 % of the wind-ups in elite, rare and dungeon-boss fights; autoplay about 70 %, the opener 78 %.
4. **How often the player has to think.** By the definition asked for (one button dies, or ends 20 % of max HP or more below the planned opening), a think fight comes every 4.2 nodes (classic P0; 6.8 per run; PALL every 4.1 nodes) and four in five are not special fights. For a player with a damage rotation (the masher on the same fights) it comes every 6.1 nodes (PALL 6.3); special fights are think fights 58 % of the time for that player (PALL 65 %), normal and grind fights 10 % (7 %). On the expedition (P0) a think fight comes every 4.6 nodes and a rotation think fight every 6.6 nodes; special fights are rotation think fights 41 % of the time there, normal and grind fights 13 %.
5. **Where the game misses the owner's target today.**
   - **Zone 1 teaches nothing.** Think fights are 7 % of Z1 fights and its special fights only 22 % (PALL 41 %), against 63-89 % of special fights in Z2-Z5.
   - **Something new is not harder than something known.** Normal and grind fights with an enemy met for the first time in the run are think fights as often as familiar ones (16.0 against 16.3 %; PALL 14.4 against 12.8 %). New enemy types do not ask for a different answer today; only the special fights do.
   - **In normal fights the penalty for not thinking is HP, which comes back.** The normal fights that are think fights cost 20 % of max HP or more but rarely a run (one-button deaths 0.3-1.3 %).
   - **Potions carry one-button play.** Without them it reaches the boss in 31 % of classic P0 runs (with them 47 %) and 52 % of PALL runs (69 %).

**Think fight** [assumption: "clearly more HP", Lead's message 18:46]: every non-boss fight of a onebutton run is replayed from the same state and fight stream with the opener's opening then autoplay (the "opener line") and with autoplay. A think fight is one in which onebutton dies (or needs a resurrect) where the opener line does not, or ends it at least 20 % of max HP lower (SIM.THINK_HP_GAP; 10 and 30 % shown too). A **rotation think fight** is the same test for the masher's replay of that fight: the fight still punishes a player who repeats a fixed damage rotation.

## Where one-button play fails and where it passes

Same fights, classic P0 and PALL (the tables list every archetype and the enemies with at least 25 replayed fights):

- **Fails: dungeon bosses, elites and rares with a wind-up.** The worst enemies are dungeon bosses: Ammeset, Voice of Veth (caster; think fight 94 % P0 / 90 % PALL, one-button play dies 57 / 27 %), Father Vael the Unfaithful (caster; 94 / 92 %, dies 40 / 30 %), Mawgrim of the Locks (brute; 68 / 73 %, dies 32 / 23 %); then elites such as Mawra Ironbrow (guardian; 79 %) and Kharun the Unburied (brute; 73 %). Their signature wind-ups land when nobody answers them, and a landed wind-up empowers the enemy for the rest of the fight (combat-v2.md 16.2).
- **Fails: casters and brutes.** Fights with only casters: one-button play dies 15.9 % (opener line 0.2 %); only brutes 9.6 % (1.4 %); PALL 11.7 % (0 %) and 9.4 % (0 %). Their turn-2 Hex Bolt and Heavy Smash are what a fixed button cannot answer.
- **Passes easily: beasts, skulkers and guardians, treasure guards, most dungeon trash.** Beast-only fights: think 3.8 %, one-button dies 1.4 %; skulker-only 4.7 %, 0.8 %; Barn Wolf, Furrow Boar, Peat Sentinel and Stonewall Sentinel in normal fights 1-4 %; Cellar Rat Swarm, Rust-Shackled Guard, Harrow Deserter and Candle Cultist (trash) 0 %.
- **Normal fights cost HP, not runs.** The worst normal enemies (Slag Brute 46-50 %, Forge Priest 43 % think fights) kill one-button play in under 3 % of their fights. HP lost in normal fights is refilled by regen and food between fights [inference: from the death rates], so the grind rarely forces a decision. Dungeon trash with a mender, caster or brute (Mourning Nun, Chapel Bellringer, Pallid Chorister: 47-59 % think fights) costs HP the same way.
- **Expedition: the same pattern** (tables below). Dimmed encounters kill the planned opening almost as often as one button (8.8 against 11.1 % of those fights), so they test the route more than the fight.

## Readings and assumptions
1. The default target is the engine's rules 7.9 choice (mender, then caster, then lowest HP), which is what an action aims at when the player picks no target. "Not choosing a target" therefore already hits the healer or caster first; this is part of why normal fights are easy.
2. HP lost per fight is the HP at the start minus the HP at the end, before post-fight regen, in % of max HP; potions drunk in the fight count as HP not lost. A death counts as losing all HP left.
3. The opener plans the first 5 rounds only (OPEN_ROUNDS), then plays autoplay; its full runs are expensive (about 2.3 s per run), so they ran 100 runs per class on classic P0 and fewer elsewhere (sample sizes in the tables). The think replays give the opener's line on the same fights as onebutton, which is the paired comparison.
4. The think sample is the first 100 seeds per class (50 for PALL and the expedition), whose onebutton runs are identical to the first runs of the full onebutton cell; for the rogue these 100 seeds are a harder sample (12 % reach the boss, 24 % in all 1 000).
7. **Simulator bug found and fixed (43d8537):** sim/lib/clone.mjs fightClone shared the expedition block and the zone graph with the live state, so a fight replayed to its end (think replays, B50 replays) or an opener or tactician look-ahead changed the live expedition run (5 of 18 fights in a check). The first expedition think cells were run again after the fix; classic runs were never affected. Earlier expedition cells that used replays, the opener or the tactician should be read with this in mind; the planner, greedy, speedrunner, masher and onebutton cells were not affected.
5. Rotation replays (masher on the same fights) cover all 1 000 onebutton runs per class on classic P0 and PALL, and the 50 think seeds per class on the expedition. Not run (compute budget): onebutton-nopot and the opener on the expedition, the masher on expedition PALL.
6. The record field fc at 3e16499 wrote '?' for dungeon trash and shardspawn enemies; fight kinds come from the fight itself, so no table is affected (fixed in aa33d22).
