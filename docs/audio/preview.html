How the game could sound draft

Eight proposed cues, all made in the browser with no sound files. Direction A (Lantern Gouache): warm wood, cloth, felt and small brass bells at dusk. One cold, glassy voice is kept for the Dimming, and one low, hollow voice for the Deathblow. Use headphones or decent speakers; laptop speakers lose the low end.

Sound starts on your first click (browser rule).
Cue log (the test hook): nothing played yet.

This page is about character, not mix: volumes, lengths and pitches are first guesses. Tell us "warmer", "shorter", "more metal", "less" for any cue and the table in docs/audio/README.md changes before anything goes into the game.
